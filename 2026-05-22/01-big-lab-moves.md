# Big Lab Moves — 2026-05-22

A 24-hour inversion. Yesterday the headline was *the state steps in* — Trump signing a federal pre-release-review order "as soon as today." Today that order is **postponed**, and the labs pivoted to the other lever of power: **the public markets.** **OpenAI is filing a confidential S-1 as early as today**, targeting a **September IPO at ~$852B–$1T**; Anthropic is eyeing October. And underneath both, the week's deepest move — **Andrej Karpathy joining Anthropic to build a team that uses Claude to accelerate Claude's own pre-training** — tells you where the frontier thinks its next leverage actually is: **automating AI development itself.** The frame this week: *the regulator paused, the bankers stepped up, and the talent voted with its feet.*

## 1. Trump's AI executive order — POSTPONED (yesterday's lead, reversed) {#1-eo-postponed}

**What happened:** The White House **pulled the planned signing** of the AI + cybersecurity executive order that this archive led with on [2026-05-21](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-trump-eo). Per reporting:

- Trump said he **delayed it because he "didn't like certain aspects"** of the order, and — bluntly — because he **"hates regulation"**; his framing: *"I don't want to get in the way of [US AI companies] leading."*
- The **draft survives** and has two parts: (1) a **voluntary framework** for frontier labs to **share "covered models" with the government ≥90 days before public release** (+ pre-release access for certain critical-infrastructure providers); and (2) a **cybersecurity section** — a Treasury-led **"clearinghouse"** (agencies + AI companies) to find and fix security vulnerabilities in *unreleased* models, plus Pentagon/national-security hardening, cyber-hiring, and breach threat-sharing.
- The text is the product of weeks of negotiation between the White House, **National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross**, and AI companies — **OpenAI, Anthropic, and Reflection AI** named.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The pre-deployment-evaluation / AI-assurance lane I flagged yesterday ([2026-05-21/05 §3](../2026-05-21/05-career-and-startup.md#3-eo-lane)) is **delayed, not cancelled.** This is actually *useful timing*: the function is coming (the draft exists, the negotiation is mature, the cyber-clearinghouse half is the part everyone agrees on), but the hiring wave hasn't fired yet. Keep "pre-deployment evaluation / model-release governance / AI assurance" in your skills vocabulary and your eval artifacts on-thesis — you're now early to a lane that's been *scheduled*, not staffed.
- **Startup lens:** The **cybersecurity clearinghouse** is the part most likely to survive any redraft (it's voluntary, industry-friendly, and bipartisan-safe). That's a tell: **tooling that helps a lab or enterprise *find and document* vulnerabilities in unreleased models** has a federal tailwind even without the 90-day frontier-review half. Note how cleanly this rhymes with Exaforce's agentic-SOC thesis ([`02` §2](./02-new-emerging.md#2-exaforce)) — *security* is where the policy and the funding agree.
- **Insight:** Read the *reason* for the delay, not just the delay. "I don't want to get in the way of leading" is the **competitiveness-over-caution** doctrine stated out loud — the same logic the labs used to lobby for 14 days instead of 90. The durable signal: **in 2026, US AI policy is being written to maximize release velocity**, and any review framework that survives will be the lightest-touch version of itself. Plan your "assurance" bets for a *voluntary, fast* regime, not a heavy one.

## 2. OpenAI files a confidential S-1 — the frontier goes public {#2-openai-s1}

**What happened:** OpenAI is **filing a confidential S-1 (draft registration statement) with the SEC as early as today (Fri, May 22)**, working with **Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley**, and targeting a **public listing as soon as September 2026 at a valuation of ~$852B–$1T**.

- **"Confidential" mechanics:** a draft registration lets OpenAI submit financials to the SEC privately, iterate with regulators, and **withhold the numbers until ~15 days before the roadshow** — so don't expect the revenue/cost detail yet.
- **What unblocked it:** the filing comes **days after Elon Musk lost his lawsuit** against OpenAI, which had been a major obstacle to the IPO path.
- **Anthropic in parallel:** separately reported to be **targeting an October 2026 listing**, on the back of its projected first profitable quarter (~$10.9B Q2 revenue, ~$559M operating profit — [2026-05-21/01 §2](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#2-anthropic-colossus)).

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** A public OpenAI (and likely Anthropic) changes the **employer calculus** in three concrete ways: (1) **equity becomes liquid/priceable** — RSUs at a public co are real comp, not paper, which changes how you value an offer; (2) post-S-1 quiet periods and Sarbanes-Oxley discipline mean **more structured, headcount-planned hiring** (good for new-grads — clearer ladders); (3) **public financials will finally show the revenue mix**, telling you which orgs (API vs ChatGPT vs ads vs enterprise/FDE) are actually growing — i.e., where to apply. Bookmark the eventual public S-1; it's the best org-chart-by-revenue map you'll ever get.
- **Startup lens:** Frontier labs going public **opens the secondary/acquisition market** — IPOs create liquidity that recycles into the next wave of founders and angels (the post-IPO alumni-founder pattern). It also means **the platform you build on now has public-market incentives**: quarterly revenue pressure pushes OpenAI toward monetization (ads, [2026-05-21/02 §1](../2026-05-21/02-new-emerging.md#1-ads-surface)) and Anthropic toward enterprise margin. Build knowing your host's incentives just got more legible — and more relentless.
- **Insight:** The juxtaposition with §1 is the whole story. **The state paused (EO postponed) and the market accelerated (S-1 filed) in the same 24 hours.** Capital, not regulation, is the force currently disciplining the frontier — and capital wants growth, not caution. That's the macro you should be pricing into every "where is this going" judgment for the next year.

## 3. Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic — to use Claude to train Claude {#3-karpathy}

**What happened:** **Andrej Karpathy** — OpenAI **founding member** (2015), ex-**Tesla AI** lead, returned to OpenAI 2023, then founded education startup **Eureka Labs** — **announced on Tuesday (May 19) that he's joining Anthropic**, and **started this week**. The specifics matter:

- He joins **Anthropic's pre-training team** (the org that runs the massive training runs giving Claude its core capabilities).
- He's helping **launch a new team focused on using Claude *itself* to accelerate pre-training research** — i.e., applying the model to the meta-task of building the next model.
- Framing in his own words: the next few years at the LLM frontier will be **"especially formative,"** and he was **"eager to get back to research."** His writing reaches ~2M followers on X.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** This is a **destination signal**, and it's the strongest possible validation of your ME.md focusing decision to invest in the **Anthropic stack**. When the person who coined "vibe coding" and arguably did the most to make ML legible to a generation of engineers picks Anthropic over starting another company or rejoining OpenAI, the talent market reads it as "the most interesting frontier work is here right now." Reference it (specifically — *pre-training automation*, not generic "Anthropic is hot") in cover letters and interviews; it shows you read past the headline.
- **Startup lens:** The *mandate* — **use the model to accelerate building the model** — is the recursive-self-improvement loop moving from research-thread ([PostTrainBench, 2026-05-21/04 §2](../2026-05-21/04-research-progress.md#2-benchmarks)) to a **staffed, named team at a frontier lab.** If "AI that improves AI" is now a first-class internal product, the downstream wedges compound: tooling for **agentic ML research**, **experiment orchestration**, **eval/verification of model-generated training changes** (the verification skill again). The most defensible founder bet is the *picks-and-shovels* layer under "AI does AI R&D."
- **Insight:** Three of this week's biggest stories are the same shift viewed from different angles: **Karpathy's hire = automate the work; the IPO wave = price the work; the real-tool benchmarks ([`04`](./04-research-progress.md)) = verify the work.** The frontier's next leverage isn't a bigger chat model — it's **compressing the AI-development loop itself.** Orient your skill investment toward that loop (orchestration + eval + cost), not toward prompting a finished model.
