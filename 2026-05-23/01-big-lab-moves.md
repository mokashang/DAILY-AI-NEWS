## 1. Anthropic's $30B round — set to close next week at $900B+, passing OpenAI {#1-anthropic-900b}

**What happened:** Multiple outlets now report that **Anthropic is set to close a funding round of more than $30B as soon as next week, at a valuation above $900B.** The structure and players being reported:

- **Co-leads:** **Sequoia Capital, Dragoneer Investment Group, Altimeter Capital, and Greenoaks Capital Partners**, each reportedly investing **~$2B**. **Founders Fund (Peter Thiel)** and **General Catalyst** are among returning existing investors.
- **The valuation flip:** at $900B+, Anthropic would **surpass OpenAI**, whose most recent round (March 2026) valued it at **$852B** — the first time Claude's maker is the more valuable of the two.
- **Cadence:** this is Anthropic's **second $30B raise of 2026** — February's round was at a ~$380B post-money valuation, so the mark has **more than doubled in roughly three months.**
- **The caveat that matters:** as of this reporting the deal is **not finalized and no term sheet is signed** — commitments are still being finalized and terms could change. (This archive has flagged the "imminent" framing for ~3 weeks; treat it as near-certain but not done.)

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** This is the cleanest possible market confirmation of the **ME.md focusing decision** to invest portfolio depth in the Anthropic stack. A $900B private mark — *above* OpenAI — funds aggressive, structured hiring (Solutions/FDE/Integration/research) and de-risks "bet my skills on this vendor." Keep applying weekly with artifacts attached; the round closing is a *hiring-acceleration* signal, not a "wait and see" one. Note **who's leading**: Sequoia/Greenoaks/Altimeter/Dragoneer are crossover/growth funds — their presence means the next stop is public markets, so the equity you'd get is increasingly liquid-trackable.
- **Startup lens:** A $900B host platform with fresh $30B is a platform that will **ship faster and subsidize developers harder** to win the ecosystem — good for anyone building *on* Claude (more capability, more distribution), and a reminder that your wedge must survive Anthropic itself shipping into adjacent space. Watch what the capital funds: compute (Colossus, [2026-05-21 §2](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#2-anthropic-colossus)), enterprise distribution, and — per this week — **acquisitions** ([`02` §1](./02-new-emerging.md#1-consolidation)).
- **Insight:** The *cadence* is the signal, not the number. **A 2.4× valuation step in ~3 months** ($380B → $900B+) is the market repricing Anthropic from "strong #2" to "co-leader / arguable #1" on the back of the **business-adoption flip** (Ramp, [2026-05-14](../2026-05-14/01-big-lab-moves.md)) and **near-term profitability** ([2026-05-21 §2](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#2-anthropic-colossus)). Private valuations are now moving on *revenue and adoption*, not narrative — which is exactly why an IPO comes next.
