## 1. Pope Leo XIV's first encyclical, *Magnifica Humanitas*, is about AI {#1-encyclical}

**What happened:** **Pope Leo XIV released his first encyclical, *Magnifica Humanitas* ("Magnificent Humanity")**, today (May 25) — and its subject is **artificial intelligence.** Key points:

- A **~42,300-word** document urging **governments to slow and regulate AI development**, with emphasis on **protecting workers, protecting children, and preserving human control over weapons** (i.e., keeping lethal-force decisions out of autonomous systems).
- It was **presented at the Vatican with Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah among the speakers** — a striking image: a frontier-lab founder helping launch a papal warning about the technology his lab builds.
- The document is **symbolically dated to mark the 135th anniversary of *Rerum Novarum*** (Leo XIII's 1891 encyclical on workers' rights), explicitly framing AI as the labor-and-dignity question of this century.

- [CNN — Pope Leo's AI encyclical Magnifica Humanitas](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/25/europe/pope-leo-ai-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas-intl) `[secondary]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** This is the loudest possible signal that **AI governance / trust-&-safety / AI-compliance is a durable career lane, not a fad.** When the demand for "responsible AI" comes from the Vatican, the EU (Article 51, August window), *and* enterprise procurement simultaneously, the roles that sit at that intersection — Responsible-AI engineer, AI-policy/assurance, trust-&-safety — get structural, multi-year demand. Add this lane to your apply list ([`05` §1](./05-career-and-startup.md#1-week-ahead)); it pairs naturally with your eval/verification portfolio (the technical half of "responsible AI" *is* evaluation).
- **Startup lens:** "Human-control / oversight / dignity-preserving deployment" just became a **buyer requirement with moral weight**, not only a regulatory one. Tooling that lets an enterprise *prove* a human stayed in the loop, that children/vulnerable users were protected, that an agent never crossed a stated line — that's the compliance-tooling wedge ([2026-05-23/04 §2 eval convergence](../2026-05-23/04-research-progress.md#2-eval-convergence)) with a new, powerful tailwind behind it.
- **Insight:** Watch the *Anthropic co-founder on stage* detail. It's the same playbook the archive has tracked all month — Anthropic positioning itself as the **safety-credible** frontier lab (the ad-free pledge, the Gates Foundation deal, now the Vatican). Whether sincere conviction or strategic differentiation (it can be both), it's a *consistent* posture, and it's the reason your `ME.md` Anthropic-stack focusing decision keeps getting reinforced: you're aligning with the lab that's making "trustworthy" its brand.

## 2. Monday status check — Anthropic's raise is closing, not yet signed {#2-anthropic-status}

**What happened:** As of Monday, **Anthropic's >$30B round at a ~$900B valuation is reported "closing as soon as this week" but is NOT yet officially closed** — no signed term sheet confirmed. The shape (from [2026-05-24/01 §1](../2026-05-24/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-anthropic-round)):

- Co-leads **Sequoia, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Altimeter** (~$2B each); Founders Fund + General Catalyst in.
