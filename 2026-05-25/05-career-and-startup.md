### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Four targeted applications a week with artifacts attached beats forty spray-and-pray. The new addition — a governance/trust-&-safety role — diversifies your funnel into a lane with *moral + regulatory + enterprise* demand stacked together, and it reuses the eval portfolio you're already building. That's leverage: one body of work, two distinct lanes (deployment *and* assurance).
- **Startup lens:** The FDE shortage is your TAM (covered [2026-05-24/05 §1](../2026-05-24/05-career-and-startup.md#1-week-setup)); the governance demand is a *second* TAM. The wedge that serves both: **deployment tooling that bakes in the oversight/audit trail** — sell the deployment *and* the compliance story in one product. That's the most defensible "Claude-for-X" framing of the month.
- **Insight:** Notice the convergence — the market wants *deployment* engineers, and the institutions (Vatican, EU, enterprise) want *oversight.* The role at the intersection — **"I deploy agents into real systems *and* I can prove they stayed within the lines"** — is the single best-positioned profile for 2026–27. That's the headline to grow into.
