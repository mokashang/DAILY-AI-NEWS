## 2. Apple registers `genai.apple.com` ahead of WWDC (June 8) — a Gemini-powered, multi-AI Siri {#2-apple-wwdc}

**What happened:** Apple **registered a `genai.apple.com` subdomain** (spotted May 23–25), a tell ahead of **WWDC**, whose keynote is **Monday, June 8, 2026** (correcting earlier "June 9" placeholders — see [WATCHLIST](../WATCHLIST.md)). Expected, per multi-source reporting:

- **iOS / iPadOS / macOS 27** introduce an **"Extensions" framework** letting users choose **ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude** inside Siri (first flagged [2026-05-07/01](../2026-05-07/01-big-lab-moves.md)).
- The **rebuilt Siri** reportedly runs a **custom Gemini-based model** (Google Cloud's Thomas Kurian confirmed at I/O that Gemini will power a new personalized Siri later in 2026 — [2026-05-25/02 §1](../2026-05-25/02-new-emerging.md#1-gemini-pro)), served via Apple's **Private Cloud Compute.**

> Confidence: the multi-AI **Extensions SDK** is HIGH-likelihood (long-rumored, corroborated in test builds); specific **Siri internals** are MEDIUM (leak-based).
