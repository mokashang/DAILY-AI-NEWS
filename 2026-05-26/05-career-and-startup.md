## 2. Startup track: AI seed valuations carry a ~42% premium {#2-seed-premium}

**The data:** Q1 2026 set venture records (~$300B global, AI-driven). **Seed-stage AI startups carry a ~42% valuation premium**, median pre-money **~$17.9M.** Net-new dollars concentrate in **growth/mega-rounds** (infra + foundation models); **seed/Series A aggregate the most deals.** The two hottest investable themes: **agentic retrieval / precision RAG** (as agents hit production — cf. [`04` §1](./04-research-progress.md#1-paperarena)) and **behavioral-first software-supply-chain security** (as agents autonomously write and deploy code).

**Sources:**
- [Crunchbase News — record AI funding, Q1 2026](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/record-breaking-funding-ai-global-q1-2026/) `[analysis]`
- [TechCrunch — AI seed startups command higher valuations](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/its-not-your-imagination-ai-seed-startups-are-commanding-higher-valuations/) `[secondary]`
- [Qubit Capital — AI startup fundraising trends](https://qubit.capital/blog/ai-startup-fundraising-trends) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens (founder-adjacent):** A ~42% seed premium means a credible AI founder can raise on *less* proof than a year ago — but it also means *more* competition for the same wedge. The differentiator is **distribution + a real deployment story**, exactly what the FDE path teaches.
- **Startup lens:** Both hot themes — **agentic retrieval** and **agent-written-code security** — are **reality-interface** wedges (grounding + verification), matching this week's research arc ([`04` §2](./04-research-progress.md#2-arc)) and the [SaaSpocalypse "own the layer gaining budget"](../2026-05-24/02-new-emerging.md#1-saaspocalypse) thesis. Re-score your [STARTUPS.md](../STARTUPS.md) wedges toward those and away from app-layer wrappers.
- **Insight:** Cheap-ish seed capital + crowded wedges = **the constraint isn't money, it's a defensible problem.** Use the FDE/Integration path to *find* the painful, repeatable workflow before you raise — the premium rewards founders who've already seen the problem five times, not those chasing the theme.
