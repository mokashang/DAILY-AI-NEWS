# New & Emerging — 2026-05-27

The emerging story that ties the week together is a **form-factor war**: the bet that AI's next platform isn't a better chatbot but a **dedicated, always-on personal-assistant device.** Hark's chipmaker-backed $6B round ([2026-05-26 §1](../2026-05-26/02-new-emerging.md#1-hark)), OpenAI's io acquisition, Google's Gemini Spark proactive agent, and Apple's reboot are four independent bets on the *same* thesis. When the interface changes, the entire application layer resets — and that reset is the biggest greenfield opportunity in the sector right now.

## 1. The form-factor war — AI moves off the screen {#1-form-factor}

**What's emerging:** Four signals, one thesis — the next AI platform is a **device + always-on agent**, not an app:

- **Hark** — $700M Series A, $6B, **all four chipmakers** (Nvidia/AMD/Intel/Qualcomm) + Salesforce/ARK/Brookfield. Models *and* hardware for a "universal AI interface." ([2026-05-26 §1](../2026-05-26/02-new-emerging.md#1-hark))
- **OpenAI / io** — the Jony Ive hardware bet; OpenAI has floated **"AI-first devices that could eliminate traditional apps."**
- **Google / Gemini Spark** — a **24/7 proactive consumer agent** ([2026-05-20 §1](../2026-05-20/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-io-scorecard)) — software-side, but the same "always-on assistant" pattern.
- **Apple** — racing to put its AI at the center of the device before the others define the category.

**The mechanism that makes it matter:** a new interface **resets the application layer.** Every assumption baked into "apps" — screens, taps, icons, notifications, app stores — is renegotiable if the primary interface becomes a conversational, proactive agent. That's exactly the kind of reset that, in past platform shifts (mobile, web), minted the biggest new companies — and they were rarely the device makers.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Two job markets open at once: **(1) the hardware/on-device-ML crossover** ([2026-05-26/05 §1](../2026-05-26/05-career-and-startup.md#1-hardware-lane)) — thin, fit-screened, high-paid; and **(2) the agent/skills app-layer** for these devices — where your existing orchestration + MCP + skills work maps directly. You don't need to build silicon; you need to be ready to build *the agent that runs on it.*
- **Startup lens:** **Don't build the device — build the app layer.** The highest-return position in a platform shift is the software that defines what the new platform is *for* (the "killer app"), not the platform itself. Watch every one of these for an **SDK / developer program**; the first credible app-layer builders on a winning device platform are the next decade's anchor companies. Add a "device app-layer" wedge to [`STARTUPS.md`](../STARTUPS.md).
- **Insight:** The deepest signal is the **chipmaker co-investment** ([2026-05-26 §1](../2026-05-26/02-new-emerging.md#1-hark)) — when fierce competitors all fund the same category, they're certain the *category* is real even if the *winner* isn't known. Position for the category (build portable agent skills), not for a specific device bet. The interface is changing; be early to the layer that sits on top of it.
