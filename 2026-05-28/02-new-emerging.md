# New & Emerging — 2026-05-28

Two checks the same week, two different bets on **what comes between the model and the customer**. **Cognition (Devin) — $1B at $26B post (May 27)** is the bet that *agents-as-engineers* is now its own software category, priced like enterprise SaaS, not like research. **OpenRouter — $113M at $1.3B led by CapitalG (May 26)** is the bet that *routing across many models* is the durable infra layer once frontier prices keep falling and customers refuse to single-vendor. Both rounds are simultaneously valid: agent-coding is a *vertical*, routing is the *infrastructure*. Notice the investor signal — **Alphabet's growth fund underwrites the model-marketplace** even as Alphabet ships Gemini 3.5 Flash at $1.50/1M. Google is hedging its own moat.

## 1. Cognition AI raises $1B at $26B post-money — agent-coding gets a public comp {#1-cognition}

**What's emerging:** On **May 27, 2026**, **Cognition AI** (the company behind **Devin**, the autonomous AI software engineer) announced **$1B+ raised at a $25B pre-money / $26B post-money valuation.**

Specifics worth absorbing:
- **Lead investors:** **Lux Capital, General Catalyst, and 8VC** co-led.
- **Follow-on / new investors:** Elad Gil, Soma Capital, Omri Casspi, Founders Fund, Atreides Management, Ribbit Capital, and Layer Global among others.
- **Valuation step-up:** **2.5×** from the **$10.2B post** at the September 2025 $400M round — in **eight months**.
- **Revenue:** **$492M annualized run-rate.** Enterprise usage of Devin **growing 50% MoM for the past six months** — a compounding curve, not a one-off spike.
- **Customer list:** **Mercedes-Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, Santander.** This is *not* a startup-developer-tools list — it's regulated-industry / multinational / aerospace.

The framing investors used (per Bloomberg + TechCrunch reporting): Devin is now closing **the enterprise gap**, not the prosumer one. The 50% MoM curve and the Fortune-500-heavy customer list are the two data points anchoring the valuation.

## 2. OpenRouter $113M Series B at $1.3B — CapitalG underwrites the router layer {#2-openrouter}

**What's emerging:** On **May 26, 2026**, **OpenRouter** — the AI model exchange platform — announced a **$113M Series B at ~$1.3B post-money**, **led by CapitalG (Alphabet's independent growth fund)**.

Specifics:
- **Other investors:** NVentures (NVIDIA), ServiceNow Ventures, MongoDB Ventures, Snowflake Ventures, Databricks Ventures, **Andreessen Horowitz, Menlo Ventures.** Note the **data-platform investor cluster** — MongoDB/Snowflake/Databricks all in the same round.
- **Valuation:** **2× year-over-year.**
- **Coverage:** **400+ models** through a **single API** — Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, xAI, DeepSeek, and the long-tail of open-weight models.
- **Scale:** **8M users**, **100T tokens / month** (≈ 25T per *week*) — a **5× increase in 6 months**.
- **Use of funds:** routing, governance, optimization, "as more enterprises integrate AI models into their production workflows."

The deeper signal: **Alphabet's growth fund just underwrote a category whose explicit pitch is "don't single-vendor"** — i.e., a layer that erodes Google's own ability to lock in Gemini distribution. Read it as confirmation that **even Alphabet thinks multi-model routing is the durable shape of enterprise AI.**

- **Job lens:** Your existing portfolio artifacts — **3-provider router, personal Claude billing audit, cost-aware agent design** — are **directly on-thesis for OpenRouter and every router/routing-adjacent startup it just legitimized.** Add a **5-line "what I'd contribute to OpenRouter"** section to your `apps/` notes. Wedges to score in `STARTUPS.md`: **(a) vertical routers, (b) routing-cost-anomaly-detection, (c) "OpenRouter inside our perimeter" self-hosted equivalents** — the last one rhymes directly with the new Claude Code self-hosted sandboxes ([`03` §1](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#1-claude-code-sandbox)).

That's the bet CapitalG just made — and it's the bet your portfolio is positioned for.
