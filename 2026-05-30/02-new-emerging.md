# New & Emerging — 2026-05-30

Three category-defining rounds inside one week, all rhyming with the [Anthropic crown](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-anthropic-crown): **Cognition (autonomous coding agents) at $26B**, **OpenRouter (multi-model routing) at $1.3B**, and **Groq (inference-neocloud after a $20B Nvidia not-acqui-hire) raising $650M**. The frame: **the application layer (Cognition) is being priced at frontier-lab multiples, the routing layer (OpenRouter) just got Alphabet-validated, and the inference layer (Groq) is showing the post-acquihire playbook for chip startups.** Together: *the agentic stack now has three distinct, well-funded layers — and your wedge has to pick a floor.*

## 1. Cognition — $1B at $26B post-money (Devin / Windsurf) {#1-cognition}

**What happened:** On **Wednesday May 27, 2026**, **Cognition** (maker of Devin + acquirer of Windsurf) announced a **$1B+ round at $26B post-money** ($25B pre).

- **Co-leads:** Lux Capital, General Catalyst, 8VC
- **Participants:** Founders Fund, Ribbit Capital, Atreides Management
- **Run-rate revenue:** **$492M ARR** (enterprise grew **>10× since January**)
- **Multiple:** ~**53× ARR** vs public-SaaS 8–15× — frontier-lab-tier multiple for an *application* company
- **Valuation step-up:** **2.5× in 8 months** ($10.2B Sep 2025 → $26B May 2026)
- **Enterprise customers (newly disclosed):** Goldman Sachs, Citi, Mercedes-Benz, **US Army, US Navy**
- **Internal stat:** **~90% of Cognition's own code is written by Devin** (per their own disclosure)

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Devin works enterprise-grade now.** US Army + US Navy + Goldman + Citi + Mercedes is the bluest list a coding-agent company has ever had. Two implications: (1) **the "AI replaces junior SWE" narrative is no longer pure hype** — Cognition's own 90% AI-written codebase is the existence proof; (2) **but the *role* growing fastest at Cognition is FDE + Customer Engineering**, because every one of those enterprise logos requires deployment scaffolding. Apply to **Cognition FDE / Customer Engineering** roles this week — they're hiring against this round.
- **Startup lens:** **53× ARR is the new ceiling for "agentic-coding" comps.** If your wedge sits in the agentic-coding band (any "Devin for X" play), you can now defensibly anchor a seed pitch on Cognition's revenue multiple. But the *real* read is: Cognition is **eating both Cursor's IDE share (via Windsurf) and the autonomous-agent layer simultaneously** — the wedge between "AI in your editor" and "AI replaces an engineer" is collapsing. Don't found in the middle. Pick a side: a *vertical* (legal-coding, biotech-coding, embedded/firmware-coding) or *infra below the agent* (eval, observability, routing).
- **Insight:** **"90% of our own code is AI-written" is the founder-credibility bar for 2026.** When you talk to investors, "we use AI to build AI tooling" is now a *floor*, not a differentiator. The interesting claim is **what specific *human* judgment you encode that the agents can't.** Update [`STARTUPS.md`](../STARTUPS.md) wedge candidates with a "what's the 10% I keep" column.

## 2. OpenRouter — $113M Series B at $1.3B (CapitalG lead) {#2-openrouter}

**What happened:** On **Tuesday May 26, 2026**, **OpenRouter** — the multi-model routing API — closed **$113M Series B at $1.3B post-money**, led by **CapitalG (Alphabet's growth fund)**.

- **Participants:** NVentures (Nvidia), ServiceNow Ventures, MongoDB Ventures, Snowflake Ventures, Databricks Ventures, **a16z, Menlo Ventures**
- **Valuation step-up:** **2.4× in ~11 months** ($547M post-money in June 2025 → $1.3B May 2026)
- **Volume:** **25 trillion tokens/week (~100T/mo)** — **5× growth in 6 months**
- **What they do:** single API over hundreds of models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, open-weights — automatic routing for price/latency/quality

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** "AI gateway / model router" is now a **funded category**, not a side-project pattern. That means companies running multi-model production stacks (which is almost everyone, post-Opus-4.8 + post-Gemini-3.5-Flash) need an **AI Platform Engineer** who can speak fluently about latency / cost / quality trade-offs and integrate gateways. **This is a less-crowded interview-question vector than "build an agent"** — go memorize the latency tables on [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/) and have a 30-second answer to "when would you route to Sonnet vs Gemini Flash vs GPT-5.5-mini?"
- **Startup lens:** **Alphabet leading a multi-model router is unusual.** Google is *also* a model provider — its growth fund just bet on the **commoditization of model choice**. Three reads: (1) Google is hedging against its own-model dominance; (2) the routing layer is where margin pools when models commoditize (cf. AWS in cloud); (3) any new entrant in "AI gateway" now competes with a CapitalG-backed incumbent at scale. **Don't found here unless you have a defensible vertical wedge** (e.g., regulated-industry routing with audit/compliance built in).
- **Insight:** 25T tokens/week → ~1.3 quadrillion tokens/year of routed traffic = the **single best market signal you can buy** for which models actually get used in production. If you can get OpenRouter's public model-share dashboard into your weekly reading list, you'll know which provider is gaining/losing developer share **before the press notices.**

## 3. Groq — raising $650M for inference-neocloud second act {#3-groq}

**What happened:** On **Friday May 29, 2026**, Axios + TechCrunch reported **Groq is raising $650M** from existing investors (round **guaranteed**: Disruptive + Infinitium have agreed to backstop pro-rata gaps).

**Context — the December 2025 Nvidia deal:**
- **$20B non-exclusive licensing agreement** with Nvidia (Dec 2025) — Groq's chip IP licensed
- **Acqui-hire of senior Groq engineers** (including CEO) departed to Nvidia
- **$7.6B distributed to shareholders** in Feb 2026 (~**$64/share** — the first major payout under the deal)

**What Groq is now:** an **inference-neocloud business** that runs Groq's homegrown LPU (language processing unit) systems as a hosted-inference service. Competes with Cerebras Cloud, Together, Fireworks for high-throughput inference workloads.

**Sources:**
- [TechCrunch — Groq raising $650M after Nvidia's $20B not-acqui-hire](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/29/after-nvidias-20b-not-acqui-hire-ai-chip-startup-groq-reportedly-raising-650m/) `[secondary]`
- [Axios (scoop) — Groq $650M for second act](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/28/groq-650-million-nvidia) `[secondary]`
- [GuruFocus — Groq $650M for AI neocloud after $20B Nvidia deal](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8889027/groq-inc-raises-650-million-for-ai-neocloud-services-after-20-billion-nvidia-deal) `[aggregator]`
- [Tom's Hardware — Nvidia buys Groq assets for $20B](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-buys-ai-chip-startup-groqs-assets-for-usd20-billion-in-the-companys-biggest-deal-ever-transaction-includes-acquihires-of-key-groq-employees-including-ceo) `[secondary]`
- [Startup Fortune — Groq neocloud second act](https://startupfortune.com/groq-raises-650-million-for-its-neocloud-second-act-after-selling-its-soul-to-nvidia-for-20-billion/) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Inference infrastructure is a smaller, less-crowded talent market than model training or applied AI**, and it's where pricing gets contested daily. If you have systems chops (CUDA, Triton, vLLM, paged-attention, KV-cache, MoE serving), apply to **Groq, Cerebras, Together, Fireworks** — these are *under-applied* relative to the size of the inference economy ($725B AI capex in 2026, much of which is inference-bound). Less-crowded lane = higher signal-to-noise in your application.
- **Startup lens:** This is the **post-not-acqui-hire pivot template.** Nvidia paid $20B for the IP + senior people, Groq kept the legal entity + customer relationships + ~$7.6B paid out + $650M new equity in. **If you ever found a deep-tech / hardware startup, the "license + acqui-hire + the remaining co keeps running" structure is now precedent** — useful to understand for exit-modeling and for talking to deep-tech VCs.
- **Insight:** Watch what **inference price-per-token** does in Q3 2026. With Groq doubling down on LPU-as-a-service, Cerebras pushing wafer-scale, and Nvidia building Groq IP into reference designs, the **inference cost curve is about to bend hard** — which is the *real* enabler of agent-team architectures (Opus orchestrator + Sonnet workers + cheap inference at the leaf) becoming default. **Cheaper inference → more agent steps per dollar → the cost lever you're learning in [`03` §1](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#1-opus-4-8-routing) gets even more leverage.**
