## 1. Anthropic closes $65B Series H at $965B → most valuable AI startup {#1-anthropic-series-h}

**What happened (Thu, May 28, 2026):** Anthropic closed a **$65B Series H at a $965B post-money valuation**, surpassing OpenAI's $852B March mark and becoming the most valuable AI startup in the world.

- **Lead/co-lead investors:** **Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia Capital, Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners**.
- **Other major investors:** **Baillie Gifford, Blackstone, Brookfield, D.E. Shaw Ventures, DST Global, Fidelity Management & Research**.
- **Strategic infra partners on the cap table:** **Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron** — chips + memory now have direct equity in Anthropic's next 18 months.
- **$15B of the $65B is previously-committed hyperscaler money** — including the **$5B from Amazon announced in April**.
- **Business state:** **$47B run-rate revenue** as of this month, up from $30B earlier this year and $10B last year. Anthropic projects its **first operating-profitable quarter** ([2026-05-21/01 §2 had it at ~$559M, Q2 ~$10.9B](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#2-anthropic-colossus)) — i.e., the company is now plausibly **the only frontier lab with a credible "we are profitable" story going into IPO**.
- **Stated use of funds:** *"advance our safety and interpretability research, expand compute to meet growing demand for Claude, and scale the products and partnerships our customers rely on."*

- **Tops GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro on benchmarks for agentic coding, financial analysis, and computer use.**

## 3. Mythos rolls out to all customers "in the coming weeks" {#3-mythos-ga}

**What happened (announced May 28–29):** Anthropic said the **Mythos** model family — its **most cyber-capable frontier model**, originally launched in restricted-access form on [2026-05-06](../2026-05-06/01-big-lab-moves.md) — will move to **wide release across all customers in the coming weeks**.

- Mythos is publicly framed as a model that can **find vulnerabilities in existing software and *chain* those vulnerabilities to execute sophisticated cyber attacks** — i.e., it's the first frontier model whose marketing positions it as *operationally cyber-capable* (not just "can write exploit code if asked"). This is the *exact* capability profile that motivated the federal AI EO ([`§4`](#4-eo-nsa) and [2026-05-21/01 §1](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-trump-eo)).
- The transition window between "Mythos available to vetted defenders" (since May 6) and "Mythos available to all paying customers" is the **single most consequential capability-release window of 2026** — the offense/defense gradient inverts in the period.

If you've been considering the security lane (even tangentially), this is the week to add **"AI red-team / model-eval / agentic-SOC"** to your scoped job-search list. The roles will be on three rosters at once: **frontier labs (Anthropic, OpenAI), banks (JPM, Goldman — direct exposure via [Anthropic's $1.5B PE JV](../2026-05-07/01-big-lab-moves.md)), and agentic-SOC startups (Exaforce, Judgment Labs, et al.)**.
- **Startup lens:** **Defender tooling specifically optimized for Mythos-class threats** is a category being created in real time. The thin slice that founders can win: **MCP servers that wrap detection / triage / response workflows around Mythos-style chained-vuln behavior**, sold into mid-market companies that won't get a dedicated Anthropic FDE assigned to them. The TAM is everyone running enterprise software who isn't already in PwC's top-100 client list.
- **Insight:** Mythos GA and the [NSA-in-the-loop EO draft](#4-eo-nsa) are the *same event seen from two sides*. The lab is releasing a cyber-capable model; the state is building the framework to test cyber-capable models. The fact that these are happening **in the same 2-week window** is the most important policy/capability coupling of 2026. Plan for a regime where **cyber-capable frontier model releases are matched by federal pre-deployment testing windows** — the assurance career lane just got its anchor product.

## 4. The Trump AI executive order returns — NSA in the loop for voluntary pre-deployment testing {#4-eo-nsa}

**What happened (week of May 25):** After the [postponement reported on 2026-05-22/01 §1](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-eo-postponed), the **AI / cybersecurity executive order** came back onto the White House calendar — this time with **the NSA in the loop**.

- The draft now structures the **voluntary information-sharing framework** so that **NSA handles classified pre-deployment testing of frontier models** that labs voluntarily submit before release.
- White House framing is FDA-analogous: *"a clear road map for how future AI that could potentially create vulnerabilities should go through a process so that they're released in the wild after they've been proven safe, just like an FDA drug."*
- The cybersecurity-clearinghouse half ([Treasury-led, from 2026-05-21](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-trump-eo)) is reportedly still in the draft.
- Driver: Mythos and its peer cyber-capable models ([`§3`](#3-mythos-ga)) — the EO is, in part, *the policy response to Mythos.*

- [Nextgov / FCW — Anticipated executive order could give NSA a role in voluntary AI model testing](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/05/anticipated-executive-order-could-give-nsa-role-voluntary-ai-model-testing/413663/) `[secondary]`

- See also: [2026-05-21/01 §1 first EO draft](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-trump-eo) · [2026-05-22/01 §1 postponement](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-eo-postponed)

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **NSA-administered classified pre-deployment testing creates a brand-new career lane** — *cleared* AI evaluators who can do red-team / capability-eval work on frontier models inside SCIFs. This is *the* opposite of a crowded lane: clearance requirements wash out 95% of the AI-engineer applicant pool. If you have *any* existing security-clearance pathway (DoD scholarship, prior gov service, family eligibility), this is suddenly your highest-leverage signal in 2026. Even without clearance, the **unclassified evaluator + assurance roles** at NIST AISI, MITRE, RAND, and the cleared contractors (Booz Allen, Leidos, SAIC) become real openings.
- **Startup lens:** The wedge to watch: **commercial tooling that mirrors NSA-style classified eval workflows for enterprise customers** (banks, defense contractors, healthcare). When the federal process specifies what "good pre-deployment testing" looks like, that becomes the *de facto enterprise-procurement spec* within 12 months. First mover on a productized version wins the category.
- **Insight:** The EO went from "postponed" to "returning with NSA" in nine days. The durable signal isn't the specific text — it's that **every postponement of the EO will resolve toward more pre-deployment-eval infrastructure, not less**, because the underlying capability (Mythos and peers) only gets more cyber-operational with each release. Plan as if pre-deployment review is **a structural fixture of 2026–27**, even if the exact mechanism changes month-to-month.

## 5. Google DeepMind acqui-hires ~20 Contextual AI researchers for $80–90M; Douwe Kiela joins {#5-deepmind-contextual}

**What happened (announced around Google I/O / late May):** DeepMind **acqui-hired ~20 researchers from Contextual AI under an $80–90M licensing deal**. Notably included: **Douwe Kiela** (Contextual AI co-founder/CEO; **co-inventor of Retrieval-Augmented Generation / RAG** while at Meta FAIR; previously head of research at Hugging Face).

- Deal structure is the now-standard **acqui-hire-via-licensing** template (used previously for Inflection→Microsoft, Character.AI→Google, Adept→Amazon).
- DeepMind's bet: **production-grade RAG, retrieval, and grounded-generation expertise** to harden Gemini's enterprise / Vertex AI offering (the "Gemini-as-a-work-tool" thesis [from I/O](../2026-05-20/01-big-lab-moves.md)) against Anthropic's coding/agent lead.
- Counterweight to [**Karpathy → Anthropic**](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#3-karpathy) two weeks earlier: the talent war is now *bidirectional* — each lab is acquiring the specialist depth its rival lacks.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** DeepMind is now hiring *aggressively* against the RAG/retrieval/eval skill tree. If your portfolio has even a thin **RAG-or-retrieval slice** (eval harness for retrieval quality, hybrid sparse+dense indexes, chunking experiments), this is a window to apply specifically to **DeepMind Applied / Vertex AI** roles citing the Contextual integration. The defensible startup wedges are still the same: **proprietary data substrate + retrieval-eval discipline + chunk/index/rerank tuning for a specific domain**. The Mendable / Glean / Vectara category just got a competitive floor underneath it.
- **Insight:** Two weeks, two giant talent moves, two different theses. **Anthropic gets Karpathy (pre-training automation — make the model better)**; **Google gets Kiela (RAG / production grounding — make the model usable on enterprise data)**. The frontier is no longer a single race — the labs are choosing different leverage points. Where you invest your skill compounding should mirror the lab whose theory of the next 18 months you actually believe. The [`ME.md`](../ME.md#current-focusing-decision-re-evaluate-monthly) focusing decision is the *answer* to that question for this archive — and it's now even more strongly Anthropic-stack.
