Most candidates focus on certifications and deployments; the firms that hit Preferred and Global Premier first will be the ones that *also* organize their customers to publish endorsements. If you're at a Select firm, **owning the endorsement-collection process is a fast track to Practice Lead.**
3. **The Partner Hub is a directory.** Anthropic just gave enterprise buyers a sorted list of consultancies. Buyer-discovery from cold went to ~zero. That's a *massive* expansion of demand for the firms above the Select line — and they will hire to meet it.

## 4. New under-priced lane: AI FinOps / inference-cost engineering {#4-finops-lane}

**What's emerging:** The combination of (a) the **$36B Apollo/Blackstone chip-financing deal** ([`02` §1](./02-new-emerging.md#1-chip-financing)), (b) the **June 15 Agent SDK metering** change, and (c) the **cost-aware orchestration research thread** ([`04` §1](./04-research-progress.md#1-orchestration-cost)) all converge on a new job category: **AI FinOps / inference-cost engineering** — the role responsible for *the bill*, not the model.

**What it looks like:**
- Tracking per-call cost across vendors and model tiers.
- Building dashboards on token-per-task efficiency.
- Recommending re-routes (Opus → Sonnet → Flash) when cost thresholds breach.
- Negotiating committed-use credits, reserved-capacity contracts, and rate-card terms.
- Building cost-attribution models so each product team sees its own bill.

**Realistic starting points:**
- Senior engineer role at a $50M+ ARR AI-product company → **first FinOps hire** (titled "Staff Engineer, AI Infrastructure" or similar; expect $250–350K TC).
- Big-bank AI cost-engineering team (Goldman, JPM, Morgan Stanley are all standing these up post the Anthropic-on-Wall-Street deal from May).
- Cloud-FinOps consultancies pivoting (Cloudability, Vantage, Apptio).

**Sources / signal:**
- (Macro signal) The chip-financing deal ([`02` §1](./02-new-emerging.md#1-chip-financing)) is the strongest one I can point to — when private credit shows up, FinOps follows.
- The research thread ([`04` §1](./04-research-progress.md#1-orchestration-cost)) provides the methodology.
- [Anthropic — June 15 Agent SDK metering doc](https://www.anthropic.com/news) `[primary]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** This lane is *less competitive than AI Integration Engineer* because (a) it requires a hybrid skill (cost modeling + agent engineering) that few people have built yet, (b) the job titles don't exist yet so candidates can't search for them. If you can claim "I built a multi-vendor router with per-call cost tracking" ([`03` §3](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#3-fallback-router)), you're 50% of the way to a FinOps interview.
- **Startup lens:** **Vantage-for-LLMs** is a real venture-scale wedge that no incumbent has won. Cost visibility across multi-vendor, multi-model deployments is a clean B2B SaaS thesis ($50/seat/mo to start).
- **Insight:** Every new infra category eventually gets its FinOps function. AI is following the cloud-FinOps playbook 5 years compressed. Be early to a job title that doesn't formally exist yet.
