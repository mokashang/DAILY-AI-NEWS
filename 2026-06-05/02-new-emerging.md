## §2 — Microsoft Build 2026 reframes the vertical-AI wedge {#2-microsoft-pressure}

**What happened.** Microsoft Build 2026 (see [`01` §5](./01-big-lab-moves.md#5-build-2026)) explicitly positioned **Rayfin + Fabric** as **"agents write the code, Fabric ships it, idea-to-enterprise-grade in hours."** This is **Microsoft claiming the vertical-AI delivery channel itself** — at platform scale.

**Read for the vertical-AI wedge:**

- **The wedge does NOT close.** Microsoft has the trust + plumbing; **vertical players still have the domain depth.** Legora's $5.55B says this is repeatable.
- **But the wedge narrows.** "Build a workflow tool with GPT inside" wedges are increasingly **commoditized** by Rayfin / Agent 365 / Antigravity / Anthropic Solutions. The defensible wedges are now:
  1. **Regulated-industry trust** (legal, health, finance, defense)
  2. **Multi-jurisdiction depth** (Legora's 50-market posture)
  3. **Proprietary data network effects** (the data your agent collects from operating becomes a moat)
  4. **Customer concentration on a single vendor's stack** (e.g., builders who go all-in on Claude vs trying to abstract over providers)

**Sources:**
- [Building agentic apps with Microsoft Fabric and Databases — Azure Blog](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/microsoft-build-2026-building-agentic-apps-with-microsoft-fabric-and-microsoft-databases/) `[primary]`
- [Microsoft and Google take on Anthropic and OpenAI in AI coding models — CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/microsoft-and-google-take-on-anthropic-and-openai-in-ai-coding-models.html) `[secondary]`

**Why it matters to you:**
- **Job:** Microsoft's pitch *amplifies* demand for **AI Integration Engineers** at customer end (the inverse of the FDE role — buyers will need staff who can wire Rayfin/Fabric to their domain stack). This re-validates the [ME.md](../ME.md) specialty-lane choice.
- **Startup:** Refresh [STARTUPS.md](../STARTUPS.md) wedge cards with the four-axis defensibility test above. If your wedge doesn't score on at least 2 of 4, **the Microsoft-platform pressure will collapse it in 12 months.**
- **Insight:** Big-platform absorption of horizontal tooling is **not new** (Office did it to vertical productivity apps in the 90s) — what's new is **the speed.** Build → GA → competitive pressure used to be a 24-month arc. Today it's 90 days. Founders must ship to defensibility inside one Microsoft news cycle.
