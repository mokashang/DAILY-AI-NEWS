**The result: three hiring lanes inside one company are simultaneously thickening.**

Closest existing analog: Frontier Red Team + Alignment Science roles. **First-mover advantage matters here** — the Institute is new and applicant volume is still low.

**The Karpathy compounding factor.** [2026-05-22](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#3-karpathy) — Karpathy joining Anthropic's pre-training team drove a major applicant wave. The slowdown narrative + $965B raise + Opus 4.8 = the next wave. **Apply tonight, not Monday.**
