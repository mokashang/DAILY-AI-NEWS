## 3. The skill repricing — what the gap window said {#3-skill-repricing}

Synthesizing all six files in this edition:

**Priced UP (do more of this):**

- **Dynamic-workflow design** (Opus 4.8 + verifier-first + cost-instrumented)
- **MCP-server building + MCPAgentBench-style evaluation**
- **Cost-aware multi-provider routing** (4-provider stack: Anthropic + OpenAI + Google + MSFT MAI)
- **Per-step cost instrumentation as a deliverable** (not as a hidden internal practice)
- **Vertical-AI workflow shipping** (legal, finance, healthcare, SMB, defense)
- **Public artifacts** — GitHub repos with READMEs and demo gifs > LinkedIn posts > tweets

**Priced DOWN (do less of this):**

- **Raw prompt engineering** as a standalone skill — commoditized by `/effort` and dynamic workflows
- **Single-prompt agent demos** — the ceiling moved; one-shot agents look small
- **Mock-tool benchmarks** in interview answers — cite real-MCP benchmarks instead
- **"Generalist SWE with some AI"** positioning — the AI-first framing wins now

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The repricing is happening *now* — companies are interviewing differently than they were 14 days ago. Update your portfolio README and resume bullets to use the "priced up" vocabulary explicitly.
- **Startup lens:** The "priced up" categories are also where the cleanest founder wedges sit. Don't build for the commoditized layers.
- **Insight:** This is the second skill-repricing event in 8 weeks (the first was the May-21 verification/eval re-pricing). **Each one widens the gap between candidates who track the macro and candidates who don't.** Your `ME.md` rule — "one artifact every weekend" — is the operational version of staying on the right side of this gap.
