## 2. Anthropic × Google × Broadcom — 3.5 GW TPU partnership announced today. {#2-anthropic-broadcom}

**What happened.** Anthropic published "Expanding partnership with Google and Broadcom for multiple gigawatts of next-generation compute" today. Headline: **~3.5 GW of TPU capacity via Broadcom, expected online 2027.** Broadcom CEO Hock Tan said on the most recent earnings call that 2026 Anthropic demand was ~1 GW and 2027 demand will be "in excess of 3 GW." Mizuho models Broadcom AI revenue from Anthropic at ~$21B in 2026 and ~$42B in 2027. Sites are majority US.

## 5. Microsoft Build 2026 — MAI-Code-1 + GitHub Copilot desktop app ship. {#5-microsoft-mai-code-1}

**What happened.** At Microsoft Build (early June 2026), Microsoft launched **seven new MAI systems** across reasoning, coding, image, voice, and transcription. Headline for our world:
- **MAI-Code-1** — Microsoft's own inference-efficient coding model, in Copilot + VS Code.
- **MAI-Code-1-Flash** — fast, low-cost variant, priced **below Claude Haiku 4.5** on price-to-performance, in the GitHub Copilot model picker.
- **MAI-Thinking-1** — 35B reasoning model, 256K context.
- **GitHub Copilot Desktop App (preview)** — Microsoft's native answer to **Codex** and **Claude Code**, with **OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google models** all in the picker. Agentic by default.
