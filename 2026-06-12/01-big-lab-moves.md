## 3. Anthropic × Google × Broadcom — 3.5GW TPU partnership announced today. {#3-anthropic-broadcom}

**What happened.** Anthropic published "Expanding partnership with Google and Broadcom for multiple gigawatts of next-generation compute" today. Headline: **~3.5GW of TPU capacity via Broadcom, expected online 2027.** Broadcom CEO Hock Tan said on the most recent earnings call that 2026 Anthropic demand was ~1GW and 2027 demand will be "in excess of 3GW." **Mizuho models Broadcom AI revenue from Anthropic at ~$21B in 2026 and ~$42B in 2027.** Sites majority US.

This builds on Google's previously announced up-to-**$40B investment in Anthropic** (cash + compute) and is the second leg of Anthropic's compute diversification (xAI Colossus 1 — 220K GPUs, $1.25B/month — is the first; see [2026-05-21/01 §2](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md)).
