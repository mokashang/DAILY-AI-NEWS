## 1. Prometheus (Bezos + Vik Bajaj) — $12B Series B at $41B. Industrial / "general engineer" AI. {#1-prometheus}

**What happened.** Announced June 11. Prometheus, led by Jeff Bezos and former Google exec Vik Bajaj, raised **$12B Series B at $41B post-money** valuation. Total funding now **>$18B in <1 year** (Bezos was largest backer in the $6.2B Series A, participated in B). **~150 engineers.** Mission: build an **"artificial general engineer"** — software that automates the design and manufacturing of complex physical systems, from jet engines to drug compounds. Bezos: "*it deserves a dedicated team that is obsessed with this one thing.*" No corporate ties to Amazon or Blue Origin. Stated use of funds: "**acquire more compute.**"

**Investors (B).** JPMorgan · BlackRock · Goldman Sachs · DST Global · Arch Venture Partners.

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** 150 engineers, $18B raised, 100% physical-systems focus = each Prometheus eng eventually carries a $120M+ funding-per-head ratio. **They're already hiring; the role descriptions are likely "mechanical/aero/chem eng × CS/ML."** This is a *non-overlapping* lane with your default Anthropic/OpenAI applications — worth one application in your weekly batch if you can credibly tie it to a physical-systems project (build one over the weekend; e.g., LLM-driven CAD assist via FreeCAD's API).
- **Startup.** **Physical-AI is now the wedge with a $41B anchor.** Smaller startups can't compete on capital but *can* compete on (a) verticals Prometheus won't touch (food processing, textile machinery, oil-services rigs) or (b) **the software layer Prometheus depends on but won't sell** (CAD-to-LLM tokenizers, structural-verification eval). File the latter in [STARTUPS.md](../STARTUPS.md).
- **Insight.** **The "general agent" framing has officially migrated from digital to physical.** Read Bezos's "it deserves a dedicated team obsessed with this one thing" line as a deliberate echo of how Amazon and Blue Origin were founded. The 150-engineer constraint is the signal: this is a *focused* AGI bet, not a moonshot lab.

## 2. NEURA Robotics — up to $1.4B Series C. Physical AI confirms as a category. {#2-neura-robotics}

**What happened.** German "Physical AI" robotics platform **NEURA Robotics** announced a Series C of up to **$1.4B**, led by **Tether** (crypto-native) alongside **Qualcomm, Amazon, and NVIDIA**. This sits next to Prometheus on the same thesis (general-purpose embodied AI) at a smaller capital scale and a humanoid/industrial-arm form factor.

**Sources.**
- [Crunchbase News, "Biggest Funding Rounds: Megarounds Proliferate, Led By Enterprise Software, AI, And Space Tech" (2026-06-05)](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-june-5-2026/) `[secondary]`

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** Add **NEURA + Figure + 1X + Apptronik** to the embodied-AI watch list (separate from the SWE/MLE default). The robotics-software stack overlaps your CS skill-base if you spent any undergrad time on ROS or Gazebo.
- **Startup.** The investor mix (Tether + 3 chip giants) is the *more* interesting signal than the dollar count. Crypto-native LP capital is back at the AI table; "AI × on-chain payments" wedges re-open in [STARTUPS.md](../STARTUPS.md).
- **Insight.** **Two physical-AI mega-rounds in 30 days = a category, not a coincidence.** The June-2026 pattern: digital-frontier valuations top at ~$1T, and the marginal AI dollar starts flowing to embodied systems.

## 3. Q1 2026 venture data, recapped. {#3-q1-recap}

**What happened.** Crunchbase Q1 totals: **$300B global venture, ~80% AI.** Four of the **five largest venture rounds in history** all closed in Q1 2026: **OpenAI $122B**, **Anthropic $30B**, **xAI $20B**, **Waymo $16B** — collectively **$188B** = ~63% of all global VC for the quarter. Series A AI benchmarks now **~$51.9M average**, ~30% above non-AI peers. Mega-rounds >$100M are routine.

**Sources.**
- [Crunchbase News, "Q1 2026 Shatters Venture Funding Records As AI Boom Pushes Startup Investment To $300B"](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/record-breaking-funding-ai-global-q1-2026/) `[secondary]`
- [Qubit Capital, "AI Startup Funding Trends 2026: Data, Rounds & What's Next"](https://qubit.capital/blog/ai-startup-fundraising-trends) `[analysis]`
- [AI Funding Tracker, "50 Top AI Funded Startups (June 2026)"](https://aifundingtracker.com/top-50-ai-startups/) `[aggregator]`

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** **63% of global VC into 4 companies tells you who's hiring at scale.** OpenAI / Anthropic / xAI / Waymo are the four "well-capitalized for 4+ years" labs irrespective of revenue. Adjust your application priority order accordingly.
- **Startup.** **$51.9M Series A average is a competitive trap, not an opportunity.** A $1–3M seed founder competing with $51.9M-funded Series A teams has to win on (a) wedge specificity, (b) speed-to-customer, or (c) cost-per-deployed-agent. None of those require $51.9M. Track this average — when it bends down, the bubble is reversing.
- **Insight.** When 63% of capital flows to 4 companies, the **remaining 37%** is where mid-sized startups (and your equity comp at one) actually compete. Apply to those 4 *and* to one Series B at $200–500M (e.g., Sierra, Decagon, Parallel) for risk diversification.
