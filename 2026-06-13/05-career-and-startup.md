## 3. Apple WWDC opens two new lanes — Siri AI integration & EU sovereignty {#3-sovereignty-lane}

Apple's WWDC 2026 ([`01` §4](./01-big-lab-moves.md#4-apple-wwdc)) created two distinct hiring lanes inside a single keynote:

The **iOS 27 Foundation Models framework now ships with built-in Claude support** ([from the May 22 thread](../2026-05-22/00-tldr.md)), and Siri AI needs people who can **make app developers' lives easier on Apple's terms.** Resume-grade phrasing: *"Built an iOS 27 Extension using Foundation Models framework with Claude routing for high-context queries and on-device Apple Intelligence for low-latency ones."*

- **Mistral** (or another EU sovereign-AI vendor) becomes Apple's EU fallback — a job lane *and* a startup tailwind for any EU AI tooling.
- **GDPR/EU AI Act-native AI infrastructure** is now a category buyers will pay for. Compliance + on-device + EU-data-residency are the three keywords.

**This week's add to your apply / watch list:**
- **Apple AI Engineer / Apple Intelligence (Cupertino, London).**
- **Google Cloud Apple-partnership Solutions roles** (Gemini distribution).
- **Mistral Solutions / Integration / EU rollout.**
- **EU AI compliance tooling startups** (Series A stage; watch Crescendo AI's [VC deals list](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-vc-investment-deals-in-ai-startups) weekly).

### Why it matters to you
- **Job lens:** A *new* major-platform AI hiring lane in 2026 is rare; the WWDC keynote opened one. Apple has been *under-hiring* its AI strategy for two years and just publicly committed to a multi-year overhaul. Apply before the queue thickens.

- **Insight:** AI sovereignty stopped being a 2027 thesis and became a 2026 product spec this week. Read every future "AI for X" pitch with the question *"how does this work in the EU?"* — it's now a buyer question, not a regulatory one.
