## 2. The pre-deployment-eval career lane is LIVE — on a 30-day clock {#2-eo-lane-live}

**What changed:**

The [2026-05-22 watchlist](../2026-05-22/00-tldr.md#watchlist-deltas) flagged the pre-deployment-evaluation hiring wave as **"delayed, not dead."** It's now **live, on a 30-day clock** — per [`01` §3 EO 14365](./01-big-lab-moves.md#3-eo-signed):

- **Federal cyber hardening** must begin within **30 days** of signing (June 2 → July 2 deadline).
- **AI cybersecurity clearinghouse** stands up on the same clock (Treasury-led).
- **Framework for voluntary pre-release engagement** finalized by **August 1, 2026.**
- The **Fable 5 suspension** ([§1](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-fable-suspension)) is the first real-world incident under the new regime — i.e., proof that *capability-equivalence analysis* and *fast-turnaround release-review* are now job functions with named stakeholders.

**Concrete employers hiring (or about to):**

1. **Federal agencies + GRC consultants** standing up the clearinghouse — **Deloitte / Accenture / KPMG / EY / Booz Allen / MITRE / SAIC**. These post under titles like "AI Risk Engineer," "Model Evaluation Specialist," "Frontier Model Auditor." Salary band slightly below frontier labs but more entry-level slots.
2. **Frontier labs' internal release-review / red-team / capability-equivalence teams** — Anthropic Safety Team, OpenAI Preparedness, Google DeepMind Frontier Safety, xAI Safety. Anthropic's June 12 public statement ("capability widely available from other models") is *exactly* the work product these teams produce.
3. **Bank / regulated-industry "AI assurance"** roles — Goldman, JPMC, Chase, Schwab, Plaid — these were already listed in the [May-21 thread](../2026-05-21/05-career-and-startup.md#3-eo-lane) but the EO and Fable 5 incident together convert "nice to have" into "regulator-expected."

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Apply this week. The pattern from prior compliance hiring waves (SOX, GDPR) is **fastest hiring in the first 60 days after the framework deadline** — i.e., **window opens July, peak hiring July–September.** Vocabulary to put on your resume *now*: "pre-deployment evaluation," "capability-equivalence analysis," "model-release governance," "incident response for AI systems." Don't wait for the JD to use these exact phrases — recruiters will start searching for them within 2 weeks.
- **Startup lens:** The startup wedge is **tooling that *automates* the capability-equivalence argument.** Anthropic essentially won the Fable 5 PR fight by saying "this jailbreak's capability is widely available." A startup that does this *as a service* (jailbreak → capability extraction → cross-model availability check → evidence package) saves every frontier lab and every Big-4 AI Engineer practice ~weeks of analyst time. **Demo-able in a hackathon; defensible as a Series A.**
- **Insight:** EO 14365 is the **first US AI regulation that's *labor-creating* for the AI Engineer / FDE persona** rather than threat-creating. Treat it as such — every prior governance wave (SOX, HIPAA, PCI) produced 5–10 years of high-demand niche careers. This is yours if you move now.

## 3. FDE market 10× in 18 months — band widens, top end pulls away {#3-fde-validated}

**What the latest data says:**

- **Forward-Deployed Engineer postings: ~10× growth in 18 months** (multiple recruiter aggregators).
- **~18,029 open AI Engineer roles** in the US right now (Glassdoor) — a meaningful slice in the FDE category.
- **Compensation:** most FDE roles **$130K–$300K base + bonus**; **senior FDE at frontier labs $400K–$500K TC including equity.** The top end is materially above what May data showed.
- **Migration of the role:** FDE postings have moved **from AI-pure companies into broader enterprise software and consulting** — Deloitte / Accenture / KPMG / BCG **collectively post more FDE roles than any single product company except Google.**
- **Skill bar shift:** **30–50% of lab FDE listings now ask for LLM integration experience, RAG, or model evaluation** explicitly (not implicitly). This is direct demand for *exactly the artifacts in [`03` §1](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#1-self-hosted-sandboxes) and [`03` §2](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#2-cost-router).*
- **Fastest-growing FDE hirers:** **OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, and the YC-backed agent-startup cohort.**

- **Job lens:** Three concrete moves this week, in priority order. (1) **Apply to 2 Anthropic Solutions / FDE / Integration roles** before Thursday — Anthropic is the [ME.md focus](../ME.md) lab and is in the fastest-growing-FDE-employer set; quote your [§1 self-hosted-sandbox artifact](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#1-self-hosted-sandboxes) and the Ramp-41% data point in your cover letter. (2) **Apply to 1 OpenAI FDE role** — even with the [Anthropic-stack focus](../ME.md), the OpenAI FDE pipeline is the second-largest in the industry and the interview prep transfers. (3) **Add 2 Big-4 AI Engineer — Client Delivery roles** (Deloitte / Accenture / KPMG / EY) — these are now collectively the largest FDE employers; the salary band is lower but the volume + new-grad slots are higher.
- **Startup lens:** The FDE role is *itself* a thin-startup wedge: a "FDE-as-a-service" boutique that ships customer integrations for AI-app startups (especially the YC cohort) has obvious unit economics and a flat go-to-market — the customers are *the startup founders themselves*, who are explicit about hating to hire FDEs in-house. Build a one-person consultancy this summer, convert to product later if the integration patterns repeat.
- **Insight:** Salary band widening **$130K → $500K TC** means the role is **stratifying** — early-career, mid-career, and senior FDE are starting to be priced as distinct ladders. **Pick your entry rung deliberately** — most new-grads should aim mid-range ($180K–$220K base at a frontier-AI-adjacent product company) and target the *step up* into a frontier lab in 12–18 months. **Don't take the first $130K offer** unless the team / mentor / project is in the top-1% of what you'd ship from.
