## 1. Trump's AI executive order — SIGNED 06/02 (closes the 05/22 thread) {#1-trump-eo-signed}

**What happened:** On **June 2, 2026**, Trump signed an executive order titled **"Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security"** — closing the 33-day "postponed → signed" thread carried from [2026-05-22 §1](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-eo-postponed). The shape:

- **Voluntary** — not mandatory. No licenses, no permits for new AI models.
- **30-day pre-release government access** (down from the 90-day draft) — a process by which developers voluntarily provide federal agencies early access to "covered" frontier models for up to 30 days before public release.
- **AI Cybersecurity Clearinghouse** — federal agencies + AI companies share intelligence on vulnerabilities in unreleased models; Treasury-led.
- **Benchmarks for cyber capability** — agencies develop standardized assessments for model cyber-offense / cyber-defense potential.
- **Federal hardening** — improvements to government's own security defenses.

## 3. US Commerce Dept pulls Claude Fable 5 + Mythos 5 from market (06/12) — the first post-deployment recall {#3-fable-mythos-recall}

**What happened:** A four-day arc that is the biggest export-control event in commercial-AI history:

- **June 9** — Anthropic released **Claude Fable 5** publicly (1M-token context, 128K output, $10/M in · $50/M out — Anthropic's most capable production model) and the twin **Claude Mythos 5** for vetted defenders (same base model, security filtering removed for critical-infra red-team use).
- **June 12, 5:21pm ET** — Anthropic received a US government **export-control directive** citing national-security authorities. The directive **suspended all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 by any foreign national, whether inside or outside the US, including foreign-national Anthropic employees**.
- **Net effect:** Anthropic disabled Fable 5 + Mythos 5 globally to comply. All other Anthropic models continued operating.
- **Stated cause:** the government cited *verbal* evidence of a "narrow potential jailbreak" — essentially asking the model to read a specific codebase and fix flaws (i.e., the same capability OpenAI just shipped as Patch the Planet — see [`02` §1](./02-new-emerging.md#1-patch-planet-ibm-daybreak)).
- **Anthropic's public response:** disagreed publicly. Position: *if "narrow potential jailbreak" is the standard, no frontier model from any provider could ever be deployed.*
- **June 23** — Fable 5 returned to **paid-only access for US users** (the 13-day complimentary preview ended); access for foreign nationals remains suspended pending resolution.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Read this section twice if you are a foreign national.** The directive blocked foreign-national Anthropic *employees* — meaning even employed engineers lost access to the company's flagship product. Watch carefully whether: (a) Anthropic FDE / Solutions postings start adding a *US-person clearance* preference; (b) the directive expands to other models; (c) other labs (OpenAI, Google) get parallel directives. Your application timing now has a citizenship-aware dimension it didn't have on 05/22. See also [`05` §3](./05-career-and-startup.md#3-foreign-national-recalibration).

- **Insight:** The state can now **post-deployment-recall** a deployed frontier model in 4 days, via verbal evidence, with no published technical disclosure. The EO's voluntary framing is the polite face of this; the export-control authority is the actual lever. **Plan around the lever, not the framing.** This is the single most important durable update to your mental model in 2026 so far.
