## 3. The assurance lane re-read — Anthropic-vs-DOD + Colorado + Mythos relift = same shape {#3-assurance-lane}

**What happened:** The three Big Lab Moves of this edition — [`01` §1 DOD lawsuit](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-anthropic-dod), [`01` §2 Mythos relift](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-mythos-relift), and [`01` §3 Colorado AI Act](./01-big-lab-moves.md#3-colorado-ai-act) — are three faces of the same shift: **AI governance is now happening at three layers simultaneously** (federal-litigated, federal-operational, state-statutory).

**The hiring implication:** *three* different sub-lanes inside the "assurance" umbrella:

1. **Policy Engineering** (the litigated layer) — at Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind. Small teams, hard to get in, top of the band.
2. **AI Access-Control / Trust & Safety Engineering** (the federal-operational layer) — at frontier labs *and* Tier-2 API vendors (Cohere, Mistral, AI21). Larger teams, less competitive, very technical.
3. **AI Compliance Engineering** (the state-statutory layer) — at *every* enterprise selling into a Colorado high-risk vertical. Largest teams, lowest competition for a CS-grad with the Claude-Code + MCP stack.

The "assurance" keyword tree at the top: *pre-deployment evaluation, AUP enforcement, model-release governance, adversarial-query detection, agent-RBAC, telemetry-channel sanitization, distillation-attack classification, output provenance / watermarking, federal clearance, state high-risk AI compliance.*
