## 4. Job-market pulse — H2 2026 hiring accelerates; specialization wins {#4-market-pulse}

**Data-quick.**
- **AI Engineer is still #1 fastest-growing US job title** — postings +143% YoY (per PwC + industry trackers); adjacent AI/ML postings +163% 2024→2025.
- **Wage premium for AI skills: 56%** vs comparable non-AI positions (up from 25% a year ago).
- **H2 2026 hiring intent:** **78% of tech leaders** plan to increase permanent headcount; **66%** increasing contractors. The hiring window is open.
- **Specialization signal:** **75%+ of AI-engineer job listings specifically seek domain experts** — generalists are being priced out. Pick a specialty lane and go deep.

**How to read this against your target list** (`ME.md`):
- **Anthropic Solutions / FDE / Integration** — best hiring window is now through Sept.
- **OpenAI FDE / Deployment Co** — 5% government-stake overhang may compress equity math but base + bonus still strong.
- **Google Cloud Agent / Antigravity Solutions** — DeepMind-departure headwinds ([Jumper etc.](./01-big-lab-moves.md#4-geneva)) mean *product-side* hiring at Google may accelerate to keep the customer story going.
- **Big-4 (PwC/Deloitte/Accenture/EY) AI Engineer — Client Delivery** — post-Geneva expansion is highly likely.
