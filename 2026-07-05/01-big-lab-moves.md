## 1. Geneva UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance opens Monday (T-1) {#1-geneva}

**What happens tomorrow.** The **first-ever Global Dialogue on AI Governance** convenes at the Palais des Nations, Geneva, on **Monday–Tuesday, July 6–7, 2026**, under the Global Digital Compact. It's the first international AI-governance platform where all **193 UN Member States sit at the same table with the private sector, civil society, academia, and the technical community**.

**Structure.**
- **Two-day format**: high-level segment + thematic sessions + side events.
- **Four thematic areas**: (1) AI opportunities and implications; (2) bridging AI divides; (3) safe and trustworthy AI; (4) respecting human rights.
- **Co-chairs**: Ambassador **Egriselda López** (El Salvador) and Ambassador **Rein Tammsaar** (Estonia). The pairing — a Latin American middle-income voice + a small-EU-state technical-governance voice — is deliberate; watch how they trade off equity-of-access vs. safety framings in the plenary.
- **Bundled with**: **WSIS Forum 2026** (July 6–10, same venue) and **ITU's AI for Good Global Summit** (July 7–10). The whole week is one continuous governance event chain, not a single dialogue.

**What to watch.** The plenary is where language gets negotiated; the **side events** are where positions get stated. Anthropic, Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI have delegations. Expect side-event summaries and delegate one-off posts to be the highest-signal source — **not** the closing communiqué.

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** Governance-lane roles — **Policy / T&S / Applied AI (Policy)** at Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and the Big-4 AI-assurance practices — have been the "delayed but scheduled" hiring lane since the [postponed Trump EO](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-eo-postponed). Geneva **restarts the clock** by giving companies a real deliverable ("our Geneva position" translates to hiring + JD language within 4–6 weeks). By-Monday move: bookmark the co-chairs' opening statements + one side event per lab; quote them in cover letters this week.
- **Startup.** The four themes double as founding wedges. **Bridging AI divides** = tooling for smaller-language/lower-resource-country deployment (Anthropic Solutions × Isomorphic-style sovereign-fund partnerships mature in this direction). **Safe and trustworthy AI** = the [MCP-Atlas / Toolathlon](../2026-07-04/04-research-progress.md#1-mcp-atlas)-adjacent verification-infra category. **Respecting human rights** = compliance / consent / audit-trail tooling for AI-augmented public services (the shape of a G2G product).
- **Insight.** Governance dialogues do **not** produce binding rules — they produce **vocabulary + red lines + coalitions**. The right way to read Geneva is: what shared language emerges by Wed, and which coalitions of states signal they'll enforce it via their own national laws? That vocabulary becomes the JD wording every AI-policy role uses for the rest of the year.

## 3. Sonnet 5 promo pricing — T-57 days to standard ($3/$15) {#3-sonnet-5-timer}

**Reminder.** Claude Sonnet 5's [June 30 launch](../2026-07-01/01-big-lab-moves.md) came with **promotional pricing of $2 per M input tokens / $10 per M output tokens through August 31, 2026**, after which standard pricing is **$3/$15**. That's a **57-day countdown** as of today (Sunday July 5).

**What that means concretely.**
- If your July + August workloads run on Sonnet 5 default, you're locked into the promo through Aug 31; anything you defer beyond that window pays 50% more per token.
- The **new tokenizer produces ~30% more tokens** for the same text vs Sonnet 4.6. Net-of-tokenizer, Sonnet 5 promo is still cheaper than Sonnet 4.6 in most workloads — but the arithmetic changes after Aug 31.
- Prompt caching + subagent routing (see [`03` §4](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#4-spend-followup)) are the direct mitigations.

**Sources.**
- **[primary]** [Anthropic — Introducing Claude Sonnet 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)
- **[primary]** [Claude Platform Docs — What's new in Claude Sonnet 5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/whats-new-sonnet-5)

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** Interview cost stories that reference **"before/after Aug 31 promo end"** show you're planning around Anthropic's pricing calendar — a stronger signal than a static per-token quote.
- **Startup.** If you're pricing a Claude-native product for enterprise, model **two SKU tiers** — promo-window pricing vs standard — so your buyer can see the July/August discount and the September+ full-price line separately. That framing wins trust.
- **Insight.** Promo pricing is a **customer-lock-in mechanism** — Anthropic buys 60 days of migration momentum from OpenAI and open-source alternatives, so switching cost is real by September. Structure your own architecture assuming the promo *works* on your usage patterns.
