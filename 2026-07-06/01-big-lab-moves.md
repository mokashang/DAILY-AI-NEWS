## 1. Geneva Day 1 — the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance opens {#1-geneva-day-1}

**What happened:** At **10:00 CEST today (Mon Jul 6)**, the UN convenes the first formal Global Dialogue on AI Governance under the [Global Digital Compact](https://www.un.org/global-digital-compact/en). Key facts:

- **All 193 UN Member States + private sector + academia + civil society** at the same table.
- **Two-day format** (Mon–Tue Jul 6–7): **high-level segment + thematic sessions + side events**.
- **Four thematic pillars**: (1) AI opportunities + implications; (2) bridging AI divides; (3) safe and trustworthy AI; (4) respecting human rights.
- **Co-chairs**: Ambassador **Egriselda López (El Salvador)** + Ambassador **Rein Tammsaar (Estonia)** — a deliberate "small-state" chairing lens.
- **Bundled events**: **WSIS Forum 2026 (Jul 6–10)** and **ITU AI for Good Global Summit (Jul 7–10)** run alongside — most of the actionable signal will land in **side-event summaries and one-off tweets** from lab delegations, not the plenary.

**US lab delegations expected**: Anthropic (leading with the [new constitution](../2026-07-03/01-big-lab-moves.md#4-constitution) as position paper), OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and xAI. Expect the **"we already self-regulate"** frame from US labs and the **"who governs the governors"** counter from small-state and Global-South delegations.

## 3. China distillation follow-through — Alibaba named + enforcement matures {#3-china-follow-through}

**What happened (as of this morning):** The [July 3 loophole-closure story](../2026-07-04/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-china-loopholes) matures into a fully-attributed enforcement action. Confirmed details:

- **~25,000 fraudulent Claude accounts** running **~28.8 million exchanges** with the model between **April and June 2026** — characterized by Anthropic Trust & Safety as **"the largest distillation campaign in Anthropic's history."**
- **Alibaba-affiliated entities** publicly named as the coordinating actor.
- **Documented routes**: Ant Group via a **Singapore-based subsidiary**; ByteDance reimbursing engineers for **personal Claude subscriptions on VPN**; hundreds of **"transfer station" relay services** on Chinese-language sites + GitHub, taking WeChat/Alipay payment.
- **Detection stack**: behavioral fingerprinting (timezones + usage patterns) + **government-ID + live-selfie verification** for flagged accounts (rolled out from April 2026).
- **The steganography footnote closes**: the hidden `base_url`/timezone/hostname check inside Claude Code was **removed in the July 2 release** — the enforcement mechanism is moving from covert instrumentation to transparent behavioral signals.
