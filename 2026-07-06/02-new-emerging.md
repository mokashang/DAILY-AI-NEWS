# New & Emerging — 2026-07-06

The IPO wave is now a category-defining macro. Beneath it, the H1 2026 funding data landed and reset expectations: **$510B in global VC, ~88% concentrated in US-HQ'd companies, OpenAI + Anthropic alone taking 43% of the pool.** In the same week we got four of the year's most instructive rounds — **Together AI $800M / $8.3B** (open-source-infra layer with Aramco leading), **Shield AI $1.5B** (defense unmanned), **AI2 Robotics $735M** (Chinese wheeled humanoids), and **Venice $65M** (privacy-first AI at unicorn) — plus the **Claude Science + Anthropic in-house drug-discovery** move as a founder template for vertical + own-industry. The narrative underneath: **capital is picking sides more sharply**, not less; the concentration is up, not down; and the "wedge inside a defensible vertical" pattern (Sierra, Cursor, Claude for X, now Anthropic-in-drugs) is the dominant winning template.

## 1. The IPO wave as an asset-class shift — H1 2026 = $510B, top-2 = 43% {#1-ipo-wave-effects}

**What happened:** H1 2026 venture data crossed:

- **Global VC hit a record $510B in H1 2026** — largest single half in the history of the asset class.
- **OpenAI + Anthropic captured 43%** of it — a concentration ratio without precedent for a two-company pair in any sector.
- **~88% of AI startup funding went to US-HQ'd companies** — the funding boom is not global; it is emphatically North American.
- The two S-1s ([`01` §5](./01-big-lab-moves.md#5-ipo-wave)) mean the top of the market is now on a path to public liquidity within 6 months.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The concentration means that when Anthropic + OpenAI hire, they hire *big*, and when they slow, everyone slows. Your **calibration**: assume Q3–Q4 hiring at the frontier labs will be *stronger*, not weaker, as they position for the roadshow (public companies don't want to open cycle with a hiring freeze story). This is your best applying window since 2023.
- **Startup lens:** For a founder, 43%-to-top-2 is a **death-warning** for anyone building horizontal foundation-model competitors. It's simultaneously a **buy signal** for anyone building **vertical wedges on top of** those labs — that's where the LP dollars *are* going, because the horizontal race is over.
- **Insight:** Non-US AI concentration below 12% is the most under-analyzed number in the report. It's not because international companies aren't building — it's because **capital pooled around US-HQ'd cos to buy US-lab compute + US-lab distribution**. That geography is going to matter a lot in the export-control fights ([`01` §1](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-fable-5-return), [`01` §3](./01-big-lab-moves.md#3-china-loopholes)). Consider it when planning any international founding.

Shield AI, Anduril, Palantir Defense, Scale Federal — all of these are hiring for a rare mix of embedded systems + ML + security clearance. AI2 Robotics + wheeled humanoids means **China's robotics lane is on the same growth curve as US defense** — either extreme of the "atoms + AI" market has capital.
- **Startup lens:** Shield AI's +140% one-year valuation gain is a data point most people miss: **defense AI outpaced software AI on valuation growth in the last year.** The wedges: **autonomy-eval-as-a-service**, **defense-specific MCP servers** (secure comms, redacted-log storage), **field-training data marketplaces**. ## 5. DeepMind × Contextual AI acqui-hire template + Karpathy team continues {#5-acquihire}

**What happened:** **DeepMind hired 20+ Contextual AI researchers under an $80–90M licensing deal** — the fifth big AI acqui-hire of the year (after Character.AI, Adept, Inflection, and now Contextual). The template: **hire the team via a licensing structure that avoids formal acquisition**, keep the startup entity nominally alive, retain the IP.

Meanwhile, **Andrej Karpathy's Anthropic pre-training team** (announced May 19, [see 2026-05-22/01 §3](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#3-karpathy)) is now ~7 weeks in. No shipping signal yet, but the tenure alone hardens the recursive-self-improvement thread.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The acqui-hire template is now a valid **exit path for a small research team** — it's the "pre-Series-B" outcome the ecosystem was missing. That reshapes what "research startup" means: **you don't need to build a product; you need to build a team a lab wants to license.** For an ambitious grad student, that's a category to consider. Adjacent read: the Karpathy team gestation window (7 weeks and counting with no shipped output) tells you what "high-signal talent moves" actually cost in throughput — patience is priced in.
- **Startup lens:** Founders: if you're building a small research team, plan for the acqui-hire exit as an option, not a failure mode. Structure your equity + IP for it. That means: **avoid heavy IP entanglements that limit licensing structures**, keep the team small enough to be acquire-able, and publish enough to be discoverable.
- **Insight:** The Karpathy quiet period is meaningful. When a top-signal researcher joins a frontier lab, the market wants immediate output. The fact that seven weeks have passed with no released paper or product tells you **the "Claude accelerates Claude" work is deep-research-shaped, not product-shaped** — infrastructure + methodology, not a shipped feature. When it lands, it will be a paper first. Watch arXiv.

## 6. The vertical-Claude template as a founder playbook {#6-vertical-template}

**What happened:** With **Claude Science (June 30, [`01` §2](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-claude-science))**, Anthropic has now shipped **five vertical products in eight weeks**: Legal, Small Business, Personal Finance (with Plaid), Finance, Science. Each vertical follows an increasingly clear formula:

1. **Curated tool + data catalog** for the vertical (60+ tools for Science; 12 practice-area plugins + 20 MCP connectors for Legal).
2. **Agentic workflows** for the vertical's "screen → narrow → decide" pattern (compound screening + memo for Science; document review + drafting for Legal).
3. **First-party demo showcasing the entire pipeline** (PKU enzyme-stabilizer for Science; Thomson Reuters CoCounsel Legal for Legal).
4. **Partnership set** with the vertical's dominant tools (Novartis/Lilly/J&J for Science; iManage/NetDocuments/LexisNexis for Legal).
5. **New hiring pool** in the FDE / Solutions / Integration Engineer lane.

Now that the template is repeatable, it's a **founder playbook**.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Every new vertical Anthropic ships adds ~10–30 FDE/Solutions/Integration roles at Anthropic itself, plus ~50–200 similar roles at consultancies (Deloitte / PwC / Accenture / EY) hiring against the vertical. Track the vertical rollouts in this repo as **hiring-market indicators**.
- **Startup lens:** The founder read: **pick a vertical Anthropic hasn't done yet** (education? construction? logistics? cybersecurity ops?) and build the same shape — curated tools, MCP connectors, agentic workflow, launch demo, partnership set. You are not competing with Claude; you are the **default distribution partner** for Claude in that vertical. This is the highest-clarity founder wedge available right now.
- **Insight:** The five-in-eight-weeks cadence is a message: **Anthropic thinks vertical is faster to defend than horizontal is to expand.** They're building a distribution *portfolio*, not a single super-app. That's a corporate strategy read to internalize — Salesforce didn't win by having one app; it won by having one hundred.
