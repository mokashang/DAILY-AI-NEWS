## 2. The IPO wave changes the "which employer" calculus {#2-ipo-employer-calculus}

**What happened:** With **Anthropic (June 1, $965B S-1)** and **OpenAI (~June 8, $852B S-1)** both filing confidentially and both eyeing **fall 2026 listings** ([`01` §5](./01-big-lab-moves.md#5-ipo-wave)), the frontier labs are transitioning from private growth stories to publicly-priced equities. Practical implications on hiring + comp:

- **Equity becomes liquid + priceable** — RSUs at a public company are real, tradeable comp. The pre-IPO "paper equity" discount goes away.
- **Comp gets standardized** — public companies bandit-audit their comp bands quarterly. Discretion at recruiter level narrows.
- **Hiring gets more disciplined** — quarterly reporting means headcount is a line item. Expect **clearer job ladders**, cleaner titles, and less "let's create a role for you" flexibility.
- **Roadshow-quiet-period constraints** — expect Anthropic + OpenAI to open Q3 hiring aggressively, then throttle in the pre-listing quiet period.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Apply to Anthropic + OpenAI **now**. Roadshow quiet-periods are 4–6 weeks. If either files a public S-1 in September, both will slow hiring through November. Your Q3 window is real.
- **Startup lens:** Two founder implications. **First**: once the labs are public, ex-lab talent liquidity is meaningful — the **next founder wave from Anthropic / OpenAI alumni** starts inside 6 months of IPO. Build your alumni-founder network now. **Second**: public labs = disciplined enterprise sellers. If you sell into their customer base, expect procurement to align around their public-priced compute.
- **Insight:** The IPO wave will make the labs a *safer* place to work (public equity + real ladders) and a *less exciting* one. If you value optionality (join now, leverage post-IPO alumni network, potentially found), the calculus is: **join before the S-1 goes public**. The window is measurable in weeks.

## 4. The vertical-wedge founder template — Claude-for-X, applied {#4-vertical-wedge}

**What happened:** With five verticals in eight weeks ([`02` §6](./02-new-emerging.md#6-vertical-template) — Legal, Small Business, Personal Finance, Finance, Science), Anthropic has published a repeatable founder template.

### The template (as a founder checklist)

- [ ] **Pick a vertical Anthropic hasn't done yet** — my current shortlist: **education, cybersecurity ops, construction, logistics, insurance underwriting, veterinary practice, municipal government.** The unifying property: **a high-volume "screen → narrow → decide" workflow with a bureaucratically-formalized memo at the end.**
- [ ] **Assemble the curated tool + data set** — **10–30 tools** in a first-party MCP catalog. Cite them by name in your first blog post.
- [ ] **Build the "screen → narrow → decide" pipeline** — one signature workflow, agentic, with a decision-artifact output (the memo).
- [ ] **Ship a launch-demo video** — the 90-second "here's the whole pipeline" clip. Anthropic did this for PKU; you do it for your vertical.
- [ ] **Land a partnership set** — 3–5 named partners in the vertical. Anthropic can hire Novartis / Lilly / J&J; you can hire 5 mid-sized firms in your vertical for pilots.
- [ ] **Build the eval + severity story** — cite MCP-Bench + your own domain-specific eval + the AI-jailbreak-severity framework from [`03` §1](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#1-fable-5-usage).
- [ ] **Prep the acqui-hire optionality** — even if your ambition is IPO, structure the equity + IP for a possible license/hire outcome ([`02` §5](./02-new-emerging.md#5-acquihire)).

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Even if you don't found, working through this template for one vertical is the best portfolio artifact you can build in Q3. It demonstrates: vertical selection, MCP + eval fluency, product intuition, partnership thinking. All the FDE + Founding-Engineer roles you're targeting will value it.
- **Startup lens:** This is the founder wedge. **Pick a vertical, use the template, ship in 8 weeks.** Anthropic is your platform partner; the FDE hiring surge in your vertical is your customer-acquisition team; MCP-Bench + your evals are your defensibility. **Do not overthink this — do it.**
- **Insight:** The winner in each vertical will be whoever is *first* to become the reference workflow + tool-graph + eval-suite. Land-grab dynamics apply. Speed of shipping matters more than perfection of design.
