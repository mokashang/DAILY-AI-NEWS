## 2. Apply to Claude Corps before Friday July 17 {#2-claude-corps}

**What it is:** A **12-month, full-time, in-person, paid fellowship** placing **1,000 fellows** at US **nonprofits** to help them use Claude. Anthropic committed **$150M** to the program.

**Structure:**

- **Salary:** **$85,000/year**, plus a **$10,000 grant** to accompany the placement.
- **Training:** Intensive up-front training by **Anthropic + CodePath**; **5 hours of ongoing training/week** during the placement.
- **Eligibility:** Anyone **18+ with <2 years full-time work experience**, US work-authorized, **willing to relocate if necessary**, **comfortable using Claude**. **No degree requirement.**
- **Employer of record:** **CodePath** (the largest US collegiate CS education nonprofit). Anthropic funds and steers the program. **Social Finance** runs measurement/evaluation.
- **Timeline:**

### Why this is *specifically* right for you

- **Fits ME.md exactly.** The ME.md profile is *"CS grad, ambitious, wants startup or SDE/MLE/AI role at a frontier-adjacent company."* Claude Corps is: paid Anthropic training + in-person integration work at a real org + 12 months of resume that says *"trained by Anthropic to deploy Claude in production."* That's the highest-quality single line for a next-step Anthropic Solutions / FDE application.
- **Optionality-preserving.** The fellowship *doesn't* commit you to nonprofit work forever. It commits you to *shipping a Claude-integration outcome at a real US org for 12 months*, which is the *exact same skill investment* required for a for-profit FDE role. Then you can pivot.
- **Startup-parallel.** The nonprofit workflows Claude Corps is targeting — grant writing, compliance, ops — are the same workflow shape that produces the *most defensible vertical-agent startups*. Every fellow will finish with domain-specific IP that they *own*. Take the fellowship as a **12-month founder residency in an under-served vertical.**
- **The 0-degree gate matters.** Anthropic is signaling that hiring signal comes from **evidence of Claude fluency**, not from the school. If you're at all worried about your degree being second-tier for lab hiring, Claude Corps is the intended door.

### Do this today (2 hours)

1. **Read the Anthropic FAQ + apply-page carefully.** Anthropic wrote it themselves — the questions they emphasize on the FAQ are the ones weighted most in review.
2. **Draft two 500-word essays:**
   - *Why AI + nonprofit for me now.* — Connect it to a specific problem you'd want to work on (K-12 tutoring, small-nonprofit ops, immigration legal aid, healthcare access — pick one, be specific).
   - *A time I shipped a Claude integration.* — Even a small one. If you haven't, do a **weekend MCP-server ship** (from [ME.md](../ME.md) portfolio backlog) and reference it.
3. **Line up two references** — one CS professor / mentor + one person who's seen you ship (peer, open-source maintainer, hackathon partner).
4. **Submit by Thursday July 16 EOD** — Friday-close applications get a *disproportionate* rejection rate for well-known operational reasons. Don't be the person hitting submit at 11:59 PM PT Friday.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Even if you're rejected, the *essays you write* are 90% reusable for the Anthropic AI Safety Fellowship, Solutions / FDE applications, and OpenAI Residency. It's the *only* application in your queue that produces re-usable copy.
- **Startup lens:** Compare **12 months at $85K + $10K grant + Anthropic training** vs. **12 months bootstrapping unpaid solo on your own startup idea.** For most CS grads with a runway concern, Corps is *strictly better optionality* — you finish with real revenue history, real integration IP, and 999 program alumni who are pre-warm intros to their next employer.
- **Insight:** Anthropic committing $150M to fund *the integration layer* is the biggest single career signal of 2026 for people in the ME.md profile. Treat this application with the seriousness of a big-lab interview — same effort tier, same expected return.

## 3. FDE / MLE / new-grad job market — the durable picture {#3-market}

Three data points to keep in front of you while writing the Corps essay:

### FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)

- Postings **+>1,000% YoY through early 2026** (per Perspective AI's 1,000-post analysis).
- Comp bands: **$300K–$550K TC for mid/senior**, **>$1M for frontier-lab principals.**
- Skills most-listed: **customer discovery, problem decomposition, AI product judgment.** *Not* leetcode, not raw modeling.
- Hiring concentrated at **Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Databricks, and YC-backed application startups.**

**Source:** [Perspective AI — 2026 FDE Hiring Trends](https://getperspective.ai/blog/2026-fde-hiring-trends-what-1000-job-posts-reveal) `[analysis]`

### MLE (Machine Learning Engineer)

- Base salary avg **~$160K**; **big-tech TC $300K–$500K.**
- **Highest-premium skills** (each adds $20K–$50K+ over generalist rates): **LLM fine-tuning · RAG architecture · large-scale model training.**

**Source:** [Articuler — AI Jobs Salary in 2026](https://www.articuler.ai/resources/learn/ai-jobs-salary/) `[analysis]`

### New-grad reality check

- **35% of entry-level jobs require AI skills.** Share of full-time postings mentioning AI **~2× YoY → 4.2%.**
- **BUT:** recent-college-grad unemployment reached **5.6% in March 2026** — the market is bimodal (AI candidates get hired; generic CS grads compete with a shrinking non-AI pool).
- **Evidence beats credentials.** Employers increasingly hire on *deployed project · public model · measurable result*, not diploma tier.

**Source:** [CNBC — By the Numbers: The Class of 2026 Job Market](https://www.cnbc.com/select/class-of-2026-hiring-stats-and-ai-trends/) `[secondary]`

### Practical translation

- **Weekend project → job.** The evidence-based signal is *exactly* what your ME.md portfolio backlog produces. Ship the MCP server this weekend if it's not already shipped.
- **Apply to 2 FDE roles per week.** With +1000% YoY volume, the funnel is *not* the bottleneck — the *filter side* is. Volume + precise skill-token match wins.
- **Don't wait for a title change.** If your target is FDE at Anthropic or OpenAI, apply now with the resume you have, using the *integration-layer* framing from [`§1`](#1-integration-layer). You will get better with each rejection; late applicants are ~always weaker.
