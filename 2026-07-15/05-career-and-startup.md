## 3. Consumer-AI compliance is a real, hire-a-team lane — starting today {#3-consumer-compliance}

**What:** With China's anthropomorphic-AI rules taking effect today ([`01` §2](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-china-anthropomorphic)) and the [Colorado AI Act (2026-06-30)](../2026-06-30/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-colorado-day-1) already operative, the **assurance-lane 3-sublane keyword tree** ([2026-06-30/05 §3](../2026-06-30/05-career-and-startup.md#3-policy-lane)) extends to a **fourth sublane: consumer-AI compliance engineering.**

- **Sublane 1 — Policy Engineering** (post-DOD-lawsuit AUP work).
- **Sublane 2 — Federal Access-Control Engineering** (post-Mythos-relift cleared-customer deployment).
- **Sublane 3 — State Compliance Engineering** (post-Colorado-Act impact assessments).
- **Sublane 4 (NEW) — Consumer-AI Compliance Engineering** (per-region conversational-agent product policy: minors / persistent-identity / private-conversation-training / manipulation restrictions).

**Named buyers already in market:** Meta AI Companion + Character.ai + Replika + Talkie + Kindroid + Snap My AI + Discord Clyde + xAI Ani.

**Named buyers who *will* be in market once CA/EU/UK/AU mimic CAC:** every US LLM lab with a consumer app, plus TikTok/YouTube (recommendation → generation adjacency), Instagram/Roblox/Twitch (minor-adjacent surfaces), and health-adjacent chatbots (Woebot / Wysa / Youper).

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** For an under-explored angle in an FDE application, cite this fourth sublane specifically — most applicants only know about the state-compliance sublane. Practical prep: read the [full CAC Interim Measures text](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/china-ai-companion-rules/) once, so you can name three specific restrictions in an interview.
- **Startup lens:** A **regional-AUP compliance-as-a-service** wedge is real. The B2B buyer is any consumer-AI product shipping in three or more regulatory regimes; the sold artifact is a *machine-readable policy layer + regional-inference-routing + audit log* that lets them prove per-region compliance without hiring 12 lawyers. Named the "GDPR-2018 analog" wedge — [2026-06-30/02 §2](../2026-06-30/02-new-emerging.md#2-deployer-wedge) — this is the consumer-side version of the deployer-side wedge that already exists.
- **Insight:** Regulatory arbitrage windows in tech open, then close in 24–36 months (see: GDPR, CCPA, Digital Markets Act). The AI equivalent is opening *right now*, and the tools are underspecified. Whoever ships the first credible cross-jurisdictional consumer-AI compliance stack owns the category by 2028.
