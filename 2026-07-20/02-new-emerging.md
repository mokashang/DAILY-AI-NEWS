# New & Emerging — 2026-07-20

Two threads dominate this week. **First**, a **vertical-AI funding wave that keeps compounding** — Harvey, Lovable, Glean, Hebbia, Neko Health, Emergent, Oak — with **agent-startup deals hitting $1.8B across 12+ rounds in July alone**, average valuations up ~40% QoQ to $280M, and **~88% of AI startup capital going to US-based companies**. **Second**, the *shape* of the wave: capital is concentrating where **AI meets the physical world and high-stakes operational decisions** — regulated workflows, hardware, specialized models that ship into production, not chat surfaces. Vertical AI didn't win this week — vertical AI *finished* winning.

## 2. Neko Health $700M — preventive imaging as an AI-native health primitive {#2-neko}

**What happened:** **Neko Health closed a $700M Series C** — the single largest health-adjacent AI-inflected round of the month. Neko builds **full-body scanning clinics** (skin analysis, cardiovascular imaging, hormone/blood biomarkers) and runs the imaging through in-house AI models. Co-founder: **Daniel Ek** (Spotify). The round makes Neko one of a very small handful of **AI-native, physical-footprint health companies** with enough capital to scale a clinic network across markets.

**Sources:**
- [Crunchbase News — The Week's 10 Biggest Funding Rounds (Neko lead)](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-energy-biotech-joulent/) `[primary]`
- [AI Funding Tracker — Neko Health $700M Series C](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-startup-funding-news-today/) `[aggregator]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Neko's hiring surface is **imaging-ML + clinical-workflow eng + hardware-integration**, not generic AI eng. If your CS-grad-school work touches vision (segmentation, medical imaging, calibration), Neko's Series-C stage is exactly the "join-before-they-hire-recruiters" window. Even if you don't target Neko directly, add **"AI-native + physical-footprint"** as a category to your job-hunt filter — Nvidia's Isaac + Anthropic's Isomorphic + Neko + a growing set of robotics companies are all hiring similar hybrid ML-eng-plus-real-world profiles.
- **Startup lens:** Neko is a **hardware-plus-workflow-plus-model** stack — three-layer defensibility. If you're building an AI startup in a category where a physical footprint is *possible*, the Neko round argues that the extra CapEx buys you a moat that a pure-software Claude-wrapper never can. Cost: the round size (~$700M) is the *table stakes*, not the *upside*.
- **Insight:** **Preventive health is a policy tailwind story as much as a tech story.** US employers + Medicare Advantage plans are *desperate* for preventive-diagnostic mechanisms that shift cost curves left. An AI-native Neko-style clinic is the closest thing the market has to a scalable answer. Add **"preventive health" and "clinical AI-workflow"** to your WATCHLIST.md alongside the biotech/protein/materials science thread — same buyer thesis (regulated, high-stakes, workflow-native), different vertical.

## 3. Harvey $200M @ $2.1B, Emergent $130M — the two shipped AI-vertical playbooks converge {#3-harvey-emergent}

**What happened:** **Harvey AI** — the AI-for-large-law-firms leader — closed a **$200M Series C at $2.1B**. **Emergent** — a fast-rising AI-coding company — closed a **$130M Series C** (valuation undisclosed). Two companies, two verticals (legal + code), but the *shape* of the round is identical:

- Both **shipped an agentic product** ahead of the round.
- Both are **vertically-integrated on eval** — they don't just run Claude/GPT under the hood; they own the harness, the evaluator, and the domain-benchmark that proves the answer is right.
- Both are hiring **customer-eng / FDE / integration** roles at a ~2× rate of core engineering — the growth constraint is *deployment throughput*, not model quality.

Harvey pairs with the earlier **Claude-for-Legal** vertical launch ([2026-05-13/02](../2026-05-13/02-new-emerging.md)); Emergent is the direct AI-coding competitor to Cursor, Cognition/Devin, and the Anthropic-native `claude` CLI.

**Sources:**
- [Tech Startups — Venture Capital & Startup Funding Roundup, July 6, 2026 (Harvey lead)](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/06/venture-capital-startup-funding-roundup-july-6-2026/) `[aggregator]`
- [Crunchbase News — biggest funding rounds (Emergent)](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-energy-biotech-joulent/) `[primary]`
- [AI Funding — AI Agent Startup Funding July 2026: Trends & Analysis](https://aifunding.me/insights/ai-agent-funding-july-2026) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Harvey and Emergent are two of the highest-signal "growth-stage AI vertical" landing pads for a CS grad targeting FDE / Customer-Eng / Solutions.** The specific tell to look for on their JDs: **"eval harness ownership"** and **"customer-domain benchmark authorship"** — that's the artifact you can ship *before applying*. Build a small legal-eval harness (contract-clause extraction, precedent citation, redline diff) or a coding-eval harness (SWE-bench-Verified subset with your own scoring), post it publicly, cite it in your cover letter. **The eval artifact is the modern coding-challenge substitute.**
- **Startup lens:** Both rounds validate the **"vertical + eval-owned" wedge** — the *hardest* part of building an AI vertical is not the model, it's owning the *scoring function that proves you beat the incumbent workflow*. If you're picking a wedge, the go/no-go question is: **can I build the eval harness with a 3-month dataset collection effort, or will it require regulated data access I can't get?** If the answer is "yes I can build it," the wedge is real. If "no, the data is a moat someone else already has," pick a different wedge.
- **Insight:** The **coding-agent space is now a 4-way race**: Cursor (raised $2B+ this cycle), Cognition/Devin ($25B — [2026-05-12/02](../2026-05-12/02-new-emerging.md)), Anthropic's own Claude Code (the disaggregated leader on GitHub commit share — [2026-05-14/01](../2026-05-14/01-big-lab-moves.md)), and now Emergent at $130M Series C. **The next 6 months will decide which 2 of the 4 stay independent** — plan any career or founding bet accordingly. Emergent's fit differentiator (per their pitch: "AI-first coding for teams that already ship") looks small enough to survive if incumbents don't attack the segment; larger enterprise coding-agent seats will likely consolidate.

## 4. Oak $60M seed — AI-native identity as the next Zero-Trust {#4-oak}

**What happened:** **Oak raised a $60M seed** — an unusually large seed even in a hot market — for **"AI-native identity management."** The pitch, distilled from public coverage: as agents proliferate and act on humans' behalf, every enterprise needs a first-class answer to **"which agent, acting on whose behalf, with what scope, and how do I revoke and audit?"** Oak positions itself as the *agent* replay of the Okta-era identity-provider category.

**Sources:**
- [Crunchbase News — biggest funding rounds (Oak seed)](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-energy-biotech-joulent/) `[primary]`
- [Fundup AI — Recently Funded Startups Jul 2026](https://fundup.ai/recently-funded-startups) `[aggregator]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Identity + AI is a two-tailwind lane** — every enterprise needs it, the buyer is the CISO office (a well-funded seat), and the required skill set is a rare combo of *authN/authZ engineering* + *LLM-agent instrumentation*. If your background touches security, OAuth, RBAC/ABAC, or SIEM — this is your best-fit AI-adjacent lane. Add Oak, plus the incumbent-vendor AI-identity teams (Okta AI, Auth0 AI, Cloudflare Access-for-Agents) to your apply list.
- **Startup lens:** The **agent-identity wedge** is a small enough starting point that a 2-person team can ship v1 in a quarter, and it composes with three other threads on this repo: (a) **Anthropic's MCP + hooks** ([2026-05-21/03](../2026-05-21/03-practical-skills-and-tools.md)), (b) **Exaforce's agentic SOC** ([2026-05-22/02 §2](../2026-05-22/02-new-emerging.md#2-exaforce)), and (c) **the EO cyber clearinghouse** (surviving half — [2026-05-22/01 §1](../2026-05-22/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-eo-postponed)). Re-rank STARTUPS.md this weekend: **agent-identity/scope-management** deserves a top-3 slot.
- **Insight:** The most important thing about Oak isn't the $60M — it's that **a specialist category-creating startup can still raise a $60M seed in 2026.** That's a signal that VC is still willing to pay a *category premium* for well-scoped, well-named wedges even in a "capital-concentrated top-of-market." Contrast with a generic "AI-productivity" pitch which now gets pattern-matched to the pile.
