## 1. The hiring market — read it honestly {#1-market}

**What the data actually says (mid-2026):**

- **ML-engineering demand-to-supply gap ≈ 3.2:1** — ~1.6M open roles, ~518k qualified candidates. Sounds good.
- **But: only ~6% of ML-engineer job ads are entry-level.** ~70% are mid-level / senior IC; ~2% director+.
- **New-grad tech hiring is down ~50% YoY at top companies**, entry-level tech hiring down ~25% overall.
- **Median ML-engineer salary ≈ $197k**; senior at top labs $470k–$630k. The comp is real; the shape is barbelled.
- **75%+ of AI-engineering postings explicitly require domain specialization.** Generalists are screened out before the first interview.
- **~490 new US ML roles posted per week** — steady, not booming.

## 2. Specialty-as-moat — pick your lane before Aug 1 {#2-specialty-moat}

**Why the deadline.** The [White House framework](./01-big-lab-moves.md#3-wh-framework) is announcing in the first week of August. It will re-price two lanes in real time:

- **Pre-deployment evaluation / AI assurance** goes from *anticipated* to *actively hiring*.
- **Trust & safety / applied-AI-integration** widens because of the [Erdős-model incident](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-erdos-escape) — labs need people who can talk about instruction-hierarchy resolution and sandbox design without being annoying about it.

**Lane map** — pick *one* to lead with, one to keep as a hedge:

| Lane | Signal strength | Best portfolio artifact | Best entry role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Integration Engineer / FDE** | 🟢 strongest | MCP server + MCP App + Tasks action (see §3) | Anthropic FDE, OpenAI FDE, Sierra Customer Eng |
| **Applied AI / Trust & Safety** | 🟢 widening now | Instruction-hierarchy linter + sandbox supervisor MCP ([03 §3](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#3-sandbox-lessons)) | Anthropic Applied AI, OpenAI Safety Systems |

**How to translate this into concrete moves this week:**

1. Pick **one** primary lane and **one** hedge. Write them down in ME.md.
2. Rewrite your resume's top-of-page **"specialty summary"** in the vocabulary of the primary lane. Two sentences, plus one artifact link.
3. Pick your **3 warm-intro asks** — one at your primary lane, one at your hedge, one at a vertical company. Draft the outreach *and* the artifact you'd link before you send.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The specialty lane is the answer to *"what do you actually do?"* — a question that used to be forgivably vague and no longer is.
- **Startup lens:** Same lane, same specialty. Pick one buyer persona and go deep before you go wide.
- **Insight:** The 75% specialty rule ([§1](#1-market)) plus the 30-day framework clock is the reason to move now. In August you're competing for a wider set of listings; you want your artifact and your positioning already published.
