## 2. Pricing your startup wedge against open-weight COGS (Together AI $800M read-through) {#2-open-weight-cogs}

**The market signal** ([`02` §1](./02-new-emerging.md#1-together-ai)): Together AI hit **$1.15B+ ARR** running **open-weight models at 6–20× lower cost** than closed frontier APIs. That's a *decisively priced* market — not a thesis anymore.

**The founder implication:** For any "Claude-for-X" wedge you're evaluating in [`STARTUPS.md`](../STARTUPS.md), **model two cost floors**:

1. **Closed-frontier COGS**: Anthropic API or OpenAI API list prices — your unit cost if you need frontier reasoning quality.
2. **Open-weight COGS**: Together AI list prices — your unit cost if a well-tuned open-weight model on their platform can do the work.

For most vertical-B2B workloads (document processing, code review, agentic RPA, structured extraction), the **open-weight COGS is 10× lower**. That's the difference between a 30% and a 70% gross margin at price parity.

**The corollary:** **Two-tier architectures win** — open-weight for the core loop, frontier for the hard tail. Any wedge that assumes closed-frontier-for-everything has a systematically worse P&L than one that plans a tiered architecture from day one.

**Sources:** *(see [`02` §1](./02-new-emerging.md#1-together-ai))*

## 3. Timing an IPO-window job app (OpenAI / Anthropic) {#3-ipo-window-jobs}

**What we know:** [Altman told employees this week](./01-big-lab-moves.md#5-openai-ipo-window) OpenAI expects to go public "within the next year." Anthropic still targeting October 2026 listing.

**The tactical takeaway** — three principles when timing a pre-IPO job app:

1. **Pre-IPO RSU grants are *bigger*** than post-IPO grants for the same title. The company is trying to close hires before the S-1 goes public and the pricing benchmark shifts to public-market comp comparables. **This is the highest-EV window to accept a frontier-lab offer for the next 24 months.**
2. **Post-S-1 quiet periods slow hiring for 60–120 days** (~15 days pre-roadshow through the IPO date). Don't wait for those windows.
3. **Post-IPO comes with Sarbanes-Oxley discipline**: clearer ladders, more predictable calibration, but a *narrower* hiring bar. The pre-IPO window has looser bars because the company is racing to fill headcount before the public disclosure.

**Practical:** apply to **Anthropic Solutions / FDE** and **OpenAI FDE** by **end of August**. That gets your interview loop scheduled before either window closes.
