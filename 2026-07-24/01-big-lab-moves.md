Three stories, one theme: **who runs the agent in production matters more than who tops the benchmark.** The **China-distillation fight** escalated from "Alibaba named on 2026-07-06" to "OSTP Director publicly accuses a *frontier-competitor lab* of *industrial theft*" and Treasury moved sanctions from "possible" to "weighing." **OpenAI shipped Presence** — the second US frontier lab (after Anthropic Managed Agents) to sell an actual managed-agent deployment platform, making the pivot **3-for-3**. ## 1. Kratsios / White House accuses Moonshot of distilling Anthropic's Fable — Treasury weighs sanctions {#1-moonshot-fable}

**What happened:** On **Wed July 22**, **Michael Kratsios**, Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP), publicly said the Trump administration has information indicating **Moonshot AI covertly distilled Anthropic's Fable model** — which has only been *publicly available since July 1* — to train **Kimi K3**, Moonshot's newly-released and (by many benchmark accounts) **most impressive open-source model shipped to date**.

- **Kratsios's specific claims:** Moonshot built **"a sophisticated internal platform capable of conducting distillation across US models while rapidly switching between different access methods to avoid detection,"** and additionally **acquired NVIDIA GB300-equipped servers and accessed GB300 chips located in Thailand**, in what he framed as export-control evasion.
- **His framing:** **"this is basically theft, not routine engineering."** Small-scale, disclosed distillation is legal and common; **covert industrial-scale** distillation of a US frontier model is the specific behavior alleged here.
- **Treasury is weighing sanctions** — reporting names **OFAC and the Bureau of Industry & Security** as the relevant instruments; no formal entity-list add yet as of today (July 24).
- **Independent expert pushback:** Fable was only *publicly available since July 1*. Multiple researchers have publicly questioned whether **~3 weeks** is enough time to distill a frontier-scale model at industrial scale, suggesting either (a) Moonshot had earlier private access via a different route, (b) the distillation is narrower than Kratsios implies (specific capabilities, not the whole model), or (c) Kimi K3 is largely its own architecture and the "distillation" claim is misapplied. **Kratsios has not published technical evidence.**
- **Prior thread continuity:** the [2026-07-06 delta](../WATCHLIST.md) already named **Alibaba** as the coordinator of "~25K fraudulent accts / 28.8M exchanges (Apr–Jun) — the largest distillation campaign in Anthropic's history." **The Kratsios statement escalates that thread from vendor-detection to government-accusation.**

## 2. OpenAI Presence launched (Jul 22) — the "sell agents, not models" pivot goes 3-for-3 {#2-openai-presence}

**What happened:** On **Wed July 22**, OpenAI announced **Presence**, an enterprise platform for building, governing, and running **production voice and chat agents**. It closes the loop the industry has been watching for 6 months: **all three US frontier labs now sell the same product shape — a *governed agent* bundled with *FDE-led deployment*.**

- **What Presence bundles:** (a) **company policies + SOPs** the agent must follow; (b) **permission controls** limiting which data and systems the agent can access; (c) **escalation rules** that route to humans when needed; (d) **pre-deployment simulations** that test the agent against common requests, edge cases, and high-risk scenarios; (e) **Codex-driven continuous improvement** — OpenAI's Codex reviews live interactions, identifies weaknesses, and *proposes* behavioral changes that human staff must approve before they ship.
- **Delivery model:** **limited GA**, delivered *with* **OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineers and select partners** — same "distribution comes with humans" template as Anthropic Solutions and Palantir FDEs. Directly ratifies the [Ode with Anthropic $1.5B JV](../WATCHLIST.md) thesis from July 15 that **the labs will deliver managed-agent products through humans, not just APIs.**
- **Proof point OpenAI cited:** Presence already **handles ~75% of OpenAI's own developer-support inbound** — the credible internal-adoption number that used to be a marker for a mature Anthropic launch.
- **Category positioning:** direct answer to **Anthropic Managed Agents** (announced Code w/ Claude May 8; rolled through Q2) and **Google's ADK 2.0 / Managed Agents in the Gemini API** ([2026-05-20/01](../2026-05-20/01-big-lab-moves.md)). **All three frontier labs now compete on the same product shape.**

- **Job lens:** Presence is a **hiring event**. What survives: **(a)** *vertical* agent products (industry-specific SOPs, domain-tuned escalation — Sierra for CX, Harvey for legal, Chapter for Medicare, Chai for biotech per [2026-07-15/02 §1](../2026-07-15/02-new-emerging.md#1-chai)); **(b)** *infrastructure below* Presence — evaluation, simulation harnesses, observability, cost routing (the [Bespoke Labs $40M](./02-new-emerging.md#1-bespoke-environments) wedge); **(c)** the *"we deploy Presence for you"* integration-consultancy layer. The frontier labs *know* the model layer will commoditize (see also [2026-07-23/02 §1 Together AI](../2026-07-23/02-new-emerging.md#1-together-ai) — the open-source-inference thesis at $1.15B ARR is the same story from the customer side), and they're racing to own the deployment layer before it does.

## 3. Claude Opus 5 — the July 23 launch that didn't happen {#3-opus5-noshow}

**What happened:** Prediction markets, several outlets, and multiple leak trackers pointed to **Thursday July 23** as Claude Opus 5's launch day. As of Friday July 24, **it has not shipped**. There is:

- **No new Anthropic launch post**, no new Opus model card, and **no `claude-opus-5` string in the public Claude API**.
- **Current live Claude lineup:** **Opus 4.8** (May 28) · **Sonnet 5** (June 30, now the default for Pro / Team / Enterprise) · **Fable 5** (Mythos-class, **restored globally July 1** after the June 12 jailbreak-triggered pause, per [WATCHLIST 2026-07-06 China follow-through](../WATCHLIST.md)) · **Opus 4.7** (still the enterprise default for many customers with silent tokenizer re-pricing per [2026-07-20/03 §3](../2026-07-20/03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#3-tokenizer-cost)).
- **Pre-launch signals that *were* live and *aren't* resolved:** (a) a research checkpoint codenamed **"Honeycomb EAP"** briefly appeared in **Cursor** around **July 9**, reportedly showing a **1M-token context window** and an **"xhigh"** reasoning-effort tier with a safety fallback to Opus 4.8; (b) a **`claude-opus-5`** model string was reportedly spotted on **Google Vertex AI's Model Garden** around **July 14**. Both unconfirmed by Anthropic or Google.
- **Anthropic developer-platform housekeeping this week** *did* ship (but is not itself a launch signal): **Claude Opus 4.1 deprecated**, scheduled to retire on the Claude API **August 5, 2026**; the **`agent-memory-2026-07-22` beta header** was added (stable server-defined order + tightened `depth`/`path_prefix`/`cursor` behavior); **HIPAA self-serve config** shipped for Enterprise + API orgs.
- **Anthropic's actual July product cadence** — everything else that shipped while Opus 5 didn't: **Claude Science** (Jun 30, biopharma), **Sonnet 5 as default** (Jun 30), **Fable 5 restoration** (Jul 1), **Ode with Anthropic** JV named (Jul 15), **Claude for Teachers** (Jul 14–15), **Record a Skill** (Jul 21, per [2026-07-23/03 §2](../2026-07-23/03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#2-record-a-skill)), **Claude Security plugin** (Jul 22, per [2026-07-23/01 §2](../2026-07-23/01-big-lab-moves.md#2-claude-security)). **That is a shipping velocity that dwarfs any single model launch.**

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Model release slips don't change hiring cadence — **product / deployment moves do.** The Sonnet-5-becomes-default flip (June 30) and the Claude Security plugin (July 22) matter more for your Anthropic-stack projects than an Opus-5 leak. Practical move: **update every project README to say "Claude Sonnet 5 default; Opus 4.8 for hard tasks; Fable 5 for Mythos-class cyber"** — that one line signals you tracked the June 30 default flip and the July 1 Fable-restore, and haven't been asleep at the wheel. Skip any project write-up that still names "Claude 3.5 Sonnet" or "Opus 4."
- **Startup lens:** The *reason* Anthropic is quiet on Opus 5 is a leading indicator. When a lab that has been shipping monthly since Q4 2025 goes silent for **~4 weeks** on its flagship line, it's usually one of: **(a)** **safety-eval regression** blocking release (the Fable 5 pause on June 12 shows the eval floor is now real), **(b)** **infra bottleneck** on the Colossus tenancy ([2026-05-21/01 §2](../2026-05-21/01-big-lab-moves.md#2-anthropic-colossus)), or **(c)** a **re-scoped launch bundling Opus 5 with a bigger product moment** — a Managed Agents 2 announcement, a Claude Code milestone, or the next fundraise close (the ~$965B S-1 window in [WATCHLIST — 2026-07-15](../WATCHLIST.md) is October). All three are more likely than "the model doesn't work." **Bet accordingly if you're building on the Anthropic API — don't lock any launch plan to "Opus 5 will land by X."**
- **Insight:** Watch what *did* ship instead. **Claude Science · Sonnet 5 default · Fable 5 restoration · HIPAA self-serve · agent-memory beta · Record a Skill · Claude Security plugin · Ode JV**. That's *at least eight material product / distribution moves in a month*. The competition permanently moved off "who has the smartest model" and onto **"who ships the most usable enterprise agent stack."** Every project you build should be doing the same — shipping the *deployment substrate*, not just calling an LLM.
