## 3. The safety narrative you'll need in every values-fit interview {#3-safety-narrative}

**What happened:** The [FLI Summer 2026 Safety Index](./01-big-lab-moves.md#4-fli-safety-index) put a **C+ ceiling** on the industry and made **the military-use reversal** public. Combined with:
- The **EU AI Omnibus** postponing high-risk deadlines but **keeping Article 50 transparency for August 2 (this Sunday)** ([`02` §3](./02-new-emerging.md#3-eu-omnibus))
- **US AI policy** in continued flux post-2024 election
- The **Anthropic + OpenAI + DeepMind defense-contract pivot** publicly documented for the first time

…the values-fit interview at any AI-serious company is now a **substantive conversation about tradeoffs the industry has already made**, not a philosophical exchange.

**The three questions to have crisp answers on:**

1. **"What do you think of the FLI C+ grade?"**
   - Weak answer: "safety is important."
   - Better answer: "The C+ ceiling tells me two things — the frontier labs are being evaluated against a rising bar they defined, and Anthropic's lead on transparency and governance is real but incremental. As a buyer or a builder, I'd read C+ as 'good relative to peers, not good in absolute terms' — which is a reasonable starting point for enterprise deployment plus a compelling reason to invest more."
2. **"What do you think about the military-use reversal?"**
   - Weak answer: "I'm concerned."
   - Better answer: "It reflects a market reality — the largest sustained AI-compute buyer is going to be a government, so a lab that categorically won't sell to governments is not viable at frontier scale. The question I care about is *which controls* the labs put on their defense products — specifically, deployment-time human-in-the-loop, use-case allowlisting, and post-deployment audit access. I'd want [Anthropic/lab X] to publish their internal deployment-controls policy for defense specifically, the way they publish their acceptable use policy for commercial."
3. **"Would you personally work on a defense-adjacent product?"**
   - **Honest answer** (and honest is the *right* answer) — say what you actually think, briefly, and why. Interviewers are screening for people who reason, not for people with pre-canned takes. If your answer is "no, and here's why" that's fine; if it's "yes, under X conditions," name the conditions specifically.

**The one-page memo — [`03` §4](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#4-this-weekends-artifact) has the structure.** Build it Friday night; keep it in a private gist; refresh quarterly.
