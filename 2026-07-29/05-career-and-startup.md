## 2. FDE market: freshest numbers on comp and hiring shape {#2-fde-market}

**What.** [Perspective AI's 2026 FDE Compensation Report](https://getperspective.ai/blog/2026-forward-deployed-engineering-compensation-report-1200-fdes) surveyed 1,200 FDEs. Updated numbers vs. [2026-07-25 §2](../2026-07-25/05-career-and-startup.md#2-fde-market):

| Level | 2026 median TC | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Mid | **$385K** | $310K–$470K |
| Staff | **$610K** | $500K–$780K |
| Principal (frontier labs) | **$1.2M+** | up to ~$1.6M |
| Palantir (median) | $215K | reference: legacy FDE org |
| Anthropic / OpenAI (senior) | **$785K+** | new-tier comp |

Equity is now **55–70% of TC** (up from 35–45% in 2024). The comp variance correlates almost 1:1 with the **customer-facing percentage** in the JD.

**Related — 2026 FDE hiring shape:** [Perspective AI hiring trends analysis](https://getperspective.ai/blog/2026-fde-hiring-trends-what-1000-job-posts-reveal) surveys 1,000 job posts. Standouts:
- **224 open FDE-shaped roles** across 39 AI companies ([Jobs by Culture](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-boom-2026))
- **~60% wash-out on the customer-conversation round**, consistent with last week's data.
- **65%+ concentration in SF and NYC** — but Dallas / Miami / Seattle growing fastest.

**Sources.** [Perspective AI comp report](https://getperspective.ai/blog/2026-forward-deployed-engineering-compensation-report-1200-fdes) `[primary]` · [Perspective AI hiring trends](https://getperspective.ai/blog/2026-fde-hiring-trends-what-1000-job-posts-reveal) `[primary]` · [Recruiting From Scratch salary guide](https://www.recruitingfromscratch.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-salary-in-2026-full-guide) `[analysis]` · [KORE1 hiring guide](https://www.kore1.com/hire-ai-forward-deployed-engineers-2026/) `[analysis]` · [FDE Pulse jobs board](https://fdepulse.com/) `[aggregator]` · [Jobs by Culture — 224 roles](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-boom-2026) `[aggregator]`

**Why it matters.** The FDE role is now **the highest-paid non-founding path in AI**, and the wash-out point is the customer-conversation round — not coding. That's actionable: it's a specific, practiceable skill, not a moving target.

3. **Check [FDE Pulse](https://fdepulse.com/)** weekly — it's now the most reliable single aggregator.

**Job · Startup · Insight.**

Do it this weekend if you're not doing the artifact from [`03` §4](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#4-this-weekends-artifact) — do the artifact if you're choosing between them.
