## 1. AI Engineer market — the lane is now structural {#1-ai-eng-lane}

**What the data says (August):**

- **~1,550 US AI-engineer postings/week**, held flat since March 2026 while general SWE postings stayed depressed (Axial Search / Pin data).
- **~1.6M open positions / ~518K qualified candidates ≈ 3.2:1 shortage.** YoY posting growth **+143%** on the AI Engineer title (extends the [+143% figure first cited on 2026-05-15](../2026-05-15/05-career-and-startup.md#1-ai-engineer-fastest); the number has held).
- **AI Engineer TC:** $134K–$193K national midpoint; **LLM specialists $220K–$280K base** at frontier-adjacent companies (add 30–70% for equity).
- **Applied AI + LLM Engineer** searches +47% YoY in Recruits Lab data — the *largest* engineering category increase they track.
- **Python 62% / cloud 55% / foundation models 51%** of postings — full-stack fluency is more valuable than deep single-tool specialization.
- **Geographic distribution:** CA 22% · NY 11% · TX 10% · **remaining 57% distributed** across the country — remote/hybrid still the norm.

### Read this against your profile

- **Job lens:** The lane hasn't cooled. Two months of held-flat postings against continued depression in general SWE means "AI Engineer" is now a structural rather than promotional category. The **3.2:1 shortage** is your leverage — a candidate who has *shipped* two Claude-based artifacts (say, this repo's suggested MCP server + Claudeforce cost-log) is *ipso facto* in the top 1/3 of the applicant pool.
- **Startup lens:** The talent shortage means **your co-founder problem is real.** A CS grad with Claude fluency can attract technical co-founders faster in 2026 than at any prior moment. Use that.
- **Insight:** Watch the *TC compression* signal. If LLM-specialist base drops below $200K by Q1 2027, the lane is starting to normalize. If it stays flat or grows, the shortage is durable and this is a 3+ year window.

## 3. Federal AI lane — reopened for the Anthropic stack after the DoW ruling {#3-federal-ai-lane}

**What changed:** The [Aug 27 First-Amendment ruling](./01-big-lab-moves.md#3-anthropic-dow-ruling) vacated the DoW's supply-chain-risk designation that had blocked Anthropic from federal defense-adjacent procurement queues. **Effect on hiring:**

- **Anthropic Federal / Public Sector Applied AI Engineer** roles will re-post with expanded headcount within 30 days.
- **Prime contractors** (Booz Allen, Leidos, SAIC, CACI, Peraton) who had de-prioritized Claude-specific staffing while the SCR held will now compete for the same Anthropic-fluent candidates.
- **Clearance-sponsorship** offers become more common — a strong Claude portfolio + willingness to sponsor for TS/SCI has historically been the fastest path into federal AI work.

### Application playbook (this week if federal is on your radar)

1. **Anthropic Careers → Public Sector filter.** Set a saved alert; check daily for the next 4 weeks.
2. **Booz Allen Hamilton careers → search "Claude" or "generative AI".** Historically the fastest post-ruling hirer among the primes.
3. **ClearanceJobs.com** — filter *AI Engineer* + *no clearance yet, willing to sponsor*. Real reqs exist here that don't post to LinkedIn.
4. **Cold-DM two people on LinkedIn** who currently hold Anthropic Public Sector or Booz Allen Claude titles. One-line message: *"Congrats on the Aug 27 ruling — I'm a CS grad student building on the Anthropic stack ([artifact link]), and would love 15 minutes to hear how the hiring picture is shifting on your side."* Two DMs, no more, tight message.

**Sources:**
- [Anthropic Careers — Public Sector](https://www.anthropic.com/careers) `[primary]`
- [ClearanceJobs.com](https://www.clearancejobs.com/) `[aggregator]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Federal AI has the **highest job security** in tech right now (budgets set by law, not by market sentiment) and pays competitively for cleared engineers. It's also the lane most under-appreciated by the CS-grad-student demographic — competition is lighter.
- **Startup lens:** GovTech-on-Claude is *newly investable* [`01` §3](./01-big-lab-moves.md#3-anthropic-dow-ruling). A founder combining Anthropic fluency + a security-cleared team member (recruit an ex-military CS grad from a G.I. Bill program) has an unusually defensible wedge.
- **Insight:** The ruling is precedent that reverberates beyond Anthropic. Every AI vendor will now be more willing to make policy statements in public — because the retaliation-via-procurement lever just got legally weakened. This makes **AI-policy-adjacent hiring** (Anthropic's Policy team, OpenAI's Global Affairs team) more active in Q4.

## 4. Startup wedges — the 3 lanes still raising, and 4 wedges to prune {#4-startup-wedges}

**What August funding data confirms** (from [`02` §2](./02-new-emerging.md#2-funding)):

**Three lanes that raised big:**
1. **Industrial / physical AI** — Prometheus $12B / $41B, Emerald AI, Gatik, Stellaria. Look for founders with hardware / manufacturing background.
2. **Regulated verticals** — Harvey $150M / $8B (legal), Chapter Medicare-AI (healthcare, prior editions), Anthropic vertical alliances (finance, legal, healthcare). Look for founders with deep domain expertise in one regulated field.
3. **AI-native dev-tools infra** — Fireworks $1.5B / $17.5B (inference), CodeRabbit $143M Series C (code review), Together AI, Modal, Baseten. Look for founders with ML platform / distributed systems experience.

**Four wedge categories that stopped raising (or should have):**
- **Generic "Chat with your docs"** — the incumbent RAG providers commoditized this to $0. Do not start here.
- **Generic Claude/GPT wrapper** — Salesforce, Slack, Notion, etc. now do this themselves as connectors.
- **Post-training-as-a-service (untargeted)** — Amazon retreated from this in July [`2026-07-25/01` §3](../2026-07-25/01-big-lab-moves.md#3-amazon-agi-lab); labs beat startups on economies of scale. The exception: [verified-reward domain fine-tunes with symbolic verifiers](../2026-07-25/04-research-progress.md#5-slai-t-rex).
- **Standalone AI-detection** — Anthropic's watermarking mandate [`01` §6](./01-big-lab-moves.md#6-anthropic-other) is starting to eat this category.

### Update to your wedge-log ([STARTUPS.md](../STARTUPS.md))

If you have entries under *"Chat-with-your-docs"*, *"Generic Claude wrapper"*, *"Untargeted fine-tuning"*, or *"AI detection"* — move them to a **"parked"** section with the Aug-31 date. Do not delete (useful history), but do not evaluate any further.

Add three new wedge entries for evaluation:

2. **Vertical-specific Claudeforce integrator** — a Claudeforce-native firm for, e.g., life-sciences CRMs (Veeva) or education CRMs (Salesforce Education Cloud).
3. **AI provenance-verification service** — enterprise console for watermark-audit workflows across Claude / GPT / Gemini as they roll out marking.

**Sources:**
- [Extruct — YC W26 Batch Breakdown](https://www.extruct.ai/research/ycw26/) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Wedges that stopped raising = companies that will lay off in the next 6 months. If you're considering a startup-first job offer, check whether the company's wedge is in the 3-that-raised or the 4-that-didn't lanes. Simple test, real signal.
- **Startup lens:** As above — prune your own wedge log, don't waste further evaluation on a stopped-raising lane.
- **Insight:** The middle is dying. In consumer AI you compete against ChatGPT + Gemini at 1B MAU each [`01` §5](./01-big-lab-moves.md#5-google-gemini); in enterprise horizontal you compete against Claudeforce and the coming ServiceNow / Workday equivalents; in generic infra you compete against Fireworks / Together. The **only durable seams** are *hard physical* (industrial), *hard domain* (regulated), and *hard integration* (dev-tools infra + boutique verticals).
