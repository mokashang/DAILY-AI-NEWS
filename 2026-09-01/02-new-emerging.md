## 1. Discovery Loop: Jeff Dean + Ghemawat + Vinyals + Quoc Le co-found the highest-density AI startup of 2026 <a id="1-discovery-loop"></a>

**What happened.** Announced alongside the [Google DeepMind reshuffle](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-deepmind-reshuffle):

- **Founders (four):**
  - **Jeff Dean** — Google chief scientist, 27-year Google veteran; architect of MapReduce, Bigtable, TensorFlow-era infra.
  - **Sanjay Ghemawat** — Google Senior Fellow; co-architect of Google's foundational infrastructure with Dean.
  - **Oriol Vinyals** — VP of research at Google DeepMind; technical lead on Gemini; StarCraft AlphaStar / Seq2Seq.
  - **Quoc Le** — co-founder of Google Brain; AutoML-Zero.
- **Structure:** Delaware **Public Benefit Corporation** (PBC).
- **Mission:** **Automate the experimental loop of scientific research itself** — start by automating ML research, then expand to hardware design, drug discovery, clean energy.
- **Google's role:** **founding investor + Cloud partner.** Not just letting them leave — actively bankrolling and providing compute.

## 2. Clay raising at $7B pre-money (up from $5B in January) <a id="2-clay-round"></a>

- 40% valuation bump in ~8 months. Confirms **agent-native GTM tools** as one of the few AI product categories where enterprise revenue is scaling ahead of valuation.
- Competitive frame: Clay + Rox + Regie + Common Room + Ramp Sales AI = the "agentic RevOps" tier.

**Sources.**

- [Crescendo — Latest AI VC deals](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-vc-investment-deals-in-ai-startups) `[aggregator]`

- **Job.** Clay-tier startups typically hire **Solutions / Applied AI / Growth Engineer** — hybrid roles that sit between sales, engineering, and product. Comp is often equity-heavy but with meaningful upside at these valuation trajectories.

- **Insight.** The market has picked its winner in generic agentic sales enrichment. **The next round of category winners will be defined this quarter in agentic customer success, agentic renewals, agentic support ticket triage** — categories where Clay doesn't yet compete but where the same buyer (RevOps leader) will consolidate spend within 18 months.

## 3. Keenable — $26M seed for web search built for AI agents <a id="3-keenable"></a>

**What happened.** **Keenable** exited stealth with a **$26M seed round led by Accel** to provide **web search infrastructure tailored for AI agents.**

- Positioning: Google/Bing-tier search results are optimized for humans (10 blue links, snippet cards, ad slots). Agent-consumer search wants **structured, high-recall, dedup'd, freshness-tagged, source-verifiable JSON** — a materially different product.
- Adjacent competitors: **Parallel Web Systems** ([2026-05-19](../2026-05-19/) $100M Sequoia); Tavily; Exa; Serper.

**Sources.**
- [AI Weekly — AI News Today](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) `[aggregator]`
- [Crescendo AI — VC deals](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-vc-investment-deals-in-ai-startups) `[aggregator]`

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** Search-for-agents is a niche with **relatively small teams and high hiring bars per role.** Reach out to Keenable's founders directly if this is your lane — a seed-stage company with fresh Accel money hiring engineers 3–5 will move fast.
- **Startup.** The "agent-native infrastructure" thesis (search, vector DB, browser runtime, code sandbox, evaluation harness) is still under-served for **specific verticals** — search-for-legal-agents, search-for-biotech-agents, code-search-for-repo-agents. Wedge exists.
- **Insight.** **Web search is being unbundled by consumer type.** Human search stays with Google; agent search consolidates around 3–4 API-first players; some agent stacks build their own crawlers rather than pay per query. If you're building an agent, **budget for search cost from day one** — it's structurally different from the "free web" assumption.

## 4. Aziron (Aziro) — enterprise agent execution platform <a id="4-aziron"></a>

**What happened.** **Aziro launched Aziron**, an **enterprise agent execution platform** that brings **agents, workflows, documents, models and enterprise tools into a single governed environment.**

- Positioning: **the "agent runtime" for the enterprise CIO office** — governance, audit, cost tracking, tool-catalog, human-in-the-loop review flows.
- Adjacent: **LangGraph Platform, LlamaIndex Agents, CrewAI Enterprise, and Anthropic's Agent SDK managed offering.**

**Sources.**
- [AI Weekly — AI News Today (Sept 1)](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) `[aggregator]`

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** These platforms hire **DevRel + Solutions Architect + integrations engineer** roles. If you have MCP-server or Agent-SDK samples in your portfolio, you can walk in with pattern-matched credibility.
- **Startup.** The "agent runtime for the enterprise" market is **10 companies chasing the same 500 Fortune-2000 buyers.** Late to be the horizontal winner; earlier for **vertical runtimes** (biotech agent runtime, banking agent runtime, defense agent runtime) where compliance requirements gate out generic platforms.
- **Insight.** Enterprise agents are shifting from **"can we run this at all"** to **"can we run 500 of these safely."** The bottleneck moved from model quality to **runtime governance**. Add "agent governance" to your interview vocabulary.
