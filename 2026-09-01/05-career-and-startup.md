## 1. AI Engineer salary + demand snapshot (Q3 2026) <a id="1-ai-engineer-snapshot"></a>

**Numbers to hold in your head walking into any recruiter conversation this quarter.**

- **Demand:supply gap: 3.2 : 1** — ~1.6M open AI engineer positions vs. ~518K qualified candidates.
- **AI-skill wage premium: 56%** (up from 25% a year ago). Premium scales with seniority: 6.2% entry, 11.9% mid, 14.2% senior, **18.7% at staff**.
- **National ML/AI engineer salary range: $134K–$193K base.** Metro leaders: **San Jose $206.7K**, Boston $189.3K, NYC $189.3K.
- **FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer): +1,000% YoY through early 2026, ~1,206 US open roles.** Comp bands: **$300K–$550K TC** median-through-staff, frontier-lab principal roles clearing **$1M+ TC**.
- **MLE openings vs. Feb-2020 baseline: +59%.** General SWE roles: **declined significantly** — the substitution effect is real and well-documented now.
- **Concentration of FDE demand**: Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Databricks, YC-backed startups.

## 5. The eval-engineering / classifier-constitution lane <a id="5-eval-engineer-lane"></a>

**Emerging title.** "Trust & Safety Classifier Engineer" / "Evaluation Engineer" / "Alignment Research Engineer" — the same role three ways depending on lab. Anthropic's [Fable 5 safeguards writeup](./01-big-lab-moves.md#7-fable-5-safeguards) is the case study.

**Portfolio path.**

1. Pick a policy space (something *specific* — e.g. "what should a coding agent refuse to do when it sees an `.env` file").
2. Write the classifier constitution — 5–10 pages of enumerated rules with examples of allowed vs. safeguarded content.
3. Generate a labeled test set (500 examples, ~200 hand-labeled, rest synthetic).
4. Train / fine-tune a small classifier (Haiku 4.5 fine-tune, or a distilled BERT-tier model).
5. Report FP/FN rates. Iterate on the constitution.
6. Publish the whole thing — constitution, test set, notebook — as a repo.

**Where the roles are.**
- **Anthropic** — Trust & Safety, Alignment Team, Safety Research.
- **OpenAI** — Safety Systems, Preparedness Team.
- **Google DeepMind** — Responsible AI, Safety Reliability.
- **Applied AI at frontier labs and their consulting partners** — increasingly, as enterprise customers demand vendor-specific classifiers for their internal deployments.

**Sources.**
- [Anthropic — Improving Fable 5 Safeguards](https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-fable-5-s-biology-safeguards) `[primary]`
- [Interconnects — Fable 5 and new AI safety fables (Nathan Lambert)](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/claude-fable-5-and-new-ai-safety) `[analysis]`
- [Anthropic Careers — Safety and Alignment](https://www.anthropic.com/careers) `[primary]`

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** This is a **credentialing shortcut**: one well-executed public constitution + evaluation harness can substitute for months of otherwise-unstructured resume padding. Interview-ready without a PhD.
- **Startup.** "Constitution-editor + eval-runner" is a real potential enterprise-SaaS product for orgs deploying LLMs internally. Not a solo project — but a real B2B business.
- **Insight.** As models get faster to change, the **classifiers around them become the slow-moving surface** that carries corporate values. That's a durable stack layer worth investing in.
