## 6. Pipeline note — time your Anthropic Applied AI loop against the S-1 drop {#5-anthropic-loop-timing}

**What:** Anthropic's S-1 dropping ([`02` §1](./02-new-emerging.md#1-anthropic-ipo)) resets **grant math** for anyone hired in the pre-→post-IPO window.

- **Before S-1 file → offer letter:** options-heavy grants; more upside but more risk.
- **Between S-1 file and IPO:** grants shift toward RSUs; upside compresses but downside disappears; extended lockup is common.
- **Post-IPO:** normal public-company RSU math.

If your target is joining Anthropic, the **highest-optionality window is *right now*** — before the S-1 drops publicly and hiring loops compress against public-company disclosure timing.

### DO this month
1. **If you have an Anthropic loop in flight — compress the timeline.** Reply to recruiter within 24hrs of any prompt.
2. **If you don't yet — send your best application this weekend.** Reference the Fellows track ([§1](#1-fellows-hackathons)) as the parallel path.
3. **Track any offer's grant type explicitly.** Options vs. RSUs matters more than $ base at this stage. Ask what the effective grant date will be relative to lockup provisions.

### Why it matters to you
- **Job lens:** A 30-day timing difference on grant type can be worth **6-figure differences** in year-1–3 optionality math. This is real money; treat the loop as a compressed decision, not a leisurely process.
- **Insight:** Public-company hiring at frontier labs will look **more like standard big-tech hiring** post-IPO — comp gets more disciplined, grants smaller, disclosure heavier. The 2023–2025 "join a lab and get world-changing equity" era is closing. If you want that shape, this is the last window.
