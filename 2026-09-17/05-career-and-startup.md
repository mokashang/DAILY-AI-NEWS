## 1. Market — AI Engineer still fastest-growing, 3.2× demand gap, senior climbing {#1-market}

**What the numbers say (mid-Sept 2026):**

- **AI engineering postings grew ~14% quarter-over-quarter.** Fastest-growing AI hiring category by volume.
- **~1.6M open positions** globally vs **~518K qualified candidates** = **~3.2× demand-supply gap.** YoY posting growth ~143%.
- **Median AI engineer salary $176K**; **senior** climbing significantly higher.
- **Anthropic has the highest retention** of any lab (per interview-prep coverage). **OpenAI's 2-year retention rate ~67%** — high by tech-industry standards, low by frontier-lab standards.
- **FDE demand still surging** — most FDE JDs are unfilled 60+ days.
- **Entry-level scarcity intensified this quarter** — the gap widened. Experienced engineers still get the leverage.

## 2. Skill re-price update {#2-reprice}

**Coming into this week's news:**
- **Model-fluency** (which model is best) — deprecating fast. Nobody can be current.
- **Model-routing + eval-authoring** — appreciating. Router + eval-suite is the interview-differentiator.

**After this week's news:**
- 🔺 **Prompt-as-router / deliverable-shaped prompting** — new skill. Anthropic's one-Claude UX made the *prompt itself* the router. Writing a **spec-shaped prompt** that reliably produces a finished Doc / Slides / Design / spreadsheet is a new competence that most senior engineers **cannot demonstrate yet**. Your 90-min weekend template pack is a resume-line.
- 🔺 **BYO-cloud enterprise deployment** — new skill. EFS + regulated-industry deploys just became the hiring-criteria-of-the-quarter for Solutions roles. Reference-app + questionnaire template = differentiator.
- 🔺 **Sponsored-agent measurement / attribution** — new skill. FDE roles at OpenAI's ads org will hire around this specifically.
- ➡️ **Agent-memory design** — steady. Not repriced, but the [Memory in the Age of AI Agents](./04-research-progress.md#3-memory-survey) survey is now the reference.
- ➡️ **Cost-aware agent design** — steady. Fable 5.1 cache economics reinforce that this is table stakes, not differentiation.
- ⬇️ **"Which productivity app do I open"** — deprecating fast. The UX just merged; the answer is *neither Cowork nor chat, it's the prompt*.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Rewrite your LinkedIn "About" this weekend** to reflect prompt-as-router + BYO-cloud enterprise deployment. Those two phrases will be under-supplied in candidate copy for the next 30 days.
- **Startup lens:** If you're founder-shaped, the fastest **product-market-fit story to pitch a seed VC this month** is "we let regulated buyers deploy Claude in their own cloud, with an audit-ready policy pack, in 5 minutes" — because that is the *questionnaire pattern* every AI startup needs.
- **Insight:** Every time the frontier ships a **new deployment shape** (EFS, Sponsored Agents, one-Claude, WebMCP), a matching **skill vocabulary** gets minted. Owning the vocabulary early = being cited in interview prep threads for 6 months.

## 4. Founder wedge to sit with this weekend — "agent-mediated attribution" {#4-founder-wedge}

**Thesis:** OpenAI's Sponsored Agents launch (Sept 16) means **ad-mediated agent economics are live**. Advertisers will pay for **conversations that convert**, but **no measurement standard exists** — every advertiser today is buying blind. First-mover on a **cross-platform, open-standard sponsored-agent attribution pixel** captures the ad-tech vendor position for the entire AI-first ad market.

**Why now:** OpenAI won't ship third-party attribution (against their interest). Google will ship a proprietary attribution linked to Gemini/Ads/DV360, but not cross-platform. Anthropic won't play at all. **The neutral position is open.**

**Your-fit assessment (fill in this weekend):**
- Do you have **any** ad-tech / measurement / analytics experience? (If no: cofound with someone who does — this is the wedge to *find a cofounder* around.)
- Can you ship a **v0 pixel** as an open-source SDK that any advertiser can drop into their sponsored-agent flow in 90 days?
- Can you get **10 letters of interest** from advertisers currently buying ChatGPT Ads test-inventory in the same 90 days?
- Would you accept **YC W27 batch / seed check** as the funding path, or are you playing bootstrapped?

**If you say yes to 3+ of the above,** this wedge earns a 90-min block on your calendar this Sunday to draft a one-pager.
