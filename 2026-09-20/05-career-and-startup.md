## 1. The AI-Engineer hiring map — where the roles actually are, September 2026 {#1-hiring-map}

**What happened:** LinkedIn's Jobs on the Rise 2026 report + F5 Hiring Solutions + Dice + Ohio Tech News all reconfirm: **AI Engineer is the #1 fastest-growing job title in the United States**, with postings up **143% YoY.** Under that headline number:

- **Salary bands.** Levels.fyi median TC $245K–$269K at big-tech employers. Glassdoor base median $142K (typical range $114K–$180K). Ranges up to **$350K+** at frontier labs / hedge funds. **Machine Learning Engineer new-grad average $128K/yr; typical range $101K–$155K** (ZipRecruiter, Sept 16). **Salaries range from $180K to upwards of $350K** for the mainline AI Engineer role.
- **Wage premium.** Workers with AI skills now command **62%** more than comparable non-AI peers (up from 57% a year earlier).
- **Remote / hybrid share.** **53%** of AI Engineer roles are remote or hybrid — one of the highest in tech.
- **Geographic concentration.** San Francisco, New York City, Dallas (Ohio Tech News).

## 2. The Anthropic S-1 — a reader's playbook (Q4 2026) {#2-s1-playbook}

**What happened:** [`01` §1](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-anthropic-nasdaq) — Anthropic picked Nasdaq for October, $800B–$2T reported ranges. **The S-1 filing is now the single highest-signal document you'll read this year for career + startup + investing planning.** Here's a checklist for what to look for the day it drops.

### The 12 rows to look for in the S-1

| # | Row | Signal | So what |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Revenue split: Claude Code vs. Claude API vs. Verticals vs. Enterprise (Team/Enterprise seats)** | If Claude Code > 40% → developer-tools sub-sector re-rates upward | Claude Code adjacent roles / startups become premium; every dev-tool company reads its own valuation off this line |
| 2 | **Vertical revenue: Legal + Small Business + Financial Advisors split** | Which vertical is the biggest earner tells you where the FDE hiring goes next | Concentrate your applications there |
| 3 | **International revenue mix (US vs. UK/EU vs. APAC)** | London / Tokyo / Singapore hiring waves come after this line | The APAC number rules the Tokyo / Singapore JD availability |
| 4 | **Gross margin** | 60–70% = healthy SaaS; 40–50% = infra-heavy business (Colossus lease + inference COGS drag) | Refresh-grant math + Colossus vs. inference cost mix |
| 5 | **Compute-cost line: Colossus + other** | The $1.25B/mo Colossus tenancy from May should now be public | Every enterprise-Claude quote needs to be re-priced against this line — you can use it in interviews |
| 6 | **R&D spend (as % of revenue)** | If R&D% is falling and revenue is growing, "Claude builds Claude" is real leverage | Directly quantifies the [26% Claude-leads-R&D](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-claude-builds-claude) claim |
| 7 | **Customer concentration (top 10 by revenue)** | If Apple / Google / any single logo > 10% → concentration risk | If PwC (per [2026-05-15](../2026-05-15/00-tldr.md)) or JPMorgan or Schwab is a top-10 line, your interview prep needs their names |
| 8 | **Employee count + median comp** | Median comp = your negotiation floor | Bring it to a recruiter conversation |
| 9 | **Ad-revenue disclosure** | Anthropic pledged ad-free — see if the language is "no ads" or "no ads today" | Career + moat implication if the pledge softens |
| 10 | **Safety spend / evaluator FTE count** | The Sept 17–18 disclosure implies a growing budget line | Directly maps to the evaluator role availability |
| 11 | **Government revenue** | Any DoD / IC line = the Threat Intelligence Report has monetized side | Career: cleared-track roles |
| 12 | **Executive comp / options refresh** | Look for post-IPO grant schedules to understand equity pool remaining | Your offer math |

### How to use it

The moment the S-1 drops:
- **Tonight (S-1 day):** skim the summary + revenue split (rows 1–3 + 9). Publish a *150-word* takeaway on LinkedIn. That's a resume-visible timeliness signal.
- **Weekend (S-1 day + 2):** deep-read rows 4–8 + 10–12. Produce a **one-page S-1 map** (a public GitHub gist) — where every number in the S-1 maps to your career decision tree. This is your **second interview artifact of Q4** (after the DevDay predictions doc in [`03` §3](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#3-devday-watchlist)).
- **Following week:** apply to 2 Anthropic roles + 3 Anthropic-adjacent roles (Applied AI, Verticals) with the S-1 map linked in your cover letter.
