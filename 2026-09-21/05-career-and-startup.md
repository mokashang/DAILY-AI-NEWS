## 3. AI Engineer / MLE market — the 3.2:1 gap holds {#3-ml-engineer-market}

**What happened.** Mid-September market data:
- **AI Engineer**: #1 fastest-growing US job for the **second consecutive year**; ~$206K avg salary; +143% YoY posting growth.
- **MLE**: median $197K (national); openings ~490/wk sustained; **59% above pre-pandemic baseline** while general US tech hiring is ~36% below.
- **Demand vs supply**: ~1.6M open AI-engineer positions against ~518K qualified candidates — a **3.2:1 gap** that hasn't closed since Q1 2026.
- **Role composition**: ~70% of MLE roles are mid-level or senior IC; only ~2% Director+. **The market is IC-shaped, not manager-shaped**.

**Sources.**
- [Axial Search — AI Engineering Job Market: 43,500 postings analyzed](https://axialsearch.com/insights/ai-engineering-jobs) `[analysis]`
- [Axial Search — State of ML Engineering 2026](https://axialsearch.com/insights/ml-engineering-jobs) `[analysis]`

- [Futureproofing — AI Engineer Demand 2026](https://www.futureproofing.dev/resources/ai-talent-gap/ai-engineer-demand-2026) `[analysis]`
- [365 Data Science — AI Engineer Job Outlook 2026](https://365datascience.com/career-advice/career-guides/ai-engineer-job-outlook-2025/) `[analysis]`

**Why it matters to you.**
- **Job.** The 3.2:1 demand-supply gap is your primary tailwind. But note the *IC-shaped composition*: ~2% Director-plus means titles don't ladder the way SWE careers do — **skill mastery, not team ownership, is the lever**. Invest in one deeply-known primitive per quarter (this quarter: Anthropic-stack + MCP v2). Cadence over intensity ([ME.md personal rules](../ME.md#personal-rules)).
- **Startup.** If you're recruiting for a startup: MLE at 490 postings/wk sustained means **you cannot outbid Meta or a top-tier startup on comp alone**. Win on optionality: real product ownership, sub-10-employee equity share, technical proximity to founders.
- **Insight.** The AI-Engineer / MLE reprice is *stable*, not a spike. Two years of #1-fastest-growing status suggests the salary premium is a structural feature, not a bubble. Plan around 2027 as another year of the same. Recruit and build accordingly.
