- **The Big-4 delivery arms** — PwC / Deloitte / Accenture / EY / KPMG. Titles: "AI Delivery Consultant — Claude Practice," "Enterprise Agent Integration Lead."
- **The frontier lab's Solutions team itself** — Anthropic Solutions, OpenAI FDE, Google Gemini Enterprise Applied Science. These are the highest-leverage seats (customer-facing but with real product influence).
- **The system-integrator specialists** — Slalom, Atrium, Deloitte Digital, Publicis Sapient. Historically Salesforce-implementation shops that are pivoting hard to agent-surface integration this quarter.

**Skills that unlock this lane:**

1. **MCP-server author-experience** — you've shipped one. (Weekend project from [`03` §1](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#1-router-shim).)

4. **Agent governance primitives** — identity, allowlist, audit trail, per-tool cost caps ([`03` §2](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#2-agent-governance)).
5. **One vertical of legibility** — Legal OR Finance OR Life Sciences OR CRM OR HR. Pick one, invest 10 hours reading the vertical's regulatory + workflow context.
