## 2. The Claude Frontier Academy wedge — your highest-leverage October move {#2-frontier-academy-wedge}

**The signal (recap of [`01` §2](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-frontier-academy)):** Anthropic committed **$100M** to the **Claude Frontier Academy** — a **medical-residency-structured** program training **10,000 FDEs by end of 2027**. Initial cohorts drawn from the **Claude Partner Network**: **Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, Novo Nordisk, EY, PwC**, more TBA.

**Why this is the single-highest-leverage career move for your profile:**

1. **It solves the FDE experience catch-22.** Entry-level FDE roles ask for *"production enterprise deployment experience"* — which you can't get without an FDE role. The Academy's **"simulated enterprise deployment"** admissions bar is designed to accept candidates who don't have production exp yet.
2. **It's a credentialed path, not a lottery.** Comparable programs (OpenAI Residency, Anthropic Fellowship) are competitive one-off applications. The Academy is a **repeatable pipeline through partner firms** with ~10,000 slots over 15 months.
3. **The credential has distribution baked in.** Partner Network firms have **~1M consultants collectively**; the Academy's output is **positioned as the Partner Network's AI delivery arm.** Credentialed FDEs will get *routed into* enterprise deployments without having to independently job-hunt.

**What this does to the eval market:**

- **Frontier labs internal red teams + safety teams get a hiring boost.** Expect **OpenAI Safety Systems** reqs to post Q4; Anthropic's **Alignment / Red Team** has been hiring continuously since Mythos.
- **US + UK AI Safety Institutes** (CAISI / AISI) — the Astra cancellation strengthens the pre-deployment-review case; pre-deployment-eval contractor roles get budget allocation.
- **Independent eval orgs** — **METR, Apollo Research, Pattern Labs, Palisade** — hiring up as frontier labs pay for independent capacity.
- **Deployment-eval vendors** (new category) — "eval your own enterprise Claude/GPT deployment before shipping to prod." This is a cold-start startup category that's about to warm up.

**Where to position:**

**Resume keywords to add this week (if you haven't):**

- "Red teaming" (specifically: capability evals, dangerous-capability evals, alignment evals)
- "Eval harness authoring"
- "Model-assisted vulnerability research" (if you shipped anything per [`04` §3](./04-research-progress.md#3-cve-mythos))

**Portfolio artifact for this lane:** **A public eval suite with a documented failure-mode taxonomy** (minimum: 5 categories, 20 cases per category). Fork of **inspect-ai** or **swe-bench-live** as a starting point. Ship with real test harness code; don't just document a taxonomy.

**Where to apply:**

If you have a math/stats bent + patience for long cycles, apply here as a parallel track to Frontier Academy. The portable artifact (public eval suite) also helps the FDE apply — eval design is Academy-relevant.
- **Startup lens:** **"Deployment-eval-as-a-service"** is the single-most-underfunded wedge in the Oct 2026 funding map. Three competitors have raised; none have Series A. If you have an evals background, **this is the 3-month-build startup for Q4**.
- **Insight:** The Astra cancellation **changes the ROI math on eval investment** — every frontier lab now has an existence proof that eval capacity avoids a 7-figure comms + revenue hit. "Eval infrastructure" is now a defensive line item for AI budgets, not a nice-to-have.
