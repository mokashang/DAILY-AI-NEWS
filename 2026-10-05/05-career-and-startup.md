## 2. The 2026 new-grad reality — AI demand up, entry-level down {#2-market-reality}

**What happened:** Two numbers to internalize:

1. **The headwind:** Entry-level programmer employment is down **~27.5%** from the 2023 peak. New-grad hiring at top-tech companies is down **>50%**. In 2025, just **1 in 10 engineering hires at larger companies were recent grads**, down from nearly 3 in 10 in 2023.
2. **The tailwind:** AI engineer demand grew **~143% YoY in 2026**. **~14,800 open AI-engineer roles in the US** on Glassdoor (October 2026). **KORE1** puts the demand/supply ratio at **3.2 open AI/ML positions per qualified candidate**. **Entry-level MLE base: ~$90K–$135K** (down from the $150K band of 2024, but still a healthy premium over the broader-tech median).

The gap between the headwind and the tailwind is **"qualified"** — the demand is for people who can prove they can do the specific agentic/FDE/deployment work. Credentials alone don't bridge it; artifacts do.

## 4. Startup path — the two wedges to prototype this week {#4-startup-wedges}

Same two wedges from Sept, now with October-specific sharpening:

### Wedge A — Model-fatigue tooling → "Agent-shape tooling"

- **What to build:** Not just router/migration, but **agent-operator tooling** — observability for dots, telemetry for Claude subagents, policy-as-code for Custom Rules, incident-replay for agent runs. The $100/mo dot subscription is a product; the agent-operator tooling market around it is a $5B outcome.
- **Why now:** Dots just shipped. The observability vendors haven't shipped a dot-SDK yet. Six weeks of head start is all you need.
- **First-check target:** $500K–$1M pre-seed on a working demo + one real dot-operator who's been using your tool.

### Wedge B — Agent-native primitives (sublayer 3 from [`02` §3](./02-new-emerging.md#3-agent-infra-category))

- **What to build:** Pick ONE unfunded primitive: **identity, communication, authorization, reputation, dispute resolution, or storage.** Natural took payments; the other six are open.
- **Why now:** The Self-Organizing Agent Teams paper ([`04` §1](./04-research-progress.md#1-sat)) directly implies agents will need to recognize each other, trust each other, and negotiate roles — all of which assume a primitive layer that doesn't exist yet.
- **First-check target:** $500K–$2M pre-seed with an OSS reference implementation + integration with 2+ agent frameworks (Claude / dots / LangGraph).

### Do this weekend (4 hours)

Pick A or B. Write a 1-page memo + ship a 500-line reference impl. Post it. This is the primary step 1 for the founder-track; it's a secondary signal boost for the job-track.

### Why it matters to you

- **Startup lens:** The wedge shortlist is tighter in October than in September (Natural captured payments; dots-ops tooling has a 6-week-fresh window). Pick fast; ship fast; the market will reward October-shipped artifacts above November-shipped equivalents.
- **Insight:** The entire September-to-October arc is one message: **the deployment surface is the new product surface.** Both wedges above are deployment-surface plays. The non-deployment plays (new models, new foundation research, new chip architectures) are not for CS grads with 10 weekends of founder capital.
