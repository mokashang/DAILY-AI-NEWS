# Career & Startup — 2026-10-12

The last 60 days hard-split the hiring surface: **Anthropic growth-hire posture** (Opus 5.5 + Sonnet 5.5 + IPO prep + Claude Code revenue), **OpenAI platform-engineering posture** (ChatGPT-as-workspace, 1.2B weekly users, "not 2026" on IPO), **Google deferred** (Gemini 4 in post-training), **Mistral opened in Europe**, and **Instinct + the Oct-8 wave** inject ~700–1000 near-term engineering slots at well-funded startups. ## 1. The hiring map — Q4 2026 state {#1-hiring}

**Overall:** AI-engineering hiring is holding at **~1,550 new postings per week** (Axial Search July 2026 baseline; no October decline signal reported). Volumes ranged **~930–2,000+ postings/week** through late April → June, with no clear downward trend.

**Compensation range (reported):**

- **Median AI-engineering salary:** ~$176K (broad market).
- **Staffing-firm averages:** $200K+ (likely skewed senior).
- **MLE median base (per 2026-09-10 edition):** $200K.
- **LLM-specialist base range:** $220–280K.
- **FDE total comp (reported, unverified):** $350–550K mid-to-senior at OpenAI / Anthropic.

**Seniority distribution:**

- **66%** of postings target individual contributors.
- **35%** mid-level, **31%** senior.
- **Entry-level (0–2 YOE):** **only ~2.5% of postings** (Glassdoor-based analysis). This is the structural pinch.

**Candidate dynamics:**

- AI engineering is the **single hardest AI role to hire for** (per Axial).
- Companies **routinely lose top candidates in under 3 weeks** (one staffing-firm source).

**The split in Q4:**

| Lab / Company | Posture | Why | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anthropic** | 🟢 Growth-hire | Opus 5.5 + Sonnet 5.5 + IPO this fall + Claude Code at ~4% of all public commits | **60%** of apply effort |

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The split in the table above is the single most important re-prioritization since May. **60% of apply effort to Anthropic** is defensible because of three independent reasons (growth posture + IPO window + public-markets grant pricing). **20% to OpenAI** is specifically for FDE / Applied-AI / Platform-Engineer roles where the DevDay platform turn creates slot churn. **10% to Google** is a long-tail hedge for Vertex Agent / Antigravity teams. **5% to Mistral** if you have EU work auth; else deferred.
- **Startup lens:** The ~2.5% entry-level pinch is the clearest **candidate-pool asymmetry** of 2026. Every startup wants senior AI engineers; almost none are set up to train junior ones. **Three implications for a CS grad:** (a) your senior-signalling portfolio (router + eval suite + MCP server + Dots-and-Computer-Use demo) is **the hiring loop short-circuit** — present yourself as "senior-trajectory in a CS grad body" and skip the entry-level filter; (b) **Series B/C startups with ~1 year of runway and no new-grad program** are easier gets than big labs with structured new-grad pipelines (counter-intuitive but true in this market); (c) the **entry-level pinch is a founder wedge** — "junior-AI-engineer apprenticeship as a service" would raise easily in Q4; add to [STARTUPS.md](../STARTUPS.md).
- **Insight:** The staffing-firm "<3 weeks to lose a candidate" datapoint is the operational constant you want to exploit on your side. **If you have offers coming in, don't wait more than 2 weeks to decide between them** — the labs' urgency works for you only inside that window. Set a hard decision rule now so you don't waste leverage.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Pick the top three up-arrows that match your interests and put each on your LinkedIn headline + GitHub pinned repos + cover-letter opening paragraph. "AI Engineer | Model Routing · Agent Evals · Computer Use" is tighter than "AI Engineer | passionate about AI" and will survive the first-pass recruiter screen.
- **Startup lens:** The down-arrows are the categories where **a founder with real differentiation can still win, but a horizontal founder gets wiped**. If you're thesis-shopping, make sure your wedge sits on an up-arrow or in an orthogonal direction.
- **Insight:** The *speed* of the re-price (two months, Sept 10 → Oct 12, the "model-fluency" skill lost further value and three new up-arrows appeared) is itself the news. **AI-careers skill-pricing is now moving faster than the semi-annual re-skill cycle most CS grads budget for.** The counter-move is to pick one durable *meta-skill* (routing, eval-authoring, memory architecture) and ride it through 2027.

## 4. The OpenAI vs Anthropic split — operational playbook {#4-split}

**The problem:** Both labs are hiring; both labs are shipping fast; both labs have real roles for a CS grad. **Which one you prioritize is now situation-dependent, not identity-dependent.**

**Decision matrix:**

| If you... | Target first | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Have a routing / eval / MCP portfolio | **Anthropic Solutions / FDE** | Portfolio maps 1:1 to Anthropic's job families; IPO grant window open |
| Have a product-engineering / integration portfolio | **OpenAI FDE / Platform-Engineer** | DevDay platform turn created new slot shapes |
| Have research-leaning portfolio (benchmarks, memory, agent eval) | **Anthropic Applied-AI** OR **OpenAI Safety / Evals** | Both hire research-adjacent engineers; Anthropic Mission Programs is thinner-queue |
| Prefer earlier liquidity | **Anthropic** | Fall IPO vs OpenAI's "not 2026" |
| Prefer longer-dated upside | **OpenAI** | Private-stage valuation ceiling higher; grants more leveraged |
| Have EU work auth | **Anthropic London / Dublin** OR **Mistral Paris** | US tracks are more competitive; EU tracks are legitimately underpriced |
| Have strong UX / design-engineering | **OpenAI (ChatGPT native experiences)** | 1.2B weekly users = design-engineering is where OpenAI concentrates in Q4 |
| Have strong security / cyber | **Anthropic (Mythos team)** OR **OpenAI (Daybreak / Codex Security Cloud)** | Both have expanding dedicated teams; Daybreak is a Critical-rated-model restricted-access line |

**The honest answer:** apply to *both* for most CS grads, with the 60/20/10/5/5 weight from §1. The matrix above is for the *specific first-sent application*, not the overall strategy.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Running the decision as a *matrix* (not a brand loyalty) is itself the career signal. In an interview question "why Anthropic over OpenAI," answer with the matrix row that applies to you — specific, operational, not fan-based.
- **Startup lens:** The matrix doubles as a *startup co-founder matching* grid — if you're hunting for a co-founder, the engineers who apply this matrix *also* think in operator-terms, which is the co-founder trait you want.
- **Insight:** The brand-loyalty era of AI careers (Google 2017–2019, OpenAI 2022–2024) is ending. **2026 is the first year a CS grad can rationally hold applications at two frontier labs simultaneously without identity cost.** Treat the labs as instrumentally identical; let the matrix pick the first-pass target.
