## 1. The FDE market data — +1,000% YoY in postings, $188K median, $400K ceiling {#1-fde-data}

**What's confirmed:**

- **Postings:** FDE job postings jumped **>1,000% between Jan–Aug 2026 vs the same period 2025.** Source: Pave data via newsbytesapp roundup, cross-referenced with LinkedIn Economic Graph figures that previously flagged "AI Engineer" as #1 fastest-growing job title (+143% YoY, per Oct 2025 edition).
- **Median advertised salary:** **>$188K** (newsbytesapp, Pave).
- **Ceiling at Anthropic:** certain FDE roles report **~$400K total comp** (newsbytesapp, consistent with the Anthropic Solutions roles the Oct 14 edition quoted).
- **Pave level-normalized data:** FDEs earn **5.3% less than SWEs** on a level-normalized basis — i.e., **not a discount anymore**. The "FDE pays less than SWE" narrative that dominated 2024 is empirically dead as of mid-2026.
- **Narrower bands:** Glassdoor shows $124K–$198K annually (less-generous sample). Choppingblock.ai median TC ~$210.6K across 8 data points (small-sample).
- **New-grad applied-AI base:** $135K–$155K per betonai.net. Mid-level agent engineers: $180K–$225K.

### Why it matters to you

- **Validation of yesterday's two-track plan.** The Frontier Academy exists *because* the FDE market is up 1,000% — the Academy is Anthropic's response to a hiring bottleneck. **Partner-firm applications are *more* valuable now than yesterday**, because the partner firms are also 1,000%-up on their own FDE-equivalent hiring (McKinsey "AI Integration Engineer," Deloitte "GenAI Specialist," Accenture GenAI Studio).

New grad applied-AI base of $135K–$155K is already SWE-competitive; FDE ceilings at Anthropic are *above* most SWE ceilings. **Rewriting your résumé headline to "AI Integration Engineer" is now a strictly dominant move over "Software Engineer."**

## 2. Layoffs vs productivity: NBER / CFO survey — 9× planned cuts, no productivity gain, builders protected {#2-layoff-vs-productivity}

**What's confirmed:**

- **NBER / CFO survey:** CFOs plan **9× more AI-driven layoffs in 2026 vs 2025** — but **measured productivity gains have not materialized** at the firm level.
- **Employer surveys:** 22% say AI is reducing staffing needs among companies scaling back; **37% expect to replace roles with AI by end of 2026**.
- **Who's at risk:** High-salary employees first, then those lacking AI-related skills, then recent hires. (High-salary ≠ high-engineering-skill; this is biased toward ops / middle-management / support.)
- **Who's being hired:** **Platform engineering, data work, AI deployment.** Junior-developer employment is down 20% since 2024, but ML engineer openings are up 59%.
- **Count:** Oracle has made the largest cuts (>25,000 jobs YTD); 102 distinct layoff events have affected 51,686 workers in the US alone by late March 2026; 92,000 tech workers laid off in first 5 months of 2026; AI-attributed share ~39,000–55,000 (sources vary).

**Sources:**
- [InterviewQuery — NBER: CFOs Plan 9x More AI Layoffs in 2026, But Productivity Gains Haven't Arrived](https://www.interviewquery.com/p/ai-layoffs-2026-productivity-gap) `[analysis]`
- [JobsPikr — AI Layoffs 2026: ROI Reality Check](https://www.jobspikr.com/report/ai-layoffs-2026-roi-reality-check/) `[analysis]`
- [Analytics Insight — AI Layoffs vs AI Hiring: How Firms Are Restructuring Jobs in 2026](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/artificial-intelligence/ai-layoffs-vs-ai-hiring-how-firms-are-restructuring-jobs-in-2026) `[secondary]`
- [Staffing Industry — US firms eye job cuts in 2026 survey says](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/us-firms-eye-job-cuts-in-2026-survey-says) `[secondary]`
- [Resume Templates — 6 in 10 Companies Are Planning Layoffs in 2026](https://www.resumetemplates.com/6-in-10-companies-plan-to-lay-off-employees-in-2026-amid-economic-uncertainty/) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Counter-intuitive career signal:** the layoffs are concentrated in **ops / support / content / middle-management**, not builders. SWE / MLE / AI Integration / FDE roles are *gaining* postings — ML engineer openings +59%, FDE +1,000%. **This is actually a protective pattern for your target lanes.** Translate for interview use: "Builder-side roles are growing *because* of the ops-side cuts; the AI build-out requires more infrastructure engineers to execute the automation."
- **The productivity gap is interview gold.** "Why aren't CFOs seeing productivity gains despite 9× layoff pace?" is a question an interviewer may ask. The right answer: **(a)** cuts are being made on narrative, not output; **(b)** the automation shelf-life for ops roles is 2–3 years but the integration tax to realize it is longer than most CFOs model; **(c)** FDE / integration-engineer roles are the hiring that *fixes* this gap — i.e., your target role is the solution to the gap.
- **For startup pitches:** the productivity-gap data is an underwrite for **integration-services and FDE-tooling startups.** If CFOs are cutting on narrative but not getting output, the market is paying for a "guaranteed productivity realization" wedge. See [`02` §3](./02-new-emerging.md#3-companion-leg) for the barbell view.
