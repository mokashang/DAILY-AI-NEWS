**What this means for your profile:**

## 2. The skill re-price, October-17 update — "independent evaluator" is now a named external role (Track C opens) {#2-reprice}

**What re-priced UP this week:**

- **Evaluator-authoring as a career lane.** Anthropic's three-tier framework + METR embed ([`01` §1](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-metr-tier-1)) makes **"independent AI evaluator"** a legitimate standalone career title with a public hierarchy, published rules of engagement, and non-equity structure. Target orgs below (§3).
- **Vendor-vs-independent-benchmark fluency.** The Argon paid-tier rollout + the Artificial Analysis / Arena divergence ([`01` §2](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-gemini-4-argon)) makes "I know when the vendor benchmark lies, and how to run the independent one" a top-10 interview competency for AI Engineer / FDE / Solutions roles.
- **IoT / edge-AI safety.** The self-replication paper ([`04` §1](./04-research-progress.md#1-self-replicating)) opens IoT / edge-AI safety engineering as a specific lane. Expected hiring at Anthropic Red Team, OpenAI Safety, Google DeepMind, AISI/CAISI, and the CIDP partners (CrowdStrike / Palo Alto / Dragos / Booz).
- **Population-level eval design.** The arXiv self-orchestration / MetaShare wave ([`04` §2](./04-research-progress.md#2-arxiv-swarm)) adds "I can design evals for agent populations, not just single agents" as the Q4 2026 differentiator past the trajectory-accountability skill.
- **Observability / OpenTelemetry on agent runtimes.** The Claude Code v2.1.300 OpenTelemetry guidance ([`03` §1](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#1-claude-code-updates)) makes observability a *tooling* skill, not just a *process* skill. Pair with a published tracing dashboard for a top-decile portfolio artifact.
- **Dual-agent fluency (Claude Code + Codex CLI).** The Codex CLI 0.163.0 update ([`03` §2](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#2-codex-cli)) narrows the feature gap between Codex and Claude Code. Having experience shipping with both is more valuable than being deep in one.

**What re-priced DOWN this week:**

- **"Model fluency on the latest model."** Argon shipped vendor-leading benchmarks but **lost on Terminal-bench 4.0 to Opus 5.5** and placed 5pts below on independent Intelligence Index. The whole "I know the latest model's capabilities" résumé line keeps getting cheaper because the capabilities themselves are contested and shifting weekly.
- **"Model routing based on model selection alone."** Haiku 5.5 + effort-levels + Argon's wide capability spread mean *routing-by-model* is being replaced by *routing-by-model × effort × task-type × cost × independent-benchmark*. A 1-dimensional router is 2026-H1 era; the current state of the art is 4–5 dimensional.
- **"Vendor-reported benchmarks" as a credibility signal.** Argon + the OpenAI incident disclosures lag + METR's "we can publish without approval" line all make vendor-first citation suspicious. In an interview, lead with independent benchmarks.

**Three tracks you now carry on your career map:**

- **Track A — Direct frontier lab.** Still Anthropic Solutions / FDE / Integration, OpenAI FDE, Google DeepMind, xAI. (Unchanged.)
- **Track B — Frontier Academy partner firm.** Accenture / Bain / Capgemini / Deloitte / McKinsey / Morgan Stanley / Commonwealth Bank / Novo Nordisk. (From [Oct 14 §1](../2026-10-14/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-frontier-academy).)
- **Track C — Independent evaluator / safety org.** **🆕 NEW.** METR / SecureBio / Apollo Research / UK AISI / US CAISI. (From [`01` §1 of Oct 17](./01-big-lab-moves.md#1-metr-tier-1).)

The three tracks are mutually reinforcing — a candidate with artifacts speaking all three tracks' languages is unusually well positioned. **Build artifacts that straddle Tracks A/C** (eval-ready router) and **Tracks B/A** (FDE-ready deployment case study) first.
