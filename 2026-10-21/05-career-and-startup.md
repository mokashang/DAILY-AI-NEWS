## 1. Market snapshot — the entry-level squeeze is now measured {#1-market-snapshot}

**Open AI/SWE listings (Oct 2026):** ~**104,289** across US / UK / India / 6 others. **1-in-6 titled AI / ML** (roughly 17K). In the US, the most common AI-titled roles are **AI Engineer + Machine Learning Engineer**. About **4%** mention AI training / eval / labeling in the title. [[DEV JobRise](https://dev.to/jobrise/software-and-ai-engineering-jobs-in-october-2026-what-the-listings-show-336i)]

**Software postings index (Indeed):** climbed from **61.1 (May 2025 low)** to **78.3 (early Oct 2026)**. Rebound concentrated in **senior + AI-titled** roles. [[Pin.com Tech Job Market](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/)]

**Entry-level reality (Stanford, June 2026 data):** Workers **22–25 in AI-exposed jobs** are employed at **19% below less-exposed peers**. The gap shows up as **reduced hiring**, not layoffs or pay cuts. **This is the first academic quantification of the "AI eats juniors" thesis.** [[Ilinmaks AI jobs 2026](https://www.ilinmaks.com/blog/en/ai-jobs-market-2026)]

**Pay (Robert Half 2026 guide):** AI/ML Engineering: **$134K–$193K**. SWE: **$109K–$175K.** [[Robert Half](https://www.roberthalf.com/us/en/insights/research/data-reveals-which-technology-roles-are-in-highest-demand)]

**Supply gap (KORE1 estimate):** **3.2 open AI/ML positions per qualified candidate.** [[Herohunt](https://www.herohunt.ai/blog/fastest-growing-ai-roles-in-2026-data-and-rankings/)]

**Read-off for you:**
1. **The AI-adjacent-SWE lane is tighter at junior level than any time since 2020.** You cannot rely on "I know how to code" alone.
2. **The AI-titled sub-market is favored at 1-in-6 of all postings and higher comp band.** Your résumé title + top-of-fold line must say "AI" explicitly.
3. **Senior + AI-titled is the hottest cell;** your path is "move to the AI-titled column fast; the senior lane will come."

## 3. The skill re-price — routing + eval authoring premium confirmed {#3-skill-reprice}

**The thesis** (continuing from Sept 10 + Oct 14). Three skills repricing *up* in 2026:
1. **Model routing** (across vendors + effort levels)
2. **Eval authoring** (custom benchmarks per workload)
3. **Cost-aware agent design** (effort levels + caching + batch)

**One skill repricing *down*:** **model-fluency / "which Claude" / "which GPT"** — the parity of Argon + Opus 5.5 + GPT-6 at flagship tier makes this a tunable, not a judgment call.

**New data this week:**
- Three flagship-tier models within **1 order of magnitude on price**, **~10 pts on major benchmarks** → routing dominates picking.
- Argon's **40-day $2/$10 intro window** → cost arb is a specific, time-bounded skill with a clear deadline.
- Opus 5.5's **5 effort levels** → the "pick a cell" problem went from 1D (model) to 2D (model × effort).
- Code Review levels (Quick / Standard / Deep) in Claude Code → **policy design is also a repricing-up skill**.

**For your résumé this week:**

Replace this: **"Deep knowledge of Claude Sonnet / Opus and prompting best practices."**
With this: **"Built and benchmarked a cross-vendor model router across Claude Opus 5.5 (5 effort levels), Gemini 4 Argon, and OpenAI GPT-6 Sol/Luna; achieved X% cost reduction at Y% quality retention on a 10-case eval harness."**

Replace this: **"Prompt engineering."**
With this: **"Eval authoring (10 cases × 3 vendors with Opus-as-judge; dashboard on HF Spaces)."**

Replace this: **"Built an agent."**
With this: **"Built a cost-aware agent using the MCP-stateless spec with Claude Code auto-mode and background subagents; traced via OpenTelemetry."**

**Why it matters to you**
- **Job**: Interviews between now and Jan will ask these in some form. Have specific numbers from your weekend router artifact.
- **Startup**: These skills are a **real engineering wedge for your own co-founder story**. "I'm the person who makes the AI cost math work" is a legitimate founder resume.
- **Insight**: Hiring managers can smell "I've only called one API." Three vendors + an eval + a cost reduction = the opposite smell.
