## 1. Anthropic files S-1 with the SEC — Oct 26 — Nasdaq ticker `ANT`, GS + MS lead, roadshow reportedly week of Nov 9, controlled-company structure {#1-anthropic-s1}

**What happened (2026-10-26):** Anthropic PBC filed Form S-1 with the US Securities and Exchange Commission for a proposed Initial Public Offering, listing Class A common stock on the **Nasdaq Global Select Market** under the ticker **`ANT`**. **This is the first frontier-lab S-1 ever filed**, beating OpenAI's expected Q4/Q1 filing to market.

**Reported terms (per secondary coverage — the registration statement is not yet effective, so share count + price range are still blank):**
- **Lead banks:** **Goldman Sachs + Morgan Stanley** head the underwriting syndicate; secondary reporting puts the full syndicate at roughly 20 banks.
- **Governance:** **Class F super-voting share** held by co-founders carries **50.1% of total votes**, which makes Anthropic a **controlled company** under Nasdaq Rule 5615(c)(1) — exempts it from independent-majority-board and committee-independence requirements. (Lift from Alphabet/Facebook structure.)
- **Financials (reported, not independently verified):** **$16.3B revenue in the six months ending June 30, 2026** (vs. $1.2B same period 2025, ~13× YoY). **$80.7B reported net loss, of which ~$72B is non-cash accounting on convertible notes** (i.e. non-operating). First-half 2026 operating-cash position described as strongly positive in the Shacknews coverage, but the specific operating-income figure needs the filing to verify.
- **IPO window:** Reports place the roadshow **as early as the week of Nov 9**, with pricing + first-trade a week after. **Expect S-1/A amendments between now and mid-November** (price range appears ~1 week pre-roadshow, final price night-before pricing).
