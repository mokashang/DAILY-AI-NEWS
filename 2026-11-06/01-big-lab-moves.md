## 1. Anthropic public S-1 filed Oct 26; roadshow expected week of Nov 9–11; Nasdaq ticker ANT; Q3 2026 revenue ~$17B; $895B+ future spend committed {#1-anthropic-ipo}

**What happened (2026-10-26, running through today):** Anthropic made its **S-1 public on Oct 26, 2026**, with a planned listing on the **Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT**. The roadshow is reported to open **the week of November 9–11**, with debut targeted **before Thanksgiving** (listing week of Nov 16, per some Bloomberg/Reuters reporting; dates subject to change).

**Financials in the filing:**
- **Q3 2026 revenue ~$17B** (vs $1.4B Q3 2025 — roughly **12× YoY**).
- **Committed future spending disclosed at $895B+** (predominantly compute, including the Colossus tenancy from the May edition and Anthropic-Google TPU deal from May 8).
- **Valuation discussions ranging $1.5T–$2T** per press reports; the S-1 itself does not disclose a target range. Last private round: **$965B Series H** in May 2026.

**Rule-based timing:** Under SEC rules, the roadshow can open **15 days after the public filing** (→ Nov 10 earliest), and **June 30 financials go stale after Nov 12** — so an **amended filing with Q3 numbers** is expected before the roadshow opens, which is consistent with the Nov 9–11 kickoff window.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens — Anthropic itself:** Until the IPO prices, **Solutions / FDE / Integration Engineer hiring at Anthropic will be deliberately conservative** (every new hire becomes a disclosure item until the lock-up ends). **Target the two weeks *after* first-day trading** (~Nov 23–Dec 5 if it prices on schedule) as the first big post-IPO req-opening window. Until then, concentrate the apply budget on the **8 Frontier Academy partner firms** from [2026-10-14/01 §1](../2026-10-14/01-big-lab-moves.md#1-frontier-academy) — those roles are **immune to the IPO blackout** and the partner-firm pipeline is the hedge.
- **Job lens — public-markets literacy:** In interviews for any Anthropic-track role from today through mid-2027, expect the question **"what does our S-1 tell you about our commercial focus?"** Prepare a 90-second, three-number answer: (a) revenue trajectory ($1.4B → $17B YoY → forward-implied 2027 rev); (b) the $895B compute commitment (forcing margin discipline); (c) the commercial-mix breakdown (API vs Claude.ai consumer vs Claude Code vs Enterprise). This is the "commercial-impact storytelling" gap flagged in [`05` §2](./05-career-and-startup.md#2-reprice-public) — closing it is the single highest-ROI interview-prep move of November.
- **Startup lens:** Three wedges open the day an S-1 goes public:
  - **(a) "S-1 explainer"-as-a-service.** The press reads S-1s selectively; an independent analyst service that publishes **red-lined Anthropic S-1 pages with plain-English annotations** hits $5–10K/mo subs from VC associates + policy analysts in the first 60 days. Low moat, immediate cash.
  - **(b) Secondary-market liquidity tooling for post-lockup employees.** ~6-month lockup window → Q2 2027 liquidity event for thousands of Anthropic employees. The **"first-time public-stock advisor + tax tool" wedge** is well-trod (EquityBee, Carta) but the **Anthropic-specific vertical** (equity grants + cliff schedule + the $895B compute-commitment risk disclosure) is new.
  - **(c) Compute-commitment-tracking SaaS.** The $895B disclosure invites a **"counterparty risk dashboard for enterprise AI buyers"** — if your vendor has pre-sold its next 5 years of compute, your API quota is a derivative of their delivery discipline. A paid dashboard that scores lab-compute-commitment vs lab-revenue-visibility sells to enterprise architects and CISOs.
- **Insight:** This IPO is the **first time a frontier-lab business model gets priced by the public market**. Three things will be visible in the pricing: (a) **margin profile of the Enterprise + API lines** (the only durable high-margin lines); (b) **concentration risk in Claude Code** (sector-defining product, but concentration cuts both ways); (c) **the commercial "cost" of the safety stance** (Pentagon break, usage policy). **The number to watch is the Day-1 market cap divided by trailing-twelve-month revenue** — a multiple above 100× means the public market is buying the AGI narrative; below 50× means it's buying the SaaS narrative. The gap between those is where your job-lens risk lives.

## 3. OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra shipped Oct 26 — screen-viewing + cross-app action; >3× faster than GPT-6 Astra / GPT-6.1 Sol {#3-gpt-61-terra}

**What happened (2026-10-26):** OpenAI released **GPT-6.1 Terra** as its new computer-use + cross-app-action model. Per OpenAI's newsroom and summarizing third-party reporting:

- **Can view the user's screen and take action across apps** (computer-use frontier, extending the GPT-5.5 Codex browser-agent lineage from May 8).
- **Claimed >3× faster** than GPT-6 Astra (the Sept 10 edition's frontier) and GPT-6.1 Sol on task-completion time.
- **Rollout:** ChatGPT Business / Enterprise / EDU first; API availability mentioned in secondary reporting; **no public pricing detail in these results** (likely to follow).

**Context — the computer-use tier is now a direct three-way race:**
- Anthropic: Claude Opus 5.5 + Haiku 5.5 effort levels (Oct 7)
- Google: Gemini 4 Argon (Oct 13; see §5)
- OpenAI: GPT-6.1 Terra (Oct 26)

**Sources:**
- [OpenAI Newsroom — Company Announcements](https://openai.com/news/company-announcements/) `[primary]`
- [Signal Drifts — This Week in AI Oct 26–Nov 1 2026](https://signaldrifts.substack.com/p/this-week-in-ai2) `[aggregator]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Computer-use is the **highest-signal demo you can put on a GitHub README right now**. Build a tiny app that *routes* across all three (Terra / Argon / Opus 5.5) and reports success-rate + latency per tool-call — publish the artifact, and your résumé now says "benchmarked three computer-use frontiers across identical traces." This is the kind of artifact that reads immediately in a 10-minute recruiter screen. Plan in [`03` §2](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#2-router-v3).
- **Startup lens:** Two wedges:
  - **(a) The "computer-use pre-commit test harness":** customers who want to deploy computer-use agents to employees need a **deterministic test suite** — Terra and Argon are non-deterministic across runs, and enterprises can't ship that without a verification loop. $100–300K ACV from mid-market enterprises.
  - **(b) Vertical computer-use for specific desktop apps** — SAP GUI, Epic, Bloomberg Terminal, ArcGIS, CAD tools. Each is a $5–50M ARR market. Owning the eval suite for one of these desktop worlds is a durable moat.
- **Insight:** The ">3× faster" claim is **the first head-to-head speed advertisement of the Terra tier** — meaning OpenAI thinks speed is the differentiator of this generation, not capability. That tracks with the Haiku-5.5 effort-level framing from Oct 14 (model-choice becomes a tuning knob). **The entire frontier is converging on: "same ceiling, differentiating on cost/speed/determinism."** The highest-value engineering skill for 2027 is therefore **cost-aware routing**, not model fluency — which validates [`03` §2's weekend plan](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#2-router-v3) and the [`ME.md` focusing decision](../ME.md).

Target roles: **"AI Solutions Architect — Oracle Fusion / ServiceNow Now Assist"** (consulting), **"Enterprise AI Engineer — Internal Platform"** (Uber-side). The two labs are **differentiating distribution now**, not model capability. **Which one your job search bets on depends on whether you prefer deep-skill-on-one-model or broad-integration-across-many** — and this is a real, now-live question for any applied-AI role.
