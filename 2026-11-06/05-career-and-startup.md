## 2. The public-markets reprice — how your interviews change in a roadshow window {#2-reprice-public}

**The shift:** From the moment Anthropic's S-1 goes public (Oct 26) to its first-day trading (~week of Nov 16 if on schedule), **every interviewer at Anthropic + Frontier Academy partner firms + enterprise AI teams is reading the same filings you are**. Three specific changes to interview prep:

**(a) Commercial-impact storytelling > technical polish.** Interviewers are **now rewarded internally** for hiring people who can explain how their work contributes to revenue lines on the S-1. Convert every project in your portfolio into a **"this line maps to this revenue column"** sentence. Example: "My router artifact reduces per-task cost ~40% on Haiku 5.5 effort levels, which aligns with the Enterprise line's margin discipline on the roadmap Anthropic highlighted in its risk factors around compute commitments."

**(b) Expect direct questions about disclosure items.** The **Pentagon-Anthropic break** ([2026-10-14/01 §2](../2026-10-14/01-big-lab-moves.md#2-pentagon-break)) and the **Nov 6 Reuters "backlash" story** ([`01` §2](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-ai-backlash)) are **both likely to be interview topics** at any Anthropic-track role for the next 60 days. **Prepare a one-minute answer each**: non-defensive, acknowledges the commercial impact, connects to a safety stance you can personally advocate for.

**(c) IPO-blackout realism.** Anthropic's Solutions / FDE / Research hiring will be **deliberately conservative through the roadshow**. Reqs won't necessarily *close* — they'll **slow walk** through comms-heavy approvals. **This is not personal**; it is how late-stage startups preparing to go public hire. **Set the expectation that Anthropic's first-answer time stretches 2–4× for the next 45 days.**

- **The hedge:** The **Frontier Academy partner firms are decoupled from Anthropic's hiring calendar**.
