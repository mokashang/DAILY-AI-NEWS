### Why it matters to you

- **Market-structure read:** $25B is the **2026 valuation anchor for a non-frontier-lab LLM company** — i.e., one tier below OpenAI / Anthropic / Google. If you're evaluating LLM-lab job offers, **$25B is the "middle-tier" comp-and-equity bar** against which Mistral, Cohere, AI21, and the Chinese labs (Zhipu, Moonshot) now set their packages. Treat any offer in that cohort as a comparable, not a one-off.

If your startup pitch is B2B LLM infrastructure (fine-tuning, deployment, eval, routing), the Canadian + ME sovereign-LP stack is a real funding tap.
- **Insight:** The Series-A → Series-F valuation ladder for LLM labs has resolved into a clear step function: Seed/A ≈ $1B · B ≈ $3–5B · C ≈ $8–12B · D ≈ $15–20B · E/F ≈ $25–30B · "frontier tier" ≈ $500B+. Cohere just printed the E/F node. The frontier gap (from $30B to $500B+) remains structural, not incremental.
