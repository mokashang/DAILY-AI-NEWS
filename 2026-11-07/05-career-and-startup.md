### How to use these numbers

**(1) Negotiation floor for a frontier-lab offer.** If you receive an FDE offer from Anthropic / OpenAI / Google DeepMind / Meta / Microsoft AI as a new grad or junior-mid, the **negotiation floor** is roughly: **$220K base + $200K–$400K equity over 4 years + sign-on**. Below that, the lab is testing you; above the median-senior number, you're negotiating against the wrong level.

**(2) Partner-firm offer comparables.** The Frontier Academy partner firms (Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Novo Nordisk) will offer **below-lab base but above-consulting-typical equity/variable** for Academy-eligible AI Integration Engineer roles. The partner-firm "effective total" tends to sit in the **$220K–$380K range** with better geographic flex. The decision isn't comp-only — it's **comp + which Academy cohort entry route** gets you to the Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer credential fastest.

**(3) Vertical-AI startup offer comparables.** The vertical FDE roles (Sierra / Decagon / Cognigy / Rogo / Harvey) offer **$180K–$280K base** with meaningful **pre-IPO equity**. Expected-value calculation depends on company stage; use the Infisical / Wabi / Sierra / Decagon valuation bands as discount anchors.

**(4) Insight.** With public comp anchors now numbered, **the expected-value of adding one visible portfolio artifact is quantifiable**: a shipped MCP server + eval + demo demonstrably moves you out of the "junior" pool (base $160K + equity thin) into the **"mid" pool** (base $220K+ + meaningful equity) on the lab offer ladders.
