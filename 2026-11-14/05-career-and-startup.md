## 1. Microsoft opens 60K-employee voluntary buyout; December 1 deadline {#1-microsoft-buyout}

**What happened (2026-10-26):** Microsoft offered **voluntary buyouts to ~60,000 employees** — roughly **27% of its global workforce**, including up to VP level — across US / UK / Canada / India.
- **Terms:** minimum 60 days base pay (8 weeks US), stock vesting continues, up to 39 weeks for some.
- **Deadline:** must accept by **first week of December**, exit Dec 31.
- Microsoft told reporters the industry baseline for similar programs is **3–5% uptake** — i.e., potentially **1,800–3,000 ex-MSFT engineers hitting the market in January**.
- Spokesperson says "no plans" for another buyout.

- **Job lens:** A supply bulge of mid-to-senior MSFT engineers hits the market **Jan 1**. New grads **benefit at the startup layer** (ex-MSFT people become hiring managers who refer juniors) and **lose at the FAANG new-grad layer** (backfill freezes). **Apply now — before Jan 1 — to companies that will be swamped later.**
- **Startup lens:** Expect a **Q1 2027 founder bump**. The cohort most likely to start companies: Azure / AI-platform ICs with **5–10 YOE**. Great time to be building complementary tooling they'll want to buy.
- **Insight:** This is the **third voluntary-separation program at MSFT in 2026** (after the ~8,700-person VRP earlier). The pattern — **pay-to-leave rather than RIF** — is now the preferred Big Tech playbook because it preserves ratings and avoids WARN Act optics.

## 2. Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE) is now the #1 highest-leverage new-grad AI role {#2-fde-boom}

**What happened:** As of late Oct 2026, **OpenAI has ~26 roles with "Forward Deployed" in the title**, vs. Anthropic's 6 and Palantir's 51. OpenAI also spun up the **OpenAI Deployment Company**, a majority-owned subsidiary seeded with **$4B from a 19-firm consortium** specifically to staff FDEs into enterprise customers.
- Indeed: FDE listings up **~729% YoY to April 2026**.
- Comp bands: **Glean Founding FDE $160K–$270K base**; **Distyl $150K–$250K + equity**; **frontier labs $300K–$550K total**.
- Skills listed have shifted **away from pure Python/SQL** toward customer discovery, problem decomposition, and AI product judgment.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The FDE role is **uniquely new-grad-accessible** because interviews weight **customer thinking + rapid prototyping** over LeetCode. If you've ever shipped an internal tool for a non-engineer, that's the whole pitch.
- **Startup lens:** FDE is the **fastest path from IC to founder** — you see 5–20 real enterprise problems a year. **~40% of 2025–26 vertical-AI YC founders** came out of FDE roles at Palantir/Scale/OpenAI.
- **Insight:** The "FDE" label is being broadened to mean **"AI PM who can code."** If you can't get an FDE title, aim for **"Solutions Engineer — AI"** or **"Founding Engineer — Deployments"** — same job, less competition.

### Action this week

Then this weekend fire **10 applications**: **Glean** (Founding FDE), **Distyl**, **Decagon**, **Sierra**, **Harvey**, **Ramp** (applied-AI), **Cognition**, **Mistral FDE US team**, **Cresta**, **Observe.AI**. ## 3. Layoff trackers cross 200K for 2026; AI-attribution now 36% (but inflated) {#3-layoffs-200k}

**What happened:** Multiple trackers now register 2026 tech layoffs **well past 200K**:
- **TrueUp:** 206,817 workers across 712 events (as of Nov 13).

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** The AI-attribution caveat is important: most 2026 cuts are **funding reallocation** (cut Team X to pay for GPUs), not **AI replacement**. Translation — the safe roles are ones that **spend the GPU budget** (infra, platform, inference, FDE), not ones that justify headcount on legacy revenue.
- **Startup lens:** This is the **richest ex-FAANG founder/early-engineer pool since 2023**. Pre-seed quality is up.
- **Insight:** **Don't filter companies by "has layoffs" — filter by "which team got cut."** A company that cut Sales & G&A but *grew* Research/Infra is a buy signal; a company that cut Research is a sell signal.

### Action this week

60 min on TrueUp cross-referencing your target company list: for each, **check what team got cut**. If Research/AI infra was spared, keep them in. If Research was cut, drop them. Then follow **@gergelyorosz** on X — he publishes team-level teardowns of each major layoff within 72 hours.
