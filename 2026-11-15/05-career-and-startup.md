## 3. Comp data landed — `AI Engineer` median $155.5K vs `ML/AI SWE` $250.9K; title choice = ~$95K delta {#3-comp-data}

**What Levels.fyi says (Nov 14 pull):**
- `AI Engineer` US median TC **$155.5K** (p25 $110K / p75 $213K).
- `ML / AI Software Engineer` US median TC **$250.9K**.
- `Machine Learning Engineer` median **$261–278K**.
- Google L3 new-grad ML/AI range **$177–199K** TC per third-party pulls (not direct Levels data — treat as `[analysis]`).

**Sources:**
- [Levels.fyi — AI Engineer](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/title/ai-engineer) `[primary]`
- [Levels.fyi — ML/AI SWE focus](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/focus/ml-ai) `[primary]`
- [AI Engineer Insights — salary](https://aiengineerinsights.com/blog/ai-engineer-salary/) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Title choice = ~$95K TC delta at median.** When negotiating, **ask for the `ML/AI Software Engineer` or `Machine Learning Engineer` req** over the generic `AI Engineer` title — **same work in practice, ~1.6× comp band**. Recruiters often have both reqs open; you have to ask.
- **H-1B / OPT lens:** Higher wage weighting on the H-1B lottery means **the ML/AI SWE title materially improves H-1B odds** over the AI Engineer title. If you're on F-1/OPT, this is a direct retention lever in your favor. (Details in §5.)
- **Insight:** The `AI Engineer` title emerged as the catch-all for "LLM-adjacent engineer at a non-ML-first company" and has been diluted. **The signal is now the work scope and the team name, not the title string** — read the JD, not the headline.
