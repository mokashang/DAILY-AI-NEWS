**What happened / what's about to happen (2026-11-17):**
- NVIDIA reports fiscal Q3 FY2027 after market close **Tuesday Nov 17, 2026** — one day before Anthropic prices and two days before ANT trades.
- **Revenue guidance: ~$108B ±2%** (up from $96.2B in Q2 FY27, +105.9% YoY) — street expects a beat-and-raise.
- **Vera Rubin production shipments began Aug 2026**; management previously guided **~20% of data-center revenue** from Rubin in Q3 FY27 — the first Rubin-shipping quarter.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **"Businesses in ChatGPT" + Wallet GA = the SMB platform layer is now a hiring surface.** Expect a wave of OpenAI reqs for **Platform / Growth / Billing SWEs** and **Commerce FDEs** (payments, fraud, 1P-merchant integrations). If you quote per-token COGS in a 2027 pitch deck, you're already behind.

## 5. Apple ships Siri AI Developer Preview — every dev gets access, App Store ship blocked until spring 2027 {#5-apple-siri-ai-dev-preview}

**What happened (2026-11-03, catching up from pre-window):**
- Apple opened **Siri AI to every developer** via the Developer Preview in **iOS 27.3 / iPadOS 27.3 / macOS 27.3 / visionOS 27.3** betas.
- **App Store apps cannot ship Siri AI features until spring 2027** — this is a build-and-prototype window, not a ship window.
- **Siri AI runs on foundation models co-developed with Google**; for complex queries, the stack taps **NVIDIA GPUs** (architecture revealed at WWDC 2026).
- Apple reportedly pays **~$1B/year to Google** for the foundation-model partnership.
- Apple's new head of AI: **Amar Subramanya** (replaces John Giannandrea).

**Sources:**

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** A **4-month build window before an entire app ecosystem gets Siri AI on the same day** is the most predictable "I shipped an AI feature on day one" interview story of 2027. Pick a small vertical app (fitness, finance, calendar), build a **Siri-AI-first interaction model in the Developer Preview**, demo it in a 60-second screen-capture. That's a line on every FDE/Mobile/Applied-AI résumé next spring.
- **Startup lens:** **The App Store AI gold rush has a fixed start gun (spring 2027).** If your product can be a Siri AI–first experience (voice-first, agent-on-device, multimodal query), **the lane to be in TestFlight by Feb 2027** so you launch the day App Store opens. For AI-infra and inference startups, **Apple is the single biggest potential 2028 customer** that isn't currently in the top-5 named accounts — worth tracking.
