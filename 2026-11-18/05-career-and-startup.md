## 4. Wrapper-startup margin shock — 30–50% gross-margin compression in 48 hours {#4-wrapper-margin-shock}

**The math (rough):**
- Pre-Nov 17, a typical AI coding wrapper priced ~$30/mo retail, cost ~$6/mo in Haiku 5.5 tokens → **80% gross margin**.
- Post-Nov 17: competitor can offer same product at ~$1.20/mo in MAI Code 2 Flash tokens (if Microsoft's claim holds) → **96% gross margin at same price**, or they can price at $15/mo and still make 92% GM.
- **Your competitive floor just moved from $30 to $15.** If you don't move, a well-funded competitor (or Microsoft itself, via Copilot upsells) takes your customers.
- Additionally: **Gemini 4 Hydrogen + Orchestrator** saves ~24% token spend on multi-step tasks → coding-wrapper margins compress another ~5–10% effective.
- **GPT-6.2 Astra 20% off** is a smaller signal (premium escalation tier) but still structural.

**Three concrete moves for wrapper founders:**
1. **Re-model GM at (MAI Code 2 Flash + Gemini Orchestrator) baseline** — if your product's GM drops below 40% at that baseline, you're in distressed territory.
2. **Build a switching-cost story**: evals you own + vertical data pipelines + "hands-off integration" workflows. Thin wrapper founders I'd talk to this week: Codeium/Windsurf, Cursor, Zed, Replit — none have articulated a MAI-counterfactual response yet.
3. **Shrink the product surface to one vertical** where generic MAI-on-Azure is a worse fit (regulated verticals, offline/edge, non-English non-US markets).

**For job-seekers:** this is also a *negative* signal — wrapper startups that don't adjust will cut eng headcount in Q1 2027. **Avoid applying to thin-margin wrappers that haven't articulated a MAI-defense story**. Better: FDE/Solutions roles at vertical-depth startups (Harvey, Sierra, Decagon, Rox, Glean).
