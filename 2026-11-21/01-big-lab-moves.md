## 2. NVIDIA — Jensen Huang resigned Nov 12 (missed by last edition); Q3 FY2027 revenue $115.8B under interim CEO Kress {#2-nvidia-huang-out}

**What happened (2026-11-12 → 2026-11-17):**
- **Nov 12:** NVIDIA filed an **8-K announcing Jensen Huang's resignation as CEO** and separation from the board. **CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO.** No permanent successor named.
- Reporting cites an alleged affair with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset. Stock fell **~10% pre-market**; largely recovered by Nov 14.
- **Nov 17 (under Kress):** Q3 FY2027 earnings — **revenue $115.8B (+103% YoY)**; Data Center **$107.6B (+110%)**; **Vera Rubin ~$20B in its first production quarter = fastest ramp in NVIDIA's history**. Non-GAAP gross margin 74.9%.
- **Q4 guide: $130B ±3%** (explicitly excludes any China data-center contribution).
- **FY28 growth raised to ~75%.** Vera Rubin NVL72 + Vera CPUs now **GA on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)**.

- [TechCrunch — Jensen Huang resigns, CFO Kress interim](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nvidias-jensen-huang-resigns-chipmaker-names-cfo-colette-kress-as-interim-ceo/) `[secondary]`

- [Futurum — Rubin fastest ramp ever](https://futurumgroup.com/?p=98250) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **A founder-driven firm's CEO exits in the middle of its fastest revenue ramp.** The next 6 months are a **governance test** — if Kress holds Q4 guide and $20B Rubin pace, she locks in the permanent role. Internally that pulls bench talent up (VP → GM → SVP) and **opens mid-level infra IC reqs behind them**. Target: NVIDIA's inference-stack / Triton / CUDA / NCCL / Dynamo teams, where the hiring bar visibly loosens during exec transitions. For MLE/SWE candidates outside NVIDIA, the signal is "Rubin topology is now the production comp for 2027" — **talk about HBM, NVL72, Vera-Rubin interconnect in interviews** or sound a year late.
- **Startup lens:** Two openings. (1) **Compute-arbitrage** startups benefit as Hopper → Blackwell → Rubin tiering leaves older silicon discounted (the pattern is clearest right now). (2) **Governance shock creates a rare procurement opening** — enterprise CTOs reassessing NVIDIA dependency will shortlist AMD MI400, Broadcom-Ethernet OpenAI silicon, and Groq/Cerebras for a ~6-month window. Any startup that ships a "write once, serve anywhere" inference layer across two of these three gets the meeting.
- **Insight:** **"Founder-CEO dependency" risk is the under-priced factor in frontier-AI comp.** Public ANT, Rubin-dependent NVIDIA, Microsoft-platform-dependent OpenAI — three different founder-concentration profiles, three different succession risks. Build your career resilience against the one most likely to see a Huang-style exit in the next 24 months.

## 3. Microsoft Ignite 2026 (Nov 17–20): Office folded into Copilot; agent discovery store launched {#3-microsoft-ignite}

**What happened (2026-11-17 → 2026-11-20):**
- **Nadella keynote Nov 17** opens by apologizing for the **Nov 10 M365 global outage** — then pitches Microsoft as "the new OS for work."
- **Office folded INTO Copilot:** Word, Excel, PowerPoint now live **inside** the Copilot super-app, alongside **Autopilot** (long-running tasks), **Code** (Codex-style IDE agent), **Home (Chat + Cowork)**. The app is positioned as a single unified surface rather than a layer over 15-year-old productivity software.
- Explicit head-to-head framing: **"Office-inside-Copilot vs. Anthropic's Nov 10 Whiteboards/Sheets/Data/Meetings."**
- **Agent Discovery Store** launched — enterprise admins can publish/approve agents for their tenant, with per-agent governance, logging, usage quotas, and audit trail.
- Copilot Studio adds **Autopilot primitives** for long-running async agents (24–72h horizon).
- Azure AI Foundry expands model routing across GPT-6.2 lineup, Mistral, Meta Muse, and Anthropic Fable 5.2 (via Databricks tie).

**Sources:**
- [TNW — Microsoft puts Office in Copilot, Nadella at Ignite AGI-pilled](https://thenextweb.com/news/microsoft-office-in-copilot-nadella-ignite-agi-pilled) `[secondary]`
- [GeekWire — Microsoft unveils all-in-one Copilot app taking on Anthropic](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-unveils-all-in-one-copilot-app-taking-on-anthropic-a) `[secondary]`
- [Constellation — Ignite 2026: Microsoft's case for the work OS](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/ignite-2026-microsoft-makes-case-be-your-work-operating-system-intelligence-layer) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Microsoft is hiring "agent PMs" and "tenant agent admins" at scale** — these are the Copilot-side equivalent of Anthropic FDE / OpenAI Solutions, with the same comp bands, less glamour, and a 10× larger existing customer surface. For SDE candidates: **Autopilot's 24–72h horizon exposes a new tier of integration roles** (long-lived state, cancellation semantics, idempotent tool calls) — these are the interview topics Microsoft's team recruiters are actively screening for right now. For MLE candidates: the Agent Store's governance stack ships tenant-isolated eval harnesses to production — if you can speak Braintrust/Langfuse semantics and show one eval you shipped, you skip the leetcode round.
- **Startup lens:** **Anthropic's Whiteboards/Sheets/Data + Microsoft's Office-in-Copilot kills a swath of "AI-native Notion/Airtable" pitches this week.** Where opportunity remains: (a) **vertical workflows the big two won't touch** (regulated, offline/edge, high-compliance), (b) **governance / audit / DLP layered between a tenant's data and either Copilot or Claude Data**, (c) **the Agent Discovery Store itself** — every enterprise now needs a "private registry + eval gate + rollout controller" for agents they publish internally. The Store creates a new ISV wedge that didn't exist two weeks ago.
- **Insight:** **Both Ignite and Fable 5.2 point at the same truth: productivity software is being re-shaped around agent horizons, not around document types.** The old "Office vs. Notion" frame is dead; the new frame is **"short-lived agents (<5s latency) vs. long-running agents (hours–days) vs. live-meeting agents."** Résumés, portfolios, and startup pitches get read against those three agent shapes now — pick one and specialize visibly.

**What happened (2026-11-04 → 2026-11-17):**
- **Nov 17 release notes:** **GPT-6.2 Astra** and **GPT-6.2 Sol** listed in the API (text + images), with ChatGPT Images 2.6 improvements. **Source single-tracked via OpenAI's own release page — treat as unconfirmed until independently verified.**

- **In-window research blog ("Research acceleration: the jagged frontier,"** companion to the Sept 6 "Research intern" post): the uplift from internal agentic use is **jagged** across domains; **heaviest-user divergence is widening**; **internal agentic-use (API-equivalent) spend per researcher now ~2× non-equity comp**; **largest absolute uplift is in Safety & Alignment**, not pretraining.
- **Still unresolved from prior editions:** OpenAI–Broadcom 10 GW Ethernet-based deployment start; "Businesses in ChatGPT" GA date; OpenAI $15B at $1.25T raise (CNBC Nov 12, unannounced by OpenAI).

**Sources:**
- [OpenAI — Release Notes](https://openai.com/products/release-notes/) `[primary]`
- [OpenAI — Research acceleration: the jagged frontier](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier/) `[primary]`
- [Releasebot — OpenAI tracker, Nov 2026](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai) `[aggregator]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **"~2× non-equity comp in API spend per researcher"** is the single most citable career signal of the week. **Translation: a mid-career OpenAI researcher runs ~$400–800K/yr of inference on themselves.** In an interview, show you can **operate at that budget shape** — know per-token economics, cache-hit ratios, async batching, reasoning-token accounting. This is the vocabulary that signals "FDE-ready" at Anthropic and OpenAI. Second: **Safety & Alignment as the largest absolute uplift area** means Anthropic Alignment Science + OpenAI Safety Research + DeepMind safety bench are hiring on uplift evidence, not just safety sentiment — the roles are more technical than the "safety" label implies.
- **Startup lens:** The jagged-frontier post is a **procurement checklist in disguise** — it tells you which research-adjacent tasks are solved (literature search, code gen, data pipeline scaffolding) and which are still bottlenecked (experiment design, novel-method selection, high-trust eval). Build products **at the bottlenecks**, not at the solved tasks. The latter is a crowded wrapper market; the former is where OpenAI itself admits it still spends human research time.
- **Insight:** **Treat the "GPT-6.2 Astra + Sol" lineup as a weather report, not a shopping list.** A split SKU family (named after stars) suggests OpenAI is moving to the Claude 4.5/5/5.2 cadence pattern — smaller, more frequent, named-variant releases. Expect the new routing-as-a-skill axis to tighten: your eval harness needs to test 3–5 models per task by default. If yours doesn't, you're one Fable 5.3 or GPT-6.3 away from an outdated stack.
