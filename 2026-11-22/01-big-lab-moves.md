- **Last private mark was ~$965B** (Series H, May 2026). IPO priced roughly **55% above** that last private round.
- Dual-class structure preserves founder control (same template used in prior filing).

## 2. NVIDIA FQ3'27: $115.8B revenue, Vera Rubin is the fastest ramp in company history, $390B backlog {#2-nvidia-fq3}

**What happened (2026-11-17):**
- **Revenue:** $115.8B (+20% QoQ, **+103% YoY**).
- **Data center:** $107.6B (+21% QoQ, +110% YoY).
- **EPS:** GAAP $2.70 / non-GAAP $2.68.
- **Q4 guide:** **$130B**, assuming **no Data Center compute revenue from China** (despite $2.4B of Hopper shipments to China in Q3).
- **Vera Rubin NVL72:** production ramped; shipping at **CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, Nebius**, with **SpaceX ("SpaceXSI")** coming online soon. NVIDIA calls it **"the fastest product ramp in the company's history."**
- **Supply commitments jumped to $390B** (from $279B in the prior quarter) as NVIDIA locked in multi-year memory procurement.
- Management: **supply remains a bottleneck through end of FY28**.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** $390B of forward supply means **NVIDIA and its downstream partners (CoreWeave, Nebius, Together, Baseten, Modal, Lambda) are hiring, not cutting**, through the end of FY28. The highest-leverage résumé line this week: **a public artifact showing you've tuned an inference kernel on Rubin or Blackwell**. For MLE candidates: **know the $/GPU-hour spread between H100, H200, B200, and Rubin NVL72** — it's the single most-asked whiteboard question at inference startups right now. **Skip CoreWeave/Nebius interviews without it; they'll screen you out in 5 minutes.**
- **Startup lens:** The **"no China Data Center compute revenue"** line in NVIDIA's guidance is the clearest signal yet that the **US compute and Chinese compute markets are now fully bifurcated**. If you're building a startup, pick one side of the line and go — straddling will cost you a seed round. Side effect: expect a **premium on multi-cloud GPU scheduling startups** (e.g., someone who can arbitrage across OCI + Azure + Nebius + Lambda in a single API will raise at 100× ARR).
- **Insight:** **The "Rubin generates $40B/GW" claim is the single most important number in AI infra right now** — if it holds, data-center ROI assumptions in the current compute-bubble debate flip from bearish (overbuilt) to bullish (undersupplied for another 2 years). Watch whether hyperscaler Q1 2027 capex guides *increase* vs. current street numbers; if yes, the compute cycle extends.

## 3. OpenAI's IPO-week cluster: GPT-6.2 Astra + Sol, NATO Daybreak, ChatGPT Wallet, MedChemBench {#3-openai-gpt-6-2}

**What happened (2026-11-13 → 2026-11-20):**
- **Nov 13 — ChatGPT Wallet (US Pro beta):** agents can now make purchases using **card-network tokens**. **Biometric approval required for every purchase** (no session-level auth).
- **Nov 17 — GPT-6.2 Astra + Sol:**
  - **Astra**: flagship, aimed at harder projects. API pricing (reported): **$8/M input, $40/M output** — **exact match to Fable 5.2 flagship pricing**.
  - **Sol**: everyday-work model (pricing not disclosed in secondary coverage as of 2026-11-22).
  - **Astra is the first model rated "High" on AI Self-Improvement** under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework — the policy threshold that triggers additional safeguards.
- **Nov 17 — OpenAI × NATO:** OpenAI said it is working with NATO's Communications and Information Agency to give NATO core institutions access to its cybersecurity models through the **Daybreak** program.
- **Nov 18 — ChatGPT Accounting plugin:** prepares invoice entries, reconciles records, drafts month-end reports.
- **Nov 18 — AMD GPU circuit optimization research:** OpenAI said its models helped improve AMD GPU circuit designs with **improvements up to 14% against heavily optimized baselines**.
- **Nov 20 — MedChemBench:** OpenAI's benchmark for medicinal chemistry tasks (introduced alongside ongoing academic research program; next application round opens Nov 23).
- **Nov 20 — ChatGPT Space:** global rollout to Plus users aged 18+.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **"High" on AI Self-Improvement** is a policy-first event, not a product-first one — it means OpenAI's **Preparedness, Safety Systems, and red-team orgs just got budget**. If you've worked on evals, scaling-laws-for-safety, or RLHF with structured reward signals, **apply to Preparedness this month**. Separately: the NATO deal opens an entirely new **defense-AI FDE lane** — security-cleared engineers with LLM-eval experience are now **a top-5 highest-TC candidate profile** in all of AI hiring.
- **Startup lens:** **ChatGPT Wallet + Accounting plugin + Space** is a clear "**OpenAI is becoming a consumer SaaS company**" signal — vertical app wrappers on top of general ChatGPT just got structurally harder. Your wedge: either **sell to enterprises OpenAI can't onboard easily** (regulated verticals, government, large legacy IT) or **build the agent-payment layer that Wallet *won't* cover** (B2B invoicing, multi-party escrow, international). **Avoid: consumer finance, consumer productivity, "AI accounting."**
- **Insight:** **The $8/$40 flagship pricing floor is now the market.** Fable 5.2, GPT-6.2 Astra, and (by implication) Gemini 4's flagship tier have converged at this exact price. **Model pricing is a commodity; routing is the only remaining margin.** The next 12 months of AI infra will be dominated by **router startups** (Not Diamond, Martian, OpenRouter) and the **eval+observability layer** (Weights & Biases, Langfuse, Braintrust) — those are the places still with pricing power.

## 4. Google ships Gemini 4 Neon — fourth Gemini 4 model in under two months {#4-gemini-4-neon}

**What happened (2026-11-18):**
- **Gemini 4 Neon** released, slotting between **Gemini 4 Carbon** (deeper reasoning, Nov 10) and **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** (faster execution, Nov 12).
- Rolled out through Google's **API, AI Studio, and enterprise products** (Vertex AI + Workspace enterprise tier).
- Positioning: "balanced tier" — reasoning close to Carbon at latency closer to Hydrogen.
- Baseline cadence context: **Gemini 4 Argon** launched **Sept 30** (initial GA Oct 13 via API, Ultra, Pro). So Google has now shipped Argon → Carbon → Hydrogen → Neon in **~7 weeks**.
- Pichai has publicly committed to a **near-monthly Gemini cadence** — Neon confirms the pattern is operational, not aspirational.

**Sources:**

- [TechCrunch — Google releases Gemini 4 Argon](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/google-releases-gemini-4-argon-called-its-most-powerful-model-yet/) `[secondary]`

- [Tech-Insider — Gemini 4 Nears Launch as Google Trails by 17 Points](https://tech-insider.org/gemini-4-google-launch-deepmind-post-training-2026/) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Google DeepMind + Google AI Studio are the **highest-cadence shipping org in frontier AI right now** — four flagship models in 7 weeks is an **engineering-ops accomplishment, not a research one**. If you're an infra/platform engineer who wants to see **frontier-model shipping cadence up close**, apply to Gemini API / Model Garden / Vertex AI teams — the work is less glamorous than DeepMind research but the shipping volume beats every competitor.
- **Startup lens:** **The monthly-model-release cadence kills any startup pitch that includes "we're waiting for the next model generation to launch."** If your product narrative is version-locked to a specific capability frontier, you'll be re-pitching your deck every 4 weeks. Build **model-agnostic** or **task-specific-with-router** from day one.
- **Insight:** Four models in 7 weeks implies **Google's post-training pipeline is now semi-automated** — they've turned base-model fine-tuning into a factory. **The new competitive moat at the labs is infra, not research**: whoever ships the most training/eval infra wins. Watch for a **Gemini 5 tease at AWS re:Invent (Dec 2)** or at Google Cloud Next (early 2027).
