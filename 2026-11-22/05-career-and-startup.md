**If you want to be employee #1–20 at an ex-Anthropic startup:**

## 6. The $8/$40 flagship price floor compresses the AI consulting business model {#6-consulting-compress}

**What's happening:** With Claude Fable 5.2, GPT-6.2 Astra, and (soon) Gemini 4 flagship all at **~$8/$40 per M tokens**, the cost base for AI-driven consulting work is now **deterministic and low**.
- A full-day agent run at 10M tokens in / 2M tokens out costs **$160 per workday** at flagship pricing.
- An FDE at $225K TC with 50% utilization delivers ~1,000 billable hours = **$225/hr effective cost**.
- **The agent costs less than the engineer's margin per hour, not per day.**

### Implications for consulting-style AI work

- **Big-4 (Deloitte, PwC, EY, Accenture) AI practices:** compressed margins. If a GenAI "transformation" project was priced at **$500K for a 4-month engagement** in 2024, the same scope at 2026 model costs is defensible at **$200K**, with 60% margin maintained. Billable rates will drop or scopes will expand.
- **FDE roles at labs:** **the value prop is not "we're cheap" — it's "we can ship in 2 weeks what Big-4 ships in 6 months."** Speed + customer intimacy + defensible eval loops.
- **Independent AI consulting:** **narrow vertical + published eval + repeatable playbook.** Rate justification lives in demonstrated results + brand.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Avoid generic "AI transformation consultant" roles at Big-4** unless the comp is **>$250K base** and the practice has a clear industry wedge. Better: FDE at a product company, where the moat is product, not billable hours.
- **Startup lens:** **Productized AI consulting (fixed scope, fixed outcome)** is a defensible startup wedge — see **Distyl, Decagon, Harvey**. Avoid T&M billing; it compresses to zero.
- **Insight:** **The next generation of AI services companies will look like product companies** — productized scopes, outcome-based pricing, high gross margin. The T&M AI consulting era is ending.
