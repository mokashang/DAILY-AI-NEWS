**What changed with the IPO:**
1. **RSU dollar values are now legible.** When Anthropic says "$500K TC with $200K in RSUs", you can price the RSU in public-market dollars from day one. Pre-IPO, that number was a guess.
2. **Grant *shares* are the negotiation variable.** Ask for the grant in shares; the dollar value is pinned to today's $89.77 price. If ANT trades to $150 in Q1 2027 (plausible), your grant is worth ~$150/89.77 = 1.67× the opening promise.
3. **Vesting cliffs matter more.** 1-year cliff + 4-year vest is standard. Negotiate: (a) **double-trigger acceleration** on change-of-control, (b) **refresh grant schedule** (annual vs biennial — annual is better for you), (c) **front-loaded vest** (first-year 25% cliff, not 10%).
4. **Expect comp band crystallization in Feb 2027** when Anthropic reports first public Q. Any offer you accept before then is pre-market-reference — negotiate the review.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **This is the first week you can negotiate ANT RSUs as real money.** Use the framework above. If you have any Anthropic interviews on the schedule between now and Feb 2027, prepare for the comp conversation specifically — ask the recruiter for grant share count, vesting schedule, and double-trigger language in writing.
- **Startup lens:** Public-company RSU math is now a competitive wedge for early-stage startups. If you're hiring, be prepared to counter ANT grants with **(a) founder-grade option grants (0.5–2.0% at seed), (b) acceleration on acquisition, (c) a credible story about faster liquidity**. This is harder in 2026 than it was in 2023.
- **Insight:** The **ceiling comp ($1.2M)** is an outlier — Staff/Principal FDE at Anthropic with a specific vertical track record. Don't anchor on it. The modal new-hire FDE is in the **$350K–$500K TC** band; the top new grad is in the **$280K–$350K TC** band. That's your realistic floor target.
