## 3. Public ANT comp disclosures → the first fully legible frontier-lab offer letter

**What changed Nov 18.** With ANT trading, **every Anthropic job offer** that includes RSUs is now readable in **real-time dollars**. This replaces the prior pattern of "1,200 options at a strike derived from a tender offer six months ago."

**How to use it in your negotiation.**
1. **Ask for grant in ANT dollar value, not share count.** "The RSU grant is worth $X at today's closing price" is a cleanly negotiable number.
2. **Model the vesting cliff.** Four-year vest with a one-year cliff means **~25% of your grant becomes tradeable ~May 2028** (one year post-start after the ~May 2027 insider lockup). Build that into your plan.
3. **Ask about refresh grants.** Public-company refresh cadences (annual, Mar/Sep) will apply at Anthropic within 12 months of IPO. Ask if the recruiter can confirm the pattern.
4. **Compare to OpenAI offer PPUs** (which still trade on private secondaries at a discount to Anthropic's public comp). Use this as leverage — "your public comp beats my OpenAI private comp by $X, but your lockup is longer, so let's split the difference on sign-on."

**Why it matters.**
- **Job.** This is the biggest structural improvement to AI-lab compensation disclosure in two years. **Use it.**
- **Startup.** If you're pre-seed and hiring, your competitive offer letter now needs to *beat* a public-market comp line. Seed-stage founders are going to **lose offers to Anthropic specifically** on the legibility dimension. Adjust: lead with **mission**, **ownership %**, and **learning velocity**, not "huge upside."
- **Insight.** **Transparency is a recruiting advantage, not a disadvantage.** Candidates have always preferred legible offers. Anthropic just got the legibility; OpenAI gets it in Sept 2027.
