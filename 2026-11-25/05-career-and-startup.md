## 1. FDE is still the single highest-leverage new-grad AI role — but the bar for "senior + client-facing" is hardening {#1-fde-new-grad-window}

**The data:**
- **FDE-title postings: ~300 (2022–23) → ~5,000+ (early 2026)** — roughly **17× growth** in four years.
- **SignalFire State of Talent 2026:** Forward-Deployed Engineers up **+30%** YoY.
- **New-grad hiring at the "12 Tech Majors" is down ~65% vs. 2019.**
- **Pragmatic Engineer, Nov 2026:** *"as product companies hire fewer new grads, new grads will increasingly find FDE roles they have a realistic shot at getting."*
- **LinkUp counter-note:** *"Forward-deployed roles require senior engineering skill plus client fluency."*

**What the data means together:**
- The headline FDE boom is real, but **the title has split into two tiers**: Tier 1 ("Founding FDE" / "Senior FDE" at labs and lab-adjacent startups — $160K–$550K TC, 3+ YoE required) and Tier 2 ("Deployed Engineer" / "Customer Engineer" / "Deployment Architect" at mid-stage vertical startups — **$120K–$200K TC, open to strong new grads**).
- **Tier 2 is your lane.**

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** Your target title range (ranked):
  1. **Deployment Engineer / Customer Engineer / Deployed Engineer** at vertical agent startups ($120–$200K). **This is your lane.**
  2. **Solutions Engineer** at infra startups (fal / Modal / Baseten / Together). Higher technical bar, less client-facing.
  3. **Forward Deployed Engineer** at frontier labs (Anthropic / OpenAI / Palantir) — stretch applications; apply anyway because process teaches you.
- **Startup lens:** If you're building, **hire your first two FDEs ahead of Series A**, not after. Pragmatic Engineer's piece is being read inside every founder Slack this month — the competition for the Tier-2 pool is 90 days away from doubling.
- **Insight:** **The gap between "FDE boom" and "new-grad crunch" isn't a contradiction — it's an opportunity.** Tier 1 stays senior; Tier 2 opens to new grads *because* product companies won't hire new grads elsewhere. Your résumé should **position for Tier 2 specifically**.

### Résumé top-section rewrite (do this today, pre-Thanksgiving)

Replace your opening 3 lines with this template:

```

GitHub: <handle> · MCP servers published, 5-case evals, demo clips
```

Three lines. No objective statement. No "passionate about AI." Each line names a repo URL, a company, or a measured number.

## 3. ANT lockup schedule → the 2027 Anthropic-engineer attrition timeline {#3-ant-lockup-timing}

**The pattern.**
- Public-lab IPO day → **employees can't sell for the duration of the lockup**.
- When the first lockup tranche releases → **a subset of employees take liquidity + leave to found / join startups / take competing frontier offers**. (See Meta 2012, Snap 2017, Coinbase 2021 pattern.)
- Anthropic chose a **staggered multi-tranche lockup** vs. SpaceX-style single cliff. **This smooths the attrition** — expect waves, not a single exit.

**Mapping to your calendar:**

| Date | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Nov 19 – Dec 31, 2026** | ANT quiet period | Apply now (open reqs stay open). Don't expect flashy external hiring posts. |
| **Jan 2027** | Q4 2026 earnings (first as public co.) | **Resume optimization window** — read the 10-K for hiring-signal language. |
| **~May–Jul 2027** | **Likely first lockup tranche release** (per S-1 — verify) | **Peak application timing**. Backfill reqs open, tenured engineers start leaving. |
| **~H2 2027** | "Ex-Anthropic researcher" seed announcements begin | **Your network play**: cold-email every "ex-Anthropic researcher" seed co. that announces this half. |

**Sources:**
- [KuCoin — Anthropic extended lockup + shareholder sales structure](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/anthropic-considers-extended-lockup-and-shareholder-sales-for-60b-ipo) `[secondary]`
- [Webull — SpaceX lockup expiry as reference pattern](https://www.webull.com/news/15581005814711296) `[analysis]`
- [The Coin Republic — Anthropic lockups could trigger AI selloff](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2026/11/13/anthropic-ipo-lockups-could-trigger-ai-selloff-after-1-4t-debut/) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Don't try to force an Anthropic hire this month if the first application doesn't land.** The real door opens ~May–Jul 2027 when attrition picks up. In the meantime, **collect the ten warmest relationships you can at Anthropic now** — current engineers whose paper you've read, whose Claude Code PR you've commented on, whose podcast you've quoted. When they consider leaving in May, they'll remember your name.
- **Startup lens:** **The "ex-Anthropic researcher seed" wave of H2 2027 is the single most predictable founder-pool to watch** since the ex-OpenAI 2023 wave. If you're incubating anything AI-adjacent, prep an **"introducing my co-founder" cold-outreach template for anyone who left ANT after ~May 2027**.
- **Insight:** **IPOs don't shift hiring; lockup expiries do.** Mark May 2027 on your calendar. Everything else is prelude.

## 5. Startup thesis update: what the ANT selloff killed, and what it opened {#5-startup-thesis}

**Killed this week:**
- **Pure horizontal "AI chatbot for X" raises at Series A+.** None appeared in the funding week. This category is officially dead.
- **Pitches that require AI chip prices to crash before 2027H2.** NVIDIA's $130B Q4 guide puts that thesis on life-support.
- **Vision-deck raises at $100M+ pre-revenue.** Multiples compression means boards want logos + unit economics.

**Opened this week:**
- **Agentic workload caching products** (per ARCs paper, [`04` §1](./04-research-progress.md#1-arcs)) — if you can decouple reasoning quality from GPU spend for bounded workloads, you have a startup category.
- **Vertical compliance + regulated-industry agents** (Model ML is the proof case this week; insurance ops, treasury, supply chain compliance all have room for one more entrant each).
- **MCP-first infra products** (post-release-cadence stabilization, [`03` §2](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#2-mcp-release-cycle)). Any MCP adjacent product — gateway, catalog, observability — has a 6–12 month window before incumbents bake it in.

**Pricing discipline given the ANT-triggered selloff** (see [`02` §4](./02-new-emerging.md#4-ai-trade-selloff)):
- Cut your ask by 25% from September comps.
- Prefer lower-valuation / clean-prefs over higher-valuation / aggressive-prefs.
- Show 3+ paying logos + ≥70% gross margin in the first slide. Everything else is explanation.

### Why it matters to you

- **Insight:** **The two-year AI market is reshaping around unit economics.** Whichever side of the table you're on (founder or applicant), **"I can ship a measurable outcome at defensible cost"** is now the *only* story that lands. Build that story this week.
