## 3. The frontier is now a commodity at $8/$40 — reposition your skills {#3-commodity-price}

**What happened:**
- Claude Fable 5.2, GPT-6.2 Astra, and Gemini 4 Carbon (post-Nov 30 promo) all land at **$8/M input, $40/M output** — the frontier-tier price point.
- Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6.2 Sol ($2/$10), and Gemini 4 Hydrogen cluster at a similar daily-driver price point.
- **Three labs converged on identical commodity pricing within six weeks.**

**What this means for your résumé:**

**Reprice UP:**
- **Router design** (cost/quality tradeoff explicit)
- **Eval harness design** (what you evaluate on, why, and how you detect regressions)
- **MCP server authoring** (10× resume value vs. "I used GPT")
- **Vertical data pipelines** (your data is the moat, not your model)
- **Forward-deployed integration patterns** (customer success + eng)
- **Open-weight serving** (vLLM / SGLang / TGI) — see [`03` §5](./03-practical-skills-and-tools.md#5-open-weight-serving)
- **Agent-approval compliance** (Claude Ship + eval gate + audit trail)

**Reprice DOWN:**
- "Latest-model fluency" ("I used GPT-6.2 Astra on day one") — zero signal
- Generic prompt-engineering certs
- "We fine-tuned a Llama" without a serving story
- Pure-wrapper startup pitches (3× API cost margin is dead)
- Thin RAG pipelines without eval

**Your rewrite exercise:** take your current résumé, grep every mention of a model name, and replace each with the *system property* you actually built (routing, eval, data, deployment). **That's the FDE résumé for Dec 2026.**

**Sources:**
- [OpenAI — GPT-6.2 Astra & Sol pricing](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol) `[primary]`
- [Decrypt — Astra pricing challenges Anthropic](https://decrypt.co/383488/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-challenges-anthropic-price-but-not-every-benchmark) `[secondary]`
- [Let's Data Science — Gemini 4 Carbon public pricing](https://letsdatascience.com/news/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-carbon-to-general-public-9b41bcb4) `[secondary]`

### Why it matters to you
- **Job lens:** Your competition's résumés all mention model names. Yours should mention **system properties**. That's the differentiator this hiring cycle.
- **Startup lens:** If your product's unique value prop is "we use the latest model," you don't have a value prop. Redesign around a data moat, a workflow moat, or a compliance moat before the next round.
- **Insight:** **Commodity pricing at $8/$40 is a stable equilibrium** — none of the three labs can cut without triggering a response, and all three have the margin headroom to hold. **Price stability frees product managers to compete on surface, not cost.** Expect faster feature shipping from all three labs in Q1 2027.
