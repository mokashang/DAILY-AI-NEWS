# New & Emerging — 2026-11-27

Funding compressed into fewer, bigger rounds this fortnight — the aftershock of Anthropic's IPO sucking attention and capital toward the frontier. **BrightAI** was the only clean, well-sourced large round in the window. The more interesting signal is in the *patterns*: physical AI maturing into a backable category with a strategic partner (GE Vernova), a late-stage overhang on pure "AI wrapper" plays, and the first **cross-cloud distribution** deal (OpenAI on GCP) reshaping how new companies choose where to run.

## 1. BrightAI — $97M Series B at $750M for physical AI infrastructure (Nov 18, 2026) {#1-brightai}

**What happened:**
- **Nov 18, 2026:** BrightAI announced a **$97M round at ~$750M valuation**, bringing total raised to **~$184M**.
- Lead: **Smith Point Capital**. Strategic: **GE Vernova** (power / grid infrastructure vertical lock-in).
- Returning: Khosla Ventures, Inspired Capital, Upfront Ventures.
- Platform: **physical AI** for essential infrastructure — sensors, drones, robots, and field software for **energy, water, waste, and heavy industry**. International expansion: Europe + Asia.
- Context: $51M Series A in July 2025 (Khosla + Inspired); 16 months to the B — a brisk cadence for a hardware-software hybrid.

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **BrightAI needs the same mix of talent as GridCARE / Crusoe / Sphere AI** — field-deployable engineers who can ship firmware + model inference + data pipelines in a utility customer's basement. If your strength is "I can embed with a customer and ship end-to-end," this category is the **fast-track FDE/Solutions path** without the frontier-lab brand gatekeeping. Comp is typically lower base but higher impact/equity upside.
- **Startup lens:** **The physical-AI stack now has a seed → B pattern you can benchmark.** $51M A in 2025 → $97M B at $750M in 2026 ≈ 3× markup across 16 months. The GE Vernova investment is the real tell — **a utility-industrial strategic will underwrite physical AI the way Microsoft underwrote OpenAI**. If you're founding here, align with a vertical strategic before chasing generalist VCs.
- **Insight:** **"Physical AI" is no longer science; it is a procurement category.** Three categories graduated from "promising" to "backable" in 2026: (1) agentic sales/CX (Rox, Sierra, Decagon, Nooks); (2) code agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Zed); (3) physical AI (BrightAI, Figure, 1X, Praxis-stack). Each has a dominant buyer type (CRO, VP Eng, VP Operations). Pick one buyer *persona* and you can specialize an entire career lane.

## 2. Funding snapshot — Nov 14–27 window {#2-funding-snapshot}

**What happened:**
- **Weekly totals are messy; trackers disagree.** zplatform.ai says $24.6B across 119 verified rounds Sep 1 → Nov 16; another tracker says $15.5B+ across 62 deals in November with data refreshed Nov 12; a weekly tally reports 40 USD-denominated rounds totaling **$6.0B for Nov 9–13**.
- **Nov 18 single-day:** 7 funding rounds above $2M in 24 hours, **~$179.5M total across 5 regions**; BrightAI's $97M was the largest.
- **Thinking Machines Lab $5B Seed on Nov 6** (per one tracker — this is directionally plausible given Mira Murati's team but the "Seed" label here is doing a lot of work; verify via SEC Form D before quoting a number).

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Fewer, bigger rounds** means the ~$10–50M post-A startup cohort is squeezed; hiring there is slower and offers more variable. The best hiring velocity is at (a) $100M+ rounds (BrightAI, Thinking Machines, OpenAI) and (b) seeds that just closed on senior ex-FAANG/ex-lab founders. **Skip the awkward middle.**
- **Startup lens:** **If you are raising a Series A or B right now, the market is still open but requires a visible strategic logic** (BrightAI + GE Vernova; Nooks + GC's CVF; Rox + Databricks). Add at least one "obvious buyer" or "obvious distribution partner" to your round narrative.
- **Insight:** **The concentration pattern continues.** When ~2.5% of deals take ~65% of dollars, the first-order return strategy is **go to the top of your category or stay private, bootstrap, and milk FCF**. There is no middle anymore. Decide which game you're playing before you write a deck.

## 3. Thinking Machines Lab reportedly closes $5B "Seed" (Nov 6) {#3-thinking-machines}

**What happened:**
- Per one tracker, **Thinking Machines Lab closed a $5B Seed round on Nov 6, 2026**.
- Context: this is Mira Murati's lab founded post-OpenAI exit; prior reporting had Thinking Machines marked at ~$50B in a mid-year secondary, later re-marked to ~$40B per 2026-11-14 edition.
- The "Seed" label at $5B is unusual; it likely reflects a round structure (convertible, SAFE, or structured preferred with a Seed header for insider tax purposes) more than a traditional dilution event.
- Unconfirmed by the company. Treat as directional until an SEC Form D or company announcement surfaces.

**Sources:**
- [aifunding.me — November 2026 AI Funding Roundup](https://aifunding.me/roundups/november-2026) `[aggregator]`
- [Interconnects (Nathan Lambert, running coverage)](https://www.interconnects.ai/) `[analysis]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **Thinking Machines hiring at a $40–50B mark will anchor researcher comp for the next 6 months** — if even the "Seed" headline sticks. Expect generalist-researcher offers to re-anchor against the Thinking Machines package.
- **Startup lens:** **Structured rounds labeled "Seed" are the new secondaries.** Watch how this pattern spreads — it lets insiders mark up without triggering 409A / RSU re-strike mechanics at the lab.
- **Insight:** **Mira Murati's team has not shipped a public model yet. The $5B marks a *team* bet, not a *product* bet.** That is only possible in a market where a handful of founders are credible enough to raise against pure resume. Three names fit that bill in 2026: Murati, Sutskever's SSI, and whoever leaves Anthropic post-lockup (May 2027).

## 4. OpenAI × $15B at $1.25T (reported Nov 12) {#4-openai-15b}

**What happened:**
- **CNBC Nov 12:** OpenAI is reportedly raising **~$15B at a $1.25T valuation**.
- OpenAI has not announced the round; no lead investor named.
- Context: OpenAI's Oct 21 move onto Google Cloud (see [`01` §2](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-openai-gcp)) + its ongoing Microsoft partnership reshape the strategic-investor pool — likely to include sovereign funds and non-US strategics given Microsoft's restricted ability to anchor further.

**Sources:**
- [aifunding.me — November 2026 AI Funding Roundup](https://aifunding.me/roundups/november-2026) `[aggregator]`
- [TechCrunch AI — category](https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/) `[secondary]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** OpenAI at $1.25T primes RSU grants to be denominated in *tender-offer* increments (annual or biannual secondaries at a known mark). Ask recruiters specifically about **the tender cadence** — not about the fictional "IPO soon."
- **Startup lens:** At $1.25T × ~$30B TTM revenue, OpenAI is trading at ~40× revenue in private markets, vs. **Anthropic trading at ~4–5× revenue in public markets**. That gap will close — either OpenAI prints more revenue, or ANT's multiple expands as the market normalizes, or OpenAI's next primary clears below $1.25T. Watch which.
- **Insight:** **The public vs. private frontier-lab price divergence is temporarily enormous.** It will resolve. For employees of either lab, this is the "accept and wait" window: public labs for liquidity, private labs for upside.

## 6. OpenAI-on-GCP as a founder tell {#6-openai-gcp-founder-tell}

**What happened:**
- With OpenAI live in Google Cloud Model Garden (Oct 21 announcement, private preview scaling now — see [`01` §2](./01-big-lab-moves.md#2-openai-gcp)) and Anthropic already on AWS Bedrock + GCP Vertex, **every frontier lab now serves every major cloud**.
- The practical effect: the **cloud is the distribution channel**, not the lab.

**Sources:**
- [OpenAI — OpenAI models are coming to Google Cloud](https://openai.com/index/openai-models-google-cloud/) `[primary]`

### Why it matters to you

- **Job lens:** **A GCP / AWS / Azure Marketplace listing is now a credible startup distribution channel.** If you're applying to AI startups, ask the question: "Are we listed in Marketplace?" If not, the sales motion has a ceiling. If yes, there's a published co-sell arrangement.
- **Startup lens:** **List in the Marketplace on day one.** The 2016-equivalent of "ship on the Apple App Store" is "ship on the Google Cloud Marketplace + AWS Marketplace + Azure Marketplace." Private beta GCP OpenAI access is a tell that enterprise AI procurement is centralizing at the hyperscaler, not the lab.
- **Insight:** **The lab doesn't own the customer. The cloud does.** This is the quiet repricing of the 2026 AI stack. Multi-model routing = engineering table stakes. Cloud-native distribution = commercial table stakes.
