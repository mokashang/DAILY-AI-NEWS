- The injunction is unlikely to be granted. But the **cost of defense alone** will push labs to:
  1. Hire 2–5 **safety-governance engineers per lab** who can write release-committee docs and capability-disclosure reports.
  2. Hire **1–3 in-house litigators / policy engineers per lab** who can respond to Rule 26 discovery on model evals.
  3. Slightly **slow the pace of flagship-model releases** for the first 1–2 quarters of 2027 as the lawsuit docket moves.

### What this does to your offer

- **Equity grants for flagship-model-adjacent roles:** no structural change. Research continues.
- **Equity grants at policy / safety / T&S roles:** marginally more valuable because the function just acquired a judicial mandate.
- **Job availability:** a new sub-market of ~40 roles across the 5 defendants is forming — safety-governance eng, release-committee-ops, capability-disclosure, model-card forensics. Watch Anthropic Trust & Safety, OpenAI Preparedness, Google RSP-engineering, Meta FAIR responsible-release listings.

### Insight

- **Safety and Trust & Safety roles are the most undervalued hire-side opportunity of Q1 2027.** Before the lawsuit, they were seen as "non-ship" roles with slower career growth. Post-lawsuit, they're on the critical path of every release. **The comp will catch up in 6–9 months.** If your interest is in safety/policy, this is the moment to get in.
