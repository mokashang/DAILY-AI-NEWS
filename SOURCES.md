# Master AI News Sources

Curated by reliability and signal quality. Check primary sources before trusting secondhand reports.

---

## Tier 1: Primary Sources (Ground Truth)

These are first-party — no middleman, no spin. Always check these before reading coverage of a story.

### Official Lab Blogs & Release Notes
- [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/) — model releases, policy, research
- [Anthropic News](https://www.anthropic.com/news) — Claude updates, safety research, policy
- [Anthropic Red Team Blog](https://red.anthropic.com/) — security and capability research (e.g., Mythos)
- [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/) — Gemini, AlphaFold, robotics
- [Google AI Blog](https://ai.googleblog.com) — applied research and products
- [Meta AI Blog](https://ai.meta.com/blog/) — open models, Muse Spark, Llama
- [Microsoft AI Blog](https://blogs.microsoft.com/ai/) — Copilot, Azure AI, enterprise
- [xAI Blog](https://x.ai/blog) — Grok updates
- [Mistral Blog](https://mistral.ai/news/) — open and enterprise models
- [Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog) — open-source ecosystem, papers, tools

### Financial Filings & Revenue Data
- [Sacra — OpenAI](https://sacra.com/c/openai/) — revenue estimates, valuation, financials
- [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/) — best sourced tech financial reporting (paywall)

## Tier 2: Research Preprints (Frontier First)

Where breakthroughs land before anywhere else — often before peer review.

- [arXiv cs.AI (daily)](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current) — all AI papers, same day
- [arXiv cs.LG (daily)](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/recent) — ML theory and methods
- [arXiv cs.CL (daily)](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/recent) — NLP, LLMs, transformers
- [arXiv cs.RO (daily)](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.RO/recent) — robotics and embodied AI
- [Hugging Face Papers (Trending)](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending) — community-curated hot papers
- [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/) — arXiv + community discussion and annotation
- [Papers With Code](https://paperswithcode.com/) — papers + benchmarks + code repos
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) — citation graphs and related work

## Tier 3: Specialized AI News Platforms (High Signal)

Dedicated AI coverage, usually fast and reliable.

- [The Batch (deeplearning.ai)](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/) — Andrew Ng's weekly, very high signal-to-noise
- [Import AI (Jack Clark)](https://importai.substack.com/) — Anthropic co-founder, deep technical, weekly
- [TLDR AI](https://tldr.tech/ai) — daily digest, quick read, good for headlines
- [The Rundown AI](https://www.therundown.ai/) — daily newsletter, accessible summaries
- [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/) — reliable reporting on lab strategy and products
- [LLM Stats — AI News](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news) — model releases tracker
- [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/) — benchmarks, pricing comparisons, coding agent evals
- [Crescendo AI News](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates) — agentic AI coverage + VC deals
- [AI and News](https://www.aiandnews.com/) — breakthroughs and emerging trends

## Tier 4: Tech Journalism (Broader Context)

Business angle, policy, enterprise adoption. Useful for context but verify technical claims against primary sources.

- [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/) — fast, good startup coverage
- [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence) — consumer and product angle
- [Wired AI](https://www.wired.com/tag/artificial-intelligence/) — deeper features, societal impact
- [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/) — rigorous, academic-adjacent
- [VentureBeat AI](https://venturebeat.com/ai/) — enterprise AI, fast reporting
- [Ars Technica AI](https://arstechnica.com/ai/) — technically literate, good for model analysis
- [CNBC Tech](https://www.cnbc.com/technology/) — business/financial angle on AI companies
- [Fortune Tech](https://fortune.com/section/tech/) — executive moves, company strategy
- [Foreign Policy — Tech](https://foreignpolicy.com/tag/technology/) — geopolitics of AI (China, US policy)

## Tier 6: Developer / Practitioner Channels (Hands-On Signal)

Where real builders share what actually works.

- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) — best for "Show HN" and "Ask HN" about AI tools
- [Reddit r/MachineLearning](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/) — research discussion
- [Reddit r/LocalLLaMA](https://www.reddit.com/r/localllama/) — open-source models, benchmarks, tricks
- [DEV Community AI](https://dev.to/t/ai) — practitioner tutorials and workflows
- [GitHub Trending](https://github.com/trending) — repos blowing up right now
- [Stack Overflow Blog](https://stackoverflow.blog/) — developer surveys, tool usage data
- [Simon Willison's Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/) — LLM practical use, tools, security — highest signal for practitioners

Slower cadence, higher depth. Read on weekends.

- [Interconnects (Nathan Lambert)](https://www.interconnects.ai/) — RLHF, alignment, frontier models
- [Ahead of AI (Sebastian Raschka)](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/) — applied ML research

- [One Useful Thing (Ethan Mollick)](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/) — AI for professionals, research-backed
